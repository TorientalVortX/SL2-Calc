================================================================
1. CORE STAT SYSTEM
================================================================

Primary Stats (StatKey):
- STR (Strength) - Physical power and Fire affinity:
  * +1 Fire ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Max Battle Weight, letting you equip heavier items without penalties (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Max Encumbrance, letting you carry more items in your inventory without slowing down (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +3 Max HP (per 1 Base point only - not affected by stat scaling)
  * +0.4 Critical, for STR Primary Scaling weapons only (per 1 Scaled point)
- WIL (Will) - Mental strength, FP capacity, and elemental affinity:
  * +5 FP (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 to All Elemental ATK per 4 points (excludes Sound and Acid) (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Status Infliction, increasing your chance to inflict status effects on enemies (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Max Skill Pool Size per 10 points, increasing the amount of skills you can equip (per 1 Scaled point)
- SKI (Skill) - Coordination, accuracy, and Ice affinity:
  * +1 Ice ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +2 Hit (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +0.5 Critical (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +2 Status Infliction, increasing your chance to inflict status effects on enemies (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Max Skill Pool Size per 5 points, increasing the amount of skills you can equip (per 1 Scaled point)
- CEL (Celerity) - Speed, agility, and Wind affinity:
  * +1 Wind ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +2 Evade (per 1 Scaled point)
  * Starting Turn Order, determined by who has the highest CEL (per 1 Scaled point)
- DEF (Defense) - Physical resilience and Earth affinity:
  * +1 Earth ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +0.9% Physical Defense, lowering physical damage taken (per 1 Scaled point)
- RES (Resistance) - Supernatural protection and Dark affinity:
  * +1 Dark ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +0.9% Magical Defense, lowering magical damage taken (per 1 Scaled point)
- VIT (Vitality) - Health, durability, and Water affinity:
  * +1 Water ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +10 HP (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Max Encumbrance, letting you carry more items in your inventory without slowing down (per 1 Scaled point)
- FAI (Faith) - Conviction, belief, and Light affinity:
  * +1 Light ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +3 FP (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Status Resistance, decreasing the chance for enemies to inflict you with status effects (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Critical Evade, lowering the chance of taking critical hits (per 1 Scaled point)
- LUC (Luck) - Fortune and Lightning affinity:
  * +1 Lightning ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Critical (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Critical Evade, lowering the chance of taking critical hits (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1% Item Drop Chance, boosting chances for you or allies to receive items after battle (per 1 Scaled point)
  * Only the highest LUC in the party is applied for Item Drop Chance
- GUI (Guile) - Cunning, alternative thinking, and Acid affinity:
  * +1 Acid ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +0.5 Flanking (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1% Critical Damage (per 1 Scaled point)
  * -1 Farshot Penalty per 5 points (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 Max Skill Pool Size per 5 points, increasing the amount of skills you can equip (per 1 Scaled point)
- SAN (Sanctity) - Divine presence, racial gifts, and Sound affinity:
  * +1 Sound ATK (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +2 HP (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +2 FP (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +2 Status Resistance, decreasing the chance for enemies to inflict you with status effects (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1% Elemental Resistance per 6 points, for Fire, Ice, Wind, Earth, Water, Lightning, Dark, and Light elements (per 1 Scaled point)
- APT (Aptitude) - Flexibility and adaptability:
  * +1% Experience Earned (per 1 Scaled point)
  * +1 bonus to all other stats per 6 points (per 1 Scaled point)
  * IMPORTANT: Stats obtained by APT are NOT base stats, they are bonus stats. This means they will not count towards trait requirements, and are subject to mechanics involving bonus stats (e.g., DEF from APT is negated while Burned)


================================================================
2. BASE STATS CALCULATION
================================================================

Final Stat Formula:
Final Stat = (Base Racial + Custom Base + Added Points + Food + History + Class Passive + Stamps + Custom) + APT Bonus + Legend Extend

Base Racial: Starting stats from race/subrace
Custom Base: Manual adjustments to racial base
Added Points: Player-allocated stat points (limited by level * 4)
Food: Temporary food bonuses
History: Background choice bonuses
Class Passive: Bonuses from main/sub class passives
Stamps: Equipped stamp bonuses
Custom: Additional custom adjustments
APT Bonus: +1 to all other stats per 6 APT points
Legend Extend: +1 to selected stat if enabled

Character Level: Determines available stat points (Level * 4 = total points)
Max Points: 240 (Level 60 standard)

================================================================
3. DAMAGE CALCULATIONS
================================================================

Elemental ATK Calculation:
- Fire ATK = STR + Equipment + Bonuses
- Ice ATK = SKI + Equipment + Bonuses
- Wind ATK = CEL + Equipment + Bonuses  
- Earth ATK = DEF + Equipment + Bonuses
- Dark ATK = RES + Equipment + Bonuses
- Water ATK = VIT + Equipment + Bonuses
- Light ATK = FAI + Equipment + Bonuses
- Lightning ATK = LUC + Equipment + Bonuses
- Acid ATK = GUI + Equipment + Bonuses
- Sound ATK = SAN + Equipment + Bonuses

Weapon Scaling:
Only STR provides +0.4 Critical per point for STR Primary Scaling weapons

Scaling Types Found in Weapons:
- Basic (100% single stat): Most basic weapons
- Finesse (70% primary, 30% secondary): Balanced weapons
- Vampiric (80% primary, 30% VIT): Life-stealing weapons
- Faithful (70% primary, 40% FAI): Holy weapons
- Firearms (60% GUI, 40% SKI): Gun weapons
- Spiritual (70% GUI, 40% SAN): Spirit-based weapons
- Dextria-Sound (80% primary, 30% SAN): Musical weapons
- Cunning (70% STR, 40% GUI): Clever weapons
- Earthen (70% STR, 40% DEF): Defensive weapons
- Sylphid (70% STR, 40% CEL): Wind-based weapons
- Darkness (70% STR, 40% RES): Dark weapons

Critical Hit Calculation:
Base Critical = SKI * 0.5 + LUC * 1.0 + (STR * 0.4 if using STR Primary Scaling weapon)
Critical Damage = 150% + (GUI * 1%) base damage

================================================================
4. DEFENSE CALCULATIONS
================================================================

Physical Defense:
Physical Defense % = DEF * 0.9%
Reduces all physical damage taken by this percentage

Magical Defense: 
Magical Defense % = RES * 0.9%
Reduces all magical damage taken by this percentage

Elemental Resistance:
Elemental Resistance % = SAN * (1/6)% per element
Applies to Fire, Ice, Wind, Earth, Water, Lightning, Dark, Light

Critical Evade:
Critical Evade = FAI + LUC
Reduces chance of receiving critical hits

Evade Calculation:
Total Evade = Base Evade + Bonus Evade + (CEL * 2)
Base Evade: Equipment and racial base
Bonus Evade: Temporary buffs and skills
CEL contributes 2 evade per point

================================================================
5. DERIVED STATS
================================================================

Health Points (HP):
Base HP = 100 + (Base STR * 3) + (Scaled VIT * 10) + (Scaled SAN * 2) + Custom HP + Food/History HP
Note: Only BASE STR contributes (not scaled), all other stats use scaled values

Focus Points (FP):
Base FP = 50 + (Scaled WIL * 5) + (Scaled FAI * 3) + (Scaled SAN * 2) + Custom FP + Food/History FP

Hit Accuracy:
Hit = SKI * 2 + Equipment + Buffs + Weapon bonuses

Status Infliction:
Status Infliction = WIL * 1 + SKI * 2
Increases chance to inflict status effects on enemies

Status Resistance:
Status Resistance = FAI * 1 + SAN * 2
Decreases chance of receiving status effects

Battle Weight:
Max Battle Weight = STR + Equipment modifiers
Determines what armor/equipment you can wear without penalties

Encumbrance:
Max Encumbrance = STR + VIT + Equipment modifiers
Determines inventory carrying capacity before movement penalties

Skill Pool Size:
Base Skill Pool = 4 (or 5 for specific races)
Additional slots from: WIL/10, SKI/5, GUI/5
Total = Base + WIL/10 + SKI/5 + GUI/5

================================================================
6. WEAPON SCALING
================================================================

Weapon Types & Primary Stats:
- Swords: STR primary (gets +0.4 crit/point), various scaling types
  * Basic (100% STR), Finesse (70% STR, 30% SKI), Vampiric (80% STR, 30% VIT)
  * Faithful (70% STR, 40% FAI), Darkness (70% STR, 40% RES)
- Axes: STR primary (gets +0.4 crit/point), various scaling types
  * Basic (100% STR), Finesse (70% STR, 30% SKI), Vampiric (80% STR, 30% VIT)
  * Cunning (70% STR, 40% GUI)
- Polearms/Spears: STR primary (gets +0.4 crit/point), various scaling types
  * Basic (100% STR), Finesse (70% STR, 30% SKI), Vampiric (80% STR, 30% VIT)
  * Faithful (70% STR, 40% FAI)
- Bows: STR primary (gets +0.4 crit/point), various scaling types
  * Basic (100% STR), Finesse (70% STR, 30% SKI), Dextria-Sound (80% STR, 30% SAN)
  * Note: Crossbows use Basic (100% SKI)
- Guns: GUI primary (no crit bonus), secondary SKI
  * Firearms (60% GUI, 40% SKI), Spiritual (70% GUI, 40% SAN)
- Daggers: GUI primary (no crit bonus), various scaling types
  * Basic (100% GUI), Finesse (70% GUI, 30% SKI), Vampiric (80% GUI, 30% VIT)
  * Dextria-Sound (80% GUI, 30% SAN) for instruments
- Tomes: WIL primary (no crit bonus)
  * Basic (100% WIL) - elemental damage varies by tome type
- Fists: STR primary (gets +0.4 crit/point), various scaling types
  * Basic (100% STR), Finesse (70% STR, 30% SKI), Vampiric (80% STR, 30% VIT)
  * Earthen (70% STR, 40% DEF), Sylphid (70% STR, 40% CEL)
  * Dextria-Sound (80% STR, 30% SAN) for instruments

Material Modifiers:
Materials affect: Power, Critical, Hit, Weight
Categories: Hard Materials, Wood Materials, Page Materials
Examples:
- Dragon Remains: +3 Power, +5 Crit, +5 Hit, +8 Weight
- Boulder: +10 Power, +10 Crit, -5 Hit, +25 Weight  
- Thinsteel: -3 Power, +3 Crit, +5 Hit, -2 Weight

Enchantments:
Modify: Power, Critical, Critical Modifier, Hit, Weight, Weight Modifier
Apply multiplicative bonuses to base weapon stats

Parts System:
Different weapon types use different part categories:
- Blades, Guards, Hilts (Swords)
- Spearheads, Poles (Spears)  
- Knuckles, Wrists (Fist)
- Bow Bodies, Strings, Arrows (Bows)
- Barrels, Grips, Bullets (Guns)

================================================================
8. CLASS BONUSES
================================================================

Class Stats (Main/Sub):
Each class provides base stat bonuses when selected
Main class gets higher priority in optimization
Monoclass builds (same main/sub) get 2x modifier bonuses

Example Class Stats:
- Soldier: +2 STR, +2 DEF, +1 VIT, +1 SKI
- Mage: +2 WIL, +2 SKI, +1 CEL, +1 RES
- Rogue: +2 SKI, +2 CEL, +1 LUC, +1 GUI

Class Passives:
Rankable abilities (0-5) providing additional bonuses
Cost Focus Points to maintain
Examples:
- Soldier Mettle: +HP, +Physical Defense
- Mage Aptitude: +WIL, +Elemental ATK
- Rogue Cunning: +CEL, +Critical

Valid Weapons per Class:
Determines what weapon types can be equipped
Affects optimization weapon scaling priorities
Some classes are more flexible than others

Class Hierarchy:
Base Classes: Soldier, Mage, Rogue, Duelist
Advanced Classes: Specialized versions with unique abilities
Multi-class compatibility varies by build type

================================================================
9. RACIAL BONUSES
================================================================

Race Base Stats:
Each race/subrace combination provides different starting stats
Affects total stat budget and optimization priorities

Example Races:
- Human: Balanced stats, APT bonus, flexibility
- Elf: High WIL/SKI, magical affinity
- Doriad: Nature-based bonuses, SAN focus

Subrace Variants:
Provide additional stat modifications and special abilities
Examples:
- Imperialist (Human): Balanced approach
- Phenex (Elf): Fire magic specialization
- Wild (Doriad): Physical nature build

Racial Resistances:
Some races have innate elemental resistances
Affects defensive calculations and build viability

Special Racial Abilities:
- Giant Gene: Size/reach bonuses
- Corrupted traits: SAN penalties but unique benefits
- Beast traits: Instinct-based abilities

================================================================
10. FOOD & HISTORY BONUSES
================================================================

Food System:
Temporary stat bonuses from consumed food items
Duration varies, affects current stats only
Examples:
- Fugu: +3 STR, +3 WIL (dangerous delicacy)
- Pumpkin Lollipop: +5 CEL (seasonal treat)
- Salad: +3 VIT (healthy option)

History Backgrounds:
Permanent character background bonuses
Applied once during character creation
Examples:
- Warrior: +2 STR, +1 SKI (combat focused)
- Magician: +2 WIL, +1 CEL (arcane studies)
- Assassin: +2 SKI, +1 LUC (stealth specialist)

Optimization Integration:
Both food and history bonuses are considered in stat calculations
History bonuses count as "invested points" for efficiency
Food bonuses are temporary and calculated separately

================================================================
11. LEGEND EXTEND & ASTROLOGY
================================================================

Legend Extend:
Provides +1 to selected stat when enabled
Available stats: STR, WIL, SKI, CEL, DEF, RES, VIT, FAI, LUC, GUI, SAN
Can only select one at a time
Examples:
- Axysal (STR): +1 Strength
- Kashic (WIL): +1 Will
- Zerogyn (SKI): +1 Skill

Astrology System:
Planet selection provides +1 to associated stat
Planet-Stat Mapping:
- Mercury: +1 SKI (Ice element)
- Venus: +1 DEF (Earth element)
- Mars: +1 STR (Fire element)
- Jupiter: +1 LUC (Lightning element)
- Saturn: +1 CEL (Wind element)
- Neptune: +1 VIT (Water element)
- Uranus: +1 FAI (Light element)
- Pluto: +1 RES (Dark element)

Elemental Correspondence:
Each planet also corresponds to an elemental ATK type
Useful for builds focusing on specific elements

================================================================
12. SPECIAL MECHANICS
================================================================

APT Efficiency Rule:
Final APT should be divisible by 6 for maximum efficiency
Provides +1 to all other stats per 6 APT points
Common targets: 36 (6 bonus), 42 (7 bonus), 80 (Undeniable Innovator)

Stat Scaling vs Base:
- Base stats: Count for trait requirements, unaffected by debuffs
- Scaled stats: Affected by equipment, can be negated by conditions
- APT bonuses: Considered scaled stats, not base

Diminishing Returns:
Some stats have soft caps or diminishing returns:
- Defense stats: ~40-45 for 36% reduction is efficient
- Evade: Higher values become harder to achieve/maintain
- Damage stats: Balance between offense and accuracy needed

Status Effect Mechanics:
Infliction = WIL + (SKI * 2) + Equipment
Resistance = FAI + (SAN * 2) + Equipment
Critical Evade = FAI + LUC + Equipment

Monoclass vs Multiclass:
Monoclass (same main/sub): 2x class passive bonuses
Multiclass: Access to more diverse skills and abilities
