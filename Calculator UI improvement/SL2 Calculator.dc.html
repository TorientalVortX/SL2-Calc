Turn 1 · Stat Calculator, main screen
Three layouts, one shared state
All three are live and driven by the same build, so a change in one updates the others. Your STAT_COLORS and ELEMENT_COLORS maps are copied in unchanged and now carry the hierarchy: they tint the value and the allocation bar, so nothing else needs to shout. Nine of the twelve render at their source value. Three fall below 4.5:1 as a value color on a near-black panel and are substituted at render time by the ON_DARK map — DEF #92400e → #b5762e, VIT #1e40af → #4f7ce8, SAN #6b7280 → #9aa6bf. DEF stays an amber-brown rather than lifting into LUC's orange. The source constants are untouched. APT's 'rainbow' renders as a real gradient across the number and its bar. Every stat name is clickable for its full description. 1a is the desktop command deck, 1b the dense sheet, 1c the mobile companion. The nav in 1a and the bottom bar in 1c are live — Weapon, Armor, Optimizer and Screenshot are all designed and running off the same build. Weapon math is calculateWeaponSlot; the Optimizer is a greedy allocator scored against the real DR curve, not canned suggestions.
