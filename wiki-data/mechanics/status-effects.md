# Status Effects

## Introduction

This is a list of the most commonly-seen status effects in the game, along with a brief description of what each one does. Understanding these effects is crucial for strategizing in combat and maximizing your character's effectiveness.

## Damaging Status Effects

These effects focus purely on dealing damage and nothing else:

| Status | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Poison | Deals 10% maximum HP damage every round, to a maximum of 3*LV damage. | Menov's Fang amplifies this effect. Glykin, Dullahan and Snakemen are immune to Poison infliction. |
| Lingering Damage | Take damage that ignores protection every round equal to LV based on element. | Multiple Lingering Damages can exist at once, if their element type is different from each other. |

## Hybrid Status Effects

These effects do more than one thing, combining damage (or damage-affecting effects) with other impacts.

| Status | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Burn | Deals protection-ignoring Fire damage every round equal to LV. Negates Bonus DEF. | Salamandra and Drowned Woman are immune to Burn infliction. |
| Frozen | Reduces Evade to 0 and prevents movement skills while active. Damage reduces LV; 0 ends status. Can struggle to reduce the LV. | Grants 2 rounds of immunity after recovery. |
| Soaked | +15% Fire Res, -10% Lightning Res. When taking Lightning damage, Soaked allies within 1 Range also take the damage. Ice and Earth damage replaces with a different effect. | The replaced effects are: Immobilized (Ice), Muddy (Earth). |
| Charm | Reduces damage dealt to the target by LV%. May take damage for the causer if near them. | If the causer deals any direct damage, it reduces the duration of Charm on their target (you). Shaitan are immune to Charm infliction. |
| Flatfoot | You are flat on your feet. -5 Evade and -1 Move until your next turn. |  |
| Strangling Etacof | Hex. Prevents Silence from being cured and causes it to deal 5% HP and FP damage every round. | Max. 50 Darkness damage, and 25 FP reduction. |
| Llunel's Decay | Hex. Prevents Interference from being cured and causes it to instead reverse regeneration into damage. | Regeneration status effects also become reversed, such as Refreshing Flow. |
| Hellflame Uorate | Hex. Prevents Burn from being cured and causes it to nullify bonus RES the target has. |  |
| Menov's Fang | Hex. Prevents Poison from being cured and causes it to deal 1.5x damage. |  |
| Imagin's Despair | Hex. Prevents Fear from being cured and increases all damage taken by 10%. | This affects Damage over Time effects. |

## Momentum-Impairing

Effects that interact with momentum and action economy:

| Status | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stun | Turn is skipped, but damage taken is reduced by 50%. Ends afterwards. Taking resisted damage might end the status early and let you act with 3M. | Bosses cannot be stunned. Grants 2 rounds of immunity after recovery. |
| Knocked Down | Must spend 3M getting up during your next turn. | Kip-Up, Roc's Rise and Quick Rise mitigate the momentum needed. Grants 2 rounds of immunity after recovery. |
| Badly Beaten | Obtained after reviving. Reduces your base Momentum recovery at round start by LV. | The status worsens if you are revived multiple times while under its effect. |

## Movement-Impairing

Effects that lock or reduce your movement capabilities.

| Status | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Immobilize | Cannot use Move or Movement Skills. | Grants 2 rounds of immunity after recovery. |
| Grappled | Treated as immobilized until the causer's next turn. | If the causer or victim are moved, the effect ends. |
| Slow | -LV Move. | This only affects basic movement. |
| Clumsy | Movement skills, aside from basic movement, are disabled. | Grants 2 rounds of immunity after recovery. |
| Muddy | 15% chance to be slip and be knocked down after using Movement. Chance increases by 15% for every tile moved beyond class base Movement speed. | This will also interrupt your Move attempt right where you've been knocked down. |

## Conditional

Status effects that don't do much on their own and require an extra trigger to be effective. Can also be ones that set up for other effects.

| Status | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frostbite | Inflicts protection-ignoring Ice damage every time you walk on an Ice Sheet. | The Ice Sheet must break for this to happen. LV = Damage. |
| Thorn Wrap | Inflicts protection-ignoring Pierce damage to the target when they use a Movement skill. | This also works on basic Movement. LV = Damage. |
| Attract Objects | Pulls enemy traps and bombs LV tiles towards yourself at the start of a new round. |  |
| Guard Break | The character's guard has been broken. They temporarily take 15% more damage, cannot Guard, and Parry skills will automatically fail. | Requires the opponent to be Guarding. This effect ends on their next turn. |
| Certain Defeat | At the start of a new round, this unit's HP becomes 0. | If you are revived, you can still act before round is over. |
| Airborne | In the air, dude. | A myriad of skills interact with this status effect, but it does nothing by itself. |
| Claret Call | Increases physical damage caused by the causer. | Only increases the damage of Basic Attacks and Ghost skills. |
| Needle | Upon taking Light damage from any source but Needle, explodes, taking Light damage equal to LV and heals allies of the causer that are within 2 Range for the same amount. |  |
| Solar Lance | That can't be good. | Upon taking any Fire or Light damage, it explodes next turn, dealing 100% of the causer's Fire ATK as protection-ignoring Fire damage. |

## Crowd-Control Status Effects

Notable effects that significantly impair or control the flow of battle.

| Status | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analyze Weakness | Increases all damage suffered by LV%. | Affects Damage Over Time effects too. |
| Hunted | Increases damage taken from the causer by LV%. | Affects Damage Over Time effects too. |
| Hesitation | LV% chance to hesitate when attacking. | This only affects Basic Attack or skills that use Basic Attacks. Shaitan are immune to Hesitation infliction. |
| Sneezed On | You were sneezed on... ew. -10% Status Resistance. | You gain this effect if you're a Kaelensia and someone with Animal Allergy is nearby during combat. |
| Fear | -15 Hit VS Causer. | Shaitan are immune to Fear infliction. |
| Goose Bite | Reduces all stats by LV. |  |
| Drain | Reduces all stats by LV. | This will also increase the causer's status by LV. |
| Blotch | Decreases Hit by LV. |  |
| Wear Down | Reduces Armor by LV. |  |
| Rampage | Decreases DEF by LV. |  |
| Cursed Wound | Lowers maximum HP by LV. |  |
| Soul Chains | Prevents FP recovery as if it were Interfered. Treated as being Installed uniquely. | Interference reduction effects apply to this. |
| Acidity | Reduces Phys. Defense and Mag. Defense by LV%. |  |
| Black Drain | Power drained by an enemy. Lowers Scaled Weapon Attack equal to LV. | This also applies from non-damaging skills from Soldier. |
| Glowing | Evade is reduced by LV. |  |
| Magnetize | Increases the causer's Hit when attacking you by LV (max 50). |  |
| Pacify | Cannot use basic attacks or Offensive skills. Taking non-status, non-bonus damage will reduce its duration by 1 round. | This will only be seen used on Monsters, usually. |
| Spelldaze | Once acquired, puts a random spell you have equipped on a 2 round cooldown. Cannot be reapplied while active. | Cooldown timer cannot be reduced below the original cooldown duration. |

## Hard Crowd-Control Status Effects

Extremely crippling effects that can turn a whole fight upside-down and force your enemy to either adapt, or lose.

| Status | Description | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enemy Evaluation | Decreases Hit, Critical, Evade, and damage dealt by LV%. |  |
| Confusion | Cannot differentiate between friend and foe. | This prevents you from targeting allies with supporting or healing skills. |
| Blind | Final hit chance is capped at 50%. | Kaminah Robes turn this effect into Beyond Sight, which has a lesser impact on your hit chance. |
| Interference | Prevents recovery of HP and FP. | This also prevents revive effects. |
| Silence | This character is silenced and cannot cast spells. | Grants 2 rounds of immunity after recovery. |
| Removed From Battle | Removed from the battle while this status is active. |  |
