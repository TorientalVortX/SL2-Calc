# Protection

Protection is a term for how much incoming damage can be reduced. It is a number which is calculated like so:

- (Protection Class Reduction + Other modifiers) + Armor Factor = Protection

## Protection Classes

Defense affects Physical Damage Reduction, which reduces physical damage taken.

Resistance affects Magic Damage Reduction, which reduces magic damage taken.

In both cases, only 90% of the specific stat will be used for its damage reduction percentage, be it Physical Damage Reduction (PDR%) or Magic Damage Reduction (MDR%).

## Other Modifiers

These can be class damage reduction, equipment damage reduction, elemental resistances, skill damage reductions, etc.

Any percentages mentioned above are multiplicative with one another, preventing cases where you can reach or go over 100% damage reduction.

## Armor Factor

This is your Equipment's "Armor" value if the damage is physical, and its "Magic Armor" value if magical. Certain equipment pieces or Weapon Parts can also provide a bonus to Armor or Magic Armor, which are added up to the Armor Factor independently.

This is reduced by a flat amount after all calculations above.
