# Critical Evade

Critical Evade reduces the probability of sustaining a critical blow. It is calculated as such:

- Luck + Faith + Bonus Critical Evade = Critical Evade

## Outcome Contest

Like the other formulas, your total Critical Evade will directly subtract from an enemy's Critical rate.

So if you have 80 Critical Evade, and the enemy has 100 Critical, you'll only have a 20% chance to be critically hit.

## Bonus Critical Evade

There are a couple extra ways to increase Critical Evade:

- The Torso enchant, Boneheart, which grants 25.
- Black Knight's Negation increases critical evade by 18 while wearing Heavy Armor.
- Agile Protection from Ranger can grant up to 15 while wearing Light Armor.

## Critical Damage Reduction

Certain races and items grant resistances or immunity to critical hits:

- The accessory, Lucky Amulet, reduces Critical Damage taken by 10%.
- The torso, Padded Vest-Cloak, reduces Critical Damage taken by 10%.
- Dullahan's racial passive reduces the Critical Damage modifier by their Scaled SAN.
- Lich are immune to Critical Hits until their FP goes below a certain threshold.
