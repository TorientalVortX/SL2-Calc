# Attack Range

Attack Range dictates the number of tiles away from your current position that your weapon can perform an attack from.

Some Skills and Spells have their own independent range, separate from the weapon's own. Sometimes it can be shorter or longer, depending on the skill.

## Modifications

Attack Range can be changed by a myriad of things, temporarily enhanced by some skills or passively increased by some talents. Some of the more notable ones are:

- Marksmanship talent passively increases the range of Bow and Gun weapons.
- Tactician's Volley Formation increases the range of ranged weapons while you are standing on it.
- Longdraw from Archer increases the attack range of Bow weapons and Archer skills.
- The Huge Blade weapon component increases the attack range of Swords.
- The Gigantic enchantment increases the attack range of any weapons by 1.
