# Move

Move determines how many spaces you can move in battle by using the Move command in battle. While moving, you can become susceptible to field hazards.

Its base value is 5 squares by default, but can be increased or decreased by other factors, such as:

- Skills such as Charge or Shukuchi.
- Racials like Fleetfoot or Scurry.
- Leg slot items, like Shoes of Swiftness.

## Movement Skills

Some skills in the game allow you to safely relocate from one position to another, serving as alternatives to basic movement. These skills can be used for various purposes, including gap-closing, creating distance, and tactical repositioning.

However, these skills will be blocked by movement-impairing Status Effects such as Immobilize or Frozen.

## Movement Spells

In contrast, certain spells can completely bypass the restrictions of immobilization, such as Blink.
