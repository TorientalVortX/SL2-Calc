# Item Materials

Item materials provide different effects to a piece of equipment depending on its type. Weapon materials are profound, offering large statistical increases or making them valid casting tools for certain domains, while accessory materials provide simple elemental resistances.

Currently, there are 4 classes of material; Cloth, Wood, Chapter, and Hard. Each piece of equipment has one of these material classes, and cannot use materials of a different class — note that Hard material equipment can be either Metal or Remains material, however.

These may be changed freely with material change kits created by an alchemist so long as the equipment material class is correct.
