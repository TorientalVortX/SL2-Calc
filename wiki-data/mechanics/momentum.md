# Momentum

Momentum (often known as just 'M'), dictates your Action Economy. You utilize Momentum Points to perform Actions during your turn in combat. Once you are out of Momentum, you cannot act, and must End Turn.

By default, every character receives 7M per round, and most actions require 3M to use for the first time in a round. Some actions can have less or more costs on your Momentum, and some status impairments can affect it directly, such as Stun or Knockdown, and some abilities or racial skills can modify the amount of Momentum you start your turn with, such as Soul Engine from Dullahan.

## Repeat Action Tracking

- Repeating an action on the same round increments a 1M penalty to the action's base Momentum costs. So a repeated action that costs 3M will cost 4M, then 5M, and so on.
- This will also come with +50% FP cost penalty that increases the same way. Being 150% / 200% / 250%, and so on.

## General Momentum Modifiers

- Once per round, you may gain an additional +1M by hitting an enemy with a critical strike (Critical Hit!). This bonus is limited per weapon, per unit, per round. Fleur from Duelist can increase that bonus to +2M, but only once per enemy, per weapon, per round.
- Once per round, you may gain an additional +1M by exploiting an enemy's Elemental Weakness (Weak!). Similar to different weapons, each different spell used will yield the bonus.
- Once per round, you may lose an additional -1M for being impaired by an enemy's Elemental Resistance (Resist!). Some field effects will cause this impairment to happen before your turn starts, making your starting Momentum 6 rather than 7.
