# Enchantments

Enchantments are bonuses that can be applied to pieces of equipment by player characters with levels in the Enchanting craft and appropriate catalyst. Enchantments have limits on what types of gear they can be applied to, and may function differently depending on what they're placed on.

Most catalysts can be acquired by dismantling items that drop with the appropriate enchant, fighting certain bosses, or crafting them with Enchanting. Certain enchants, like Feasting and most Curses, are drop only - the item MUST drop with the enchantment attached, and cannot be applied through other means. As such, items like a Misplaced Coastal Squall are very valuable.
