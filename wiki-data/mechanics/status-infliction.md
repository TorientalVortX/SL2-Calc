# Status Infliction

Status Infliction is your chance to inflict a status effect on an enemy in battle. It is a percentage, calculated like so:

- Skill*2 + Will + Bonus Status Infliction = Status Infliction

## Outcome Contest

Like the other formulas, your total Status Infliction rate will directly subtract from an enemy's Status Resistance.

So if you have 150 Status Infliction, and the enemy has 50 Status Resistance, you'll have a 100% chance to inflict them.

## Unique Infliction Rates

Some skills or spells may use different reduced or unique status infliction formulas, such as:

- Signature Step from Dancer.
- Sturm Shreik from Boxer.
