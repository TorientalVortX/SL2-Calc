# Critical

Critical is your chance to land a critical hit when attacking an enemy, resulting in increased damage. It is calculated as so:

- Weapon Critical + Skill/2 + Luck + Bonus Critical = Critical Chance

Critical is most easily raised by stats and by various upgrades to your weapon, such as enchantments like Reaper or weapon parts like Razor Blade. Otherwise, you may need to rely on other equipment or class skills such as Duelist's Poise or Rogue's Northern Wind.

## Outcome Contest

Like the other formulas, your total Critical rate will directly subtract from an enemy's Critical Evade.

So if you have 100 Critical, and the enemy has 80 Critical Evade, you'll have a 20% chance to land a Critical Hit.

## Critical Hit

When performing a basic attack, there is a set chance for the attack to be a Critical Hit. This chance is a percentage based on your own Critical stat minus the target's Critical Evade. Either stat may be altered by a wide variety of skills and conditions.

A Critical Hit has the following effects:

- Increases the base damage of the attack from 100% SWA to (Weapon Critical Damage + Scaled GUI)% SWA. (Any bonus damage from on-hit effects remains the same, unless specified.)
- Triggers weapon or skill properties that activate on Critical Hits.
- Grants you a 1 Momentum bonus once per weapon, per round. Fleur grants an additional 1 Momentum per weapon, per target, per round.

## Lightning Critical

Redgull's Elemental Impact (marked by a Redgull symbol in the skill's description, which can be clicked for more information) may trigger a Lightning Critical effect. This effect operates separately from a standard critical hit:

- Increases the damage of the skill or spell by 25%.
- Uses your base Critical rate and bonuses, but not your weapon critical.
