# Damage

Damage is the overall representation of the harm inflicted on characters or objects that reduces their HP. It represents the impact of attacks, spells, traps, or other harmful events, affecting a character's ability to continue fighting.

## Damage Classes

Physical Damage is any type of damage done without the direct use of weaved Focus. For example, hitting someone with an ignited torch causes Blunt and Fire physical damage, assuming the Fire was made by natural means and not raw Focus.

Magic Damage comes from harmful spells or enchantments. Damage taken from this is often from weaved Focus, and delivered to the target, bypassing physical defenses but being resisted by magical defenses.

Protection-Ignoring Damage can be either physical or magical but bypasses all defenses and armor.

## Scaling

For Basic Attacks: Scaled Weapon Attack

For Non-Spells: Scaled Weapon Attack * (Skill's percentage SWA scaling)

For Spells: (Scaled Weapon Attack * Spell's percentage SWA scaling) + (Applicable Elemental Attack * Spell's Elemental ATK scaling)
