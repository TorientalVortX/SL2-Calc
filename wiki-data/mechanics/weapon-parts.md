# Weapon Parts

## Weapon Parts

Weapon Parts provide an extra level of customization to your weapon. Each weapon has a set of 2-4 part slots, which can be filled for a variety of bonuses. You may customize your weapon at any blacksmith by dragging and dropping a part into an appropriate slot on a weapon.

Part types are divided into Blades, Guards, Hilts, Spearheads, Axeheads, Poles, Knuckles, Wrists, Bow Bodies, Strings, Arrows, Barrels, Grips, Bullets, Covers, Binds, and Scrolls.
