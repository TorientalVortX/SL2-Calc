# Evade

Evade refers to a character's ability to avoid an enemy's attack. The Evade rate is calculated by:

- (Celerity - Weight Penalty) *2 + Equipment Extras + Bonus Evade (max. 50) = Evade

## Outcome Contest

Like the other formulas, your total Evade will directly subtract from an enemy's Hit.

So if you have 150% Evade, and the enemy has 200% Hit, you'll have a 50% chance to dodge their attack per hit check.

Hit chance will always be a minimum of 5%.

## Weight Penalty

Weight Penalty applies when you are above your Battle Weight threshold. For every 1 Battle Weight above it, you lose 2 Hit/Evade, with no cap.

## Equipment Extras

These only refer to Upgrade Levels and Quality Addons on your Torso and Shoes, which compose your Base Evade.

## Bonus Evade

It's important to note that most "Bonus Evade" amps from items or skills will only be considered up to a maximum of +50 Evade, and any additional bonus Evade beyond this cap will only serve to mitigate Evade reductions.

Passive Evade buffs, such as Unarmored Prowess or item effects like Sarasha Gi's +12 Evade, also count toward the +50 cap. However, buffs that increase base Evade, like Equipment Extras or the Dodger ability from the Rogue class, are not affected by the cap.

## Glancing Blows

Sometimes, even if your evade fails, you may still avoid a full hit. Glancing Blows reduce the damage you take by 60% and do not trigger any additional skill effects, such as status inflictions or knockback.

When you attack an opponent, the game rolls two accuracy checks to determine the outcome of your attack:

2 Roll(s) Remaining. This one was a hit. The first hit roll was successful, so no glancing. Damage taken; Evaded 0 out of 2 hits.

2 Roll(s) Remaining. This one was a miss. 1 Roll(s) Remaining. This one was a hit. Damage reduced; Evaded 1 out of 2 hits.

2 Roll(s) Remaining. This one was a miss. 1 Roll(s) Remaining. This one was a miss. Damage evaded; Evaded 2 out of 2 hits.

## Limitations

While evasion is potentially a very strong source of damage mitigation, it’s wholly unreliable as your sole defensive option. Some skills or spells may have the following tags:

- Great Accuracy. This skill tag ensures a skill will always be at least a glancing blow. An example is Firing Posture from Arbalest.
- Ignores Evasion disregards the evade system entirely.
