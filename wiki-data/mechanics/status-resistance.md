# Status Resistance

Status Resistance is your chance to resist a status effect an enemy has tried to inflict on you in battle. It is a percentage, calculated like so:

- Sanctity*2 + Faith + Bonus Status Resistance = Status Resistance

## Outcome Contest

Like the other formulas, your total Status Resistance rate will directly subtract from an enemy's Status Infliction.

So if you have 120 Status Resistance, and the enemy has 150 Status Infliction, there will only be a 30% chance you'll be inflicted.

## Bonus Status Resistance

A few equipment items and enchantments can influence your Status Resistance, such as:

- Warding Cloth increases Status Resistance by +10%.
- Holy Soles Plus increases Status Resistance by +5%.
- Protective Paper Charm, accessory, increases Status Resistance by +40% until you take Fire damage.
- Prevention Potion series potions increase Status Resistance by 20/35/50% for 3/4/5 rounds.
- Warding enchantment increases Status Resistance by 10%. (Torso/Shoes only)
