# Hit

Hit refers to a character's ability to successfully land an attack on an enemy. The Hit rate is calculated by:

- (Skill - Weight Penalty) *2 + Weapon Accuracy + Flanking + Bonus Hit (max. 50) = Hit Rate

## Outcome Contest

Like the other formulas, your total Hit rate will directly subtract from an enemy's Evade.

So if you have 200% Hit, and the enemy has 150% Evade, you'll have a 50% chance to land a Hit on each Hit check.

Hit chance will always be a minimum of 5%.

## Weight Penalty

Weight Penalty applies when you are above your Battle Weight threshold. For every 1 Battle Weight above it, you lose 2 Hit/Evade, with no cap.

## Flanking

Flanking is a bonus to your Hit based on your Guile. The amount is multiplied based on whether you are attacking an opponent from the sides or from behind and whether you have an ally adjacent to them when attacking.

If you attack an opponent from the sides, you gain 25% of your Flanking as a bonus to Hit. From the rear, you gain 50% of your Flanking Bonus.

If your ally is adjacent, it is also doubled. So if you attack an opponent from behind with an ally next to them, you gain 100% of your Flanking.

## Farshot Penalty

Basic attacks made beyond 1 range (melee weapons) or 3 range (ranged weapons) incur a farshot penalty dependent on the remaining distance to a target, and do so at a rate of -15 hit per tile beyond that range.

This penalty can be mitigated through Wind Read (Marksmanship subtalent) or Guile.

## Bonus Hit

It's important to note that most "Bonus Hit" from items or skills will only be considered up to a maximum of +50 Hit, and any additional bonus Hit beyond this cap will only serve to mitigate Hit reductions.

Passive Hit buffs, such as Trained Fists or item effects like Armor of Eyes's +8 Hit, also count toward the +50 cap. However, buffs that increase base Hit, like Equipment Extras, are not affected by the cap.
