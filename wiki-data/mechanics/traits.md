# Traits

Traits are special attributes that can be applied to a character. These can range from direct stat boosts from History traits, to traits more centered around RP. Traits can be acquired by spending trait points. By default, a character starts with one trait point, and gains one more every three levels, for a total of twenty-one points on a character at level 60. Equipped traits can be reset and points refunded through the use of a Fruit of Blandness.

Traits with stat point requirements reference your base total. Bonuses from APT or items will not contribute!

## History Traits

You can only have a single history trait at once. If your race falls under the Human category, your history trait will not cost any trait points.

History traits are considered base, but only for the purposes of trait requirements. This is not to be confused with racial base stats.
