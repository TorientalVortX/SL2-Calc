# Mystic Youkai

6 Youkai. Stats are shown at level 1 and level 60; a Summoner's Youkai
scales between the two with its own level.

### Skills (3)

**Ruby Beam** — Cost: 6 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Isespian, Area: Single, Range: 5, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Fires a beam from the Carbuncle's gem, dealing Earth magic damage. It may also inflict Blindness on the target for 2 turns.

**Positive Thinker** — Passive

Due to maintaining a sunny outlook, Dark resistance is increased by 10%.

**777** — Passive

A unique lucky charm that the Carbuncle possesses. If the Master (or you, if Installed) possesses exactly 7 Youkai contracts, the owner of this skill gains a +7 Luck bonus to all statistics except VIT, SAN, and APT. However, the bonus is only +3 if the target it would apply to is a non-Youkai.

### Skills (3)

**Whirlpool** — Cost: 8 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Aquarian, Area: Circle (3), Tags: Youkai Evoke

Creates a whirlpool around the user, dealing Water magic damage with Great Accuracy to all within 3 Range. Damaged enemies are also pulled toward the user by 1 square.

**Painful Accuracy** — Passive

The owner of this skill possess attacks that hit in... tender places. Grants the owner of the skill +2 SKI, and causes their Kicks to deal +6 damage.

**Seafarer** — Passive

A being that lives and travels in the sea. Grants immunity to Water damage.

**Shatter Beam** — Cost: 9 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Isespian, Area: Single, Range: 5, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Fires a beam from the Terrasque's chest, dealing Earth physical damage. In addition, it reduces the target's DEF and RES by 1+(Terrasque Level/12) for 3 rounds.

### Skills (3)

**Phase Fang** — Cost: 5 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Single, Range: 1, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Performs a basic attack on an enemy within 1 Range that deals bonus damage equal to Phase Python's level/3. If it hits, it may inflict Poison LV X (X = Level/2, min. 3), and if you're in the tile the enemy is facing, you teleport back to the tile you began the round on. If you are on any other tile, if any other enemies are in the battle, you are teleported behind a random one.

**Vile Mastery** — Passive

Grants Poison Resistance equal to Level/2%. Phase Python itself receives 40+Level% instead.

**Slither Mastery** — Cost: 15 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Single, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Has both passive and active components. Passively, Movement becomes teleport, and total movement speed penalties are reduced by 15+Level% (Phase Python receives 100% immunity). When activated, any Immobilize effect on the user is instantly ended.

### Skills (3)

**Burst of Wind** — Cost: 5 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Sylphid, Area: Single, Range: 5, Tags: Youkai Evoke, Cooldown: 2 Round(s)

Targets 1 enemy within 5 Range, then wrap yourself with the power of the wind elemental Talvyd (if no elemental is active) until your next turn, and then deal Wind magic damage to the target. If the user of the skill is Wind Elemental, the elemental effect is permanent, and the Range is increased by 2. (LV = Wind Elemental's LV/10, min. 2 max. 5)

**Barrier of Wind** — Passive

+5 Evade versus ranged weapons. If the owner of this skill is Wind Elemental, they gain +20 Evade instead.

**Elemental of Wind** — Passive

+15% Wind Resistance, Talvyd provides +10 Evade. If the owner of this skill is Wind Elemental, they reflect Wind damage, and gain immunity to Burn, Poison, and Immobilize.
