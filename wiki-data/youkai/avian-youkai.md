# Avian Youkai

5 Youkai. Stats are shown at level 1 and level 60; a Summoner's Youkai
scales between the two with its own level.

### Skills (3)

**Apodis** — Cost: 5 - 29 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Mercalan, Area: Single, Range: 5

Creates lines of light that attack the target, dealing Light magic damage to the target. During the night, the damage is increased by 25%.

**Fortune Feather** — Passive

When taking Fire, Ice, Lightning or Wind damage, there is a LUC+Apus's level% chance that you will gain 10% resistance for that damage instance only.

**Floating** — Passive

Maintaining an aerial positioning, increasing Earth resistance by 25%, but also increasing Wind weakness by 25%.

### Skills (3)

**Mare Wing** — Cost: 8-32 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Circle, Range: 3

Creates a buffet of wind, dealing Wind magic damage to all within 3 Range. It also creates an Air Shaft at the user's location for 3 rounds.

**Talon Claw** — Passive

Deadly, razor-sharp talons that tear apart anything. Grants +5 Power to attacks with Fist weapons and skills that require a Fist weapon.

**Gryphon Hoof** — Passive

A floating state that nullifies attacks by the ground (literally). While Airborne, you become immune to Earth damage.

### Skills (3)

**Cresyr** — Cost: 8-32 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Single, Range: 4

A loud, chilling screech, dealing Ice magic damage to the target. If the target is Weak to Ice, the caster also gains Crowcall LV2 for 3 rounds. (Crowcall increases STR and CEL by its LV. The LV is doubled if Snow Crow is the one using this skill.)

**Snowfall** — Passive

Tiles the owner of this skill walks on are covered in ice sheets, which end movement prematurely if stepped on by enemies. The ice sheets created by this skill last for 2 rounds.

**Ice Armor** — Cost: 15 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Single

Creates a protective layer of ice around the body of the caster, increasing their DEF by 2 (+1 per 5 Snow Crow levels) for 3 rounds (+1 per 10 Snow Crow levels). It also grants 25% Resistance to Ice, but also a 25% Weakness to Fire.
