# Night Youkai

4 Youkai. Stats are shown at level 1 and level 60; a Summoner's Youkai
scales between the two with its own level.

### Skills (3)

**Wink** — Cost: 10 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Huggessoan, Area: Single, Range: 4, Tags: Youkai Evoke

A low-grade enchantment that is triggered by a wink. May inflict Charm LV X (X = half of Lilu's level) on the target for 3 turns.

**Nightshade** — Passive

A protective charm that is powered by the night. They gain +2 DEF and +2 RES.

**Embrace** — Cost: 10 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Single, Range: 1, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Attempts to pull the target close, applying the Grapple skill to them. If the target is Charmed by the user, the Grapple is always successful. If used by Lilu, the Range increases to 8, and they will teleport to an empty tile next to the target.

### Skills (3)

**Frostbite** — Cost: 8 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Aquarian, Area: Single, Range: 4, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Fangs of ice appear around the target and bite down, dealing Ice physical damage. It also inflicts Frostbite LVX (Yuki-onna's LV/2, min. 2) for 2 rounds. (Frostbite causes unresistible LV Ice damage every time the afflicted unit walks on an Ice Sheet.)

**Snowshoes** — Passive

Being prepared to walk on unstable footing, the owner of this skill does not suffer reduced movement nor break Ice Sheets during Move.

**Snow Crest** — Passive

Possessed by beings born of the snow. Reflects Ice damage, but suffers a 50% Vulnerability to Fire damage.

### Skills (3)

**Drown** — Cost: 9 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Aquarian, Area: Single, Range: 4, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Create a ball of water around the target's head (or whatever counts as it), dealing Water magic damage. If the user is Drowned Woman, there is also a chance that it will Silence the target for 3 rounds.

**Ghost Sea** — Passive

Property belonging to those who have died at sea. Grants immunity to Burn status. User is treated as Ghost-race in addition to all other racial types.

**Ammendum, Sin, and Purpose** — Passive

An irregularity that results in an incorporeal body. Grants 50% Slash, Blunt, and Pierce resistance, but also grants a 25% weakness to Dark damage. (Only 25% for non-Drowned Woman units).

### Skills (3)

**Ichor Veil** — Cost: 5 FP / 3M, Target: Ally Only, Domain: Huggessoan, Area: Single, Range: 5, Tags: Youkai Evoke, Cooldown: 3 Round(s)

Costs 10% of max HP. Targets 1 other allied unit within 5 Range, granting them Ichor Veil LV X (X = 10% of max HP, 3 rounds). If the user of this skill is Nacht, they can target themself to create a burst of blood, dealing Water + Dark magic damage to all enemies within a 3 Size Circle (damage increased by HP consumed). If the summoner has the Claret Call skill active, damaged enemies have it applied to them. Additionally, when used by Nacht, this skill's CD is reduced by 1 round.

**Bloodstained Night** — Passive

When this unit is defeated, the battlefield is covered in a Bloodstained Night effect (LV = Nacht's level, 5 rounds). If the owner of this skill is Nacht, after 3 rounds (or when the battle ends), they recover to full HP (in an unsummoned state).

**Midnight Ruler** — Cost: 15 FP / 2M, Target: Ally Only, Domain: Huggessoan, Area: Single, Tags: Youkai Evoke, Cooldown: 3 Round(s) Midnight Ruler also stores HP skill costs and damage taken by Bloodstained Night as a sublevel. You can use this skill to end Midnight Ruler and recover HP equal to its sublevel. Otherwise, when Midnight Ruler's duration ends, you will automatically recover HP equal to 25% of its sublevel.

On New Round: If it is night, or the owner of this skill is standing in a Darkness tile, they gain Midnight Ruler LV X (X = higher of 25% of Nacht's level or 5, 3 rounds) if they do not already have it. If the owner of this skill is Nacht, this effect also applies when summoned and is permanent when it does.
