# Fairy Youkai

4 Youkai. Stats are shown at level 1 and level 60; a Summoner's Youkai
scales between the two with its own level.

### Skills (3)

**Paraeta** — Cost: 6 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Aquarian, Area: Single, Range: 4, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Summons the spirit of Asrai, who creates a mass of bubbles between you and the target enemy. Deals magic Water damage to the target. If the user is Asrai, they recover HP equal to 50% of the damage dealt. Otherwise, the user recovers 25% of the damage dealt.

**Fairy Ring** — Passive

A protective charm that leads away from danger. Grants +10% Resistance to luck-based status effects. If you have multiple Fairy Ring skills equipped, the effects will stack.

**Aquae Crista** — Passive

Seal of the kingdom of water. Increases Water resistance by 50%, but increases vulnerability to Fire by 25%.

### Skills (3)

**Black Rose** — Cost: 6 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Huggessoan, Area: Single, Range: 2, Tags: Youkai Evoke

Hands the target a black rose, dealing Dark magic damage to them. There is also a chance it will inflict Charm LV X (X = half of Wawa's level) or Blind to that target for 2 rounds (but only one per cast).

**Fairy Ring** — Passive

A protective charm that leads away from danger. Grants +10% Resistance to luck-based status effects. If you have multiple Fairy Ring skills equipped, the effects will stack.

**Bad Juju** — Passive

At a chance equal to (LUC+Wawa's level)%, the owner of this skill may steal FP equal to half of Wawa's level from an enemy in 2 Range at the start of a new round.

### Skills (3)

**Fairy Lance** — Cost: 4 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Mercalan, Area: Line (5), Tags: Youkai Evoke

Creates a lance made of focus at your current location and fires it in a line in the direction you are facing, which deals Light magic damage to the first enemy hit. The maximum range of this skill increases by 1 per 10 Izabe levels.

**Fairy Ring** — Passive

A protective charm that leads away from danger. Grants +10% Resistance to luck-based status effects. If you have multiple Fairy Ring skills equipped, the effects will stack.

**Radiant Roa** — Passive, Target: Ally Only, Range: 2

An aura of belief and protection granted by the sun. Increases FAI and RES for all allies in 2 Range by 2. The Range increases by 1 per 10 Izabe levels. If Izabe is installed, the stat bonus increases by 1 per 15 Izabe levels.

### Skills (3)

**Umbrella Flap** — Cost: 6 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Domain: Aquarian, Area: Circle (1), Tags: Youkai Evoke, Cooldown: 1 Round(s) Afterwards, if the user is Kasa-Obake, they gain Umbrella Up (until their next turn).

A sudden opening of an umbrella, which deals Wind magic damage to all enemies in a 1 Size Circle around the user, and applies Aero Shift (2) to field tiles. If the owner is Kasa-Obake and they are Soaked, +2 Size, and they create a burst of water as moisture is flung from it, dealing an extra hit equal to 100% of Water ATK, and curing them of Soaked.

**Disturbing Presence** — Passive, Target: Ally Only, Range: 2 At the start of a new round, if Kasa-Obake is standing on a water tile, they jump in it, inflicting themself and all enemies in 3 Range with Soaked (2 rounds).

Enemies within 1 Range of this unit are distracted by it, taking a -5 Hit, Evade, and Critical penalty when attacking a unit (except this one). If the owner of this skill is Kasa-Obake, +2 Range and additional -5 penalty.

**Mysterious Rainhat** — Passive

+15% Water Resistance, +30% damage reduction VS rain-based attacks and field effects. If the owner of this skill is Kasa-Obake, they instead are immune to Water damage and rain-based attacks and field effects, and if they have Umbrella Up active, damage to all allies in 2 Range of those types is redirected to Kasa-Obake.
