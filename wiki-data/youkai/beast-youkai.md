scales between the two with its own level.

### Skills (3)

**Cat's Claw** — Cost: 6-30 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Single, Range: 4

A magical slash from what feels like a lion's claws. Deals Slash magic damage equal to Kilkenny's level, and reduces the target's FP by half of Kilkenny's level..

**Anti-Beast** — Passive

A hatred towards beast-kind, dealing 10% more damage against Beast, Lupine, Felidae, and Grimalkin races.

**Tough Fur** — Passive

Hard to cut, but easy to soak. Increases Slash resistance by 25%, but increases Water weakness by 25%.

### Skills (3)

**Seeker Flame** — Cost: 20 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Single

Creates a Seeker Flame at a random unoccupied location around you. The Seeker Flame will seek out an enemy, and if it can stand next to them, will detonate, dealing Fire magic damage that ignores armor to all enemies within 1 Range around it equal to its LV. The LV of the Seeker Flame is equal to the user's level, and only one can be active at a time.

**Firefall** — Passive

Tiles the owner of this skill walks on are covered in cinders, which deal damage to enemies who walk on them (LV = 10+10% of Fire ATK). The cinders created by this skill last for 2 rounds.

**Firebody** — Passive

A body of living fire, which causes this unit's Fire damage to pierce up to 10% of Fire Resistance.

### Skills (3)

**Underworld Drain** — Cost: 6-33 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Circle (3), Cooldown: 3 Rounds If the user is Kerberos, +2 Size and +100% heal amount.

The user claws at the dirt and unleashes a swarm of insects that attack all enemies in a 3 Size Circle around them, dealing Dark magic damage. These insects them return to the user and restore their HP based on how many enemies were hit. (25% of level per enemy hit.)

**Stalwart Guardian** — Passive

Reduces redirected and reflected damage by 15%. If the owner of this skill is Kerberos, the reduction is increased to 40%.

**Guard Dog** — Passive

Grants a LV X% (X = Kerberos' level) chance to redirect basic attacks and skill damage from another allied unit within 2 Range to this unit.
