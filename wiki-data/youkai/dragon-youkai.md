# Dragon Youkai

### Skills (3)

**Fire Jet** — Cost: 9 FP / 3M, Target: Enemy Only, Area: Line (5), Tags: Youkai Evoke

Releases a super fast jet of fire in a line, dealing Fire magic damage to all enemies within that line. All tiles in the line are also covered in Cinders for 2 rounds that deal damage equal to 50% of the user's Fire ATK.

**Daisangen** — Passive

A powerful combination of three dragons. If the Summoner has contracted all three of Chun, Haku, and Hatsu, all Dragon-race Youkai the Summoner summons gain +2 to all stats except VIT. This also applies to the Summoner if the Summoner installs a Dragon-race Youkai. (Multiple instances of this skill do not stack.)

**Fire Scale** — Passive

Scales born in fire. Immunity to Fire damage, but has a 25% weakness to Ice damage.
