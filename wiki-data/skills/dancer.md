## Skills

### Curtain Call

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 35/33/31/29/27
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 60%/70%/80%/90%/100% Darkness ATK, 80%/85%/90%/95%/100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Diamond (4)
- **Cooldown:** 5
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

The end of the show. Can be used by any weapon.

All enemies within X Range (X = 3 + Tempo) take Dark magic damage, which is boosted by 15% per Tempo level, and then your Tempo is reset to 0. This skill will also trigger One-Two Steps on damaged enemies.

If used at 6+ Tempo, the damage ignores immunity, reflect, and absorb.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Curtain_Call)

### Dancer's Invitation

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 12/11/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 8
- **Power:** 1/2/3 Steps Taken
- **Target:** Diamond (8)
- **Cooldown:** 3
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dance skill. Invite your foes close for a dance with a saucy move or two.

Targets all enemies within 8 Range, inviting them to move towards you X tiles (based on Rank). Player characters require a Status Infliction check to be lured, unless they're Charmed. Boss units are immune.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dancer's_Invitation)

### Fan Throw

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** +5
- **Target:** Line (5)
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Fan skill. Toss your fan, sending it out as a projectile in a 5 Size Line. The first enemy it encounters takes armor-ignoring damage equal to 5 + its Power, of its damage type, and applies on-hit effects to them.

This skill is not a Dance, but it will not stop your Singing nor alter your Tempo.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fan_Throw)

### Idol Step

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 50/45/40/35/30
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 80/90/100/110/120% Light ATK, 5/10/15/20/25 Charm Lv, 3/6/9/12/15 Battle Rush Lv.
- **Target:** Single (5)
- **Cooldown:** 7
- **Flags:** Enemy, Ally, or Self

Dance skill. A simple but invigorating dance that affects all allies and enemies within 5 Range. Affected units may be inflicted with Charm LV X and are given Battle Rush LV X (based on Rank) for 3 rounds each. You will step X times (X = Rank) in place, and each time you do, you will heal a random ally and deal Light magic damage to a random enemy within 5 Range (based on Rank). If a specific enemy or ally is hit multiple times, the effectiveness is reduced by 50%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Idol_Step)

### Rose Toss

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/20/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 5/10/15 LV Charm, +10/20/30 FP
- **Target:** Single (4)
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Flags:** Location

Dance skill. Throw a rose to a target tile within 4 Range, triggering One-Two Steps on all enemies. If the target tile is empty, all enemies within 3 Range of that tile have a chance to be Charmed LV X (based on Rank, 3 rounds). If it has an enemy, it only affects them, but also inflicted them with Holding Rose if successful.

If an enemy is defeated by One-Two Step triggered by this skill, you gain Boisterous Dancer for 5 rounds, and recover FP for each.

##### Holding Rose

Holding a rose. Disables Two-Hand talent bonuses. Against enemies charming you, -LV Hit and -LV% Parry Activation chance.

##### Boisterous Dancer

You are just... so... COOL! +LV Critical, Evade, and Charm Infliction Rate. +10% Item Drop Rate.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Rose_Toss)

### Skating Spin

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/14/13/12/11/
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1-3
- **Power:** 50%/65%/80%/95%/110% Scaled WPN ATK, 5/10/15/20/25 Base
- **Target:** Line (1-3)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dance skill. An unarmed dance in a 1-3 Range line that must end in an unoccupied tile. You spin through the line, punching and kicking enemies, and every tile you move, you deal Blunt physical damage (based on Rank) to a random enemy within 1 Range. (Kick damage boosting effects apply to this skill's damage. Enemies can only be hit once per skill use.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Skating_Spin)

### Rest Beat

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 0/0/0
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 10/20/30 HP/FP per Tempo
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 8 turns
- **Flags:** Self Only

Dancing is hard work, so it's important to rest now and then. Requires Tempo. Restores HP and FP (based on Rank) and cures 1 negative status effect caused by an enemy for every 1 Tempo you have, and then resets it to 0.

### Discourteous Dip

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 45/25/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** Knockdown enemy.
- **Target:** Single (1)
- **Cooldown:** 8 turns
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dip your partner back, as in any tasteful dance... but then let them hit the ground.

Targets 1 enemy within 1 Range that is not Airborne or Flying, and inflicts Knocked Down on them. If you have Tempo, this skill's cooldown is reduced by its level when using this skill. (Note: This is not a dance skill, so you will lose your Tempo afterwards.)

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/20/20/20/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 70%/80%/90%/100%/110% Damage
- **Target:** Single (5)
- **Cooldown:** 2 turns
- **Flags:** Ally or Enemy

Dance skill. A deadly dance, with an even deadlier partner.

Targets 1 ally (except yourself) or 1 Charmed enemy within 5 Range. Both you and your target will spin around, attacking all enemies (of yours, if an enemy is selected) within 1 Range of each other. This attack deals X% normal attack damage (based on Rank).

### Dancer's Tempo

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Rank 1:** Learn Skill

You are adept at building up a rhythm into a wonderful performance.

Using a Dance skill increases your Tempo by 1 (max: 6). The damage of your Dance skills is amplified by (Tempo*5)%, but increases their FP cost by (Tempo*2). Furthermore, provides a bonus to all Elemental ATK equal to Tempo*2. Using a non-Dance skill resets your Tempo to O, but it will persist between rounds. (Basic actions will not reset Tempo. These include Movement, Attack, Skip, and End Turn.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dancer's_Tempo)

### Dancer's Tempo

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Rank 1:** Learn skill, Chance: 25% Scaled CEL + 10% Per Tempo

The power of a dancer's flexibility in full force, making fools of foes.

When an enemy with 1 Range of you performs a basic attack on you, while at least 1 other enemy is also within 1 Range of you, there is a chance that you will swerve out of the way, redirecting the attack to one of those other enemies but halving its damage. Triggering this effect requires 10 FP, and an enemy can only be affected by Master of the Stage once per round (unless you are monoclassing Dancer).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Master_of_the_Stage)

### Dancing Circles

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Rank 1:** Learn Skill

Toggle skill. While toggled on, damage you deal (except from One-Two Step) will not defeat enemies, leaving them at 1 HP instead. Furthermore, damage taken from enemies with 10 HP or less is reduced by 50%, as being so weak gives them two left feet.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dancing_Circles)

### Limbo

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Duck Chance:** 8/16/24 + Scaled CEL% Chance

Given your flexibility, you're more than capable of ducking under something when necessary. If an enemy projectile would hit you, there is a chance (based on Scaled CEL and Rank) that you will duck under it, and it will pass over you as if it didn't hit you. (Only applies to projectiles created by skills, not projectile basic attacks.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Limbo)

### One-Two Step

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Damage Increase:** 5/10/15/20/25

Dance skills that deal damage to an enemy also give them a status called 'One-Two (X)' (X = damage type) for 3 rounds. When they are hit by a basic attack, that status is consumed to deal armor-ignoring bonus magic damage of the same type to that enemy and all other enemies within 2 Range of the target.

(Different damage types will stack.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/One-Two_Step)

### Dancer's Agility

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Bonus Evade:** 5/10/15

Even weapons and claws can serve as dance partners.

Using a Dance skill gives you a bonus to Evade for 1 attack/2 rounds (based on Rank). This effect can stack, up to a maximum of 6 attacks/3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dancer's_Agility)

- **Max rank:** 1

Just pretend your mistakes were part of the show.

Damage dealt by Dance skills do not cause a Momentum penalty when hitting resistances, absorbs, or immunities.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dancer's_Confidence)

Not everyone can keep up with a true dancer.

### Signature Step

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Infliction Used:** 25%/40%/55%

Your dances sometimes leave their own 'autograph' behind.

Dance skills that deal elemental damage have a chance to inflict an appropriate status effect (per damage hit) for 2 rounds, using a percentage of your normal infliction rate (based on Rank). The LV of these statuses are predetermined based on the type.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Signature_Step)

If you're flexible enough, even longer steps pose no challenge.
