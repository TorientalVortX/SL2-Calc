## Skills

### Execute

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 30/31/32/33/34
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 110/120/130/140/150% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Sword, Spear or Axe only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Missing HP Bonus Damage:** +5/10/15/20/25%
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Can only target 1 enemy within 1 Range that is Knocked Down. Deals physical damage that ignores evasion to the target, increased by a percentage of their missing HP (max +100 damage), but cures their knockdown, making it a good idea to make sure they won't get back up after this blow.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Execute)

### Incise

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 6/7/8/9/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 50/55/60/65/70% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Lingering LV:** 4/8/12/16/20
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Sword or Dagger skill. Targets 1 enemy within 1 Range and deals Pierce physical damage to them, as well as inflicting Lingering Pierce Damage LV X (based on Rank) for 2 rounds. Daggers deal only 80% normal damage, but use 1 less Momentum, and cause the Lingering Damage's LV and duration to be increased by 50%.

If the target of this skill already has Lingering Pierce Damage, its duration will be reset and its LV will be increased by only 25% of the normal value.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Incise)

### Mad Chop

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17/18/19
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 80/90/100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Axe only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Axe skill. Repeatedly smash your axe into a target in 1 Range, dealing Slash physical damage over several hits. The total damage is increased by 0.5% for every 1% of HP you are missing. (Can only be used once per round.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Mad_Chop)

### Retreating Swipe

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/105/110/115/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Front Only Circle
- **Restriction:** Sword only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Sword skill. A close range swipe as you fall back, dealing physical damage of your weapon's damage type to all enemies within 1 Range, except those behind you. Afterwards, if the tile directly behind you is unoccupied, you will jump backwards to it.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Retreating_Swipe)

### Roundtrip

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 12/13/14/15/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/105/110/115/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (5)
- **Restriction:** Axe only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Rank 3:** Second hit pulls enemies towards you.
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Axe skill. Throw your weapon in a line, dealing Slash physical damage to all affected. Then, the axe will return to you, dealing Slash physical damage again. (Half the damage is dealt on the first trip and the other half on the return trip)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Roundtrip)

### Shinken

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/11/12/13/14/15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1, 5-10 (projectile only)
- **Power:** 100/105/110/115/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Sword only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Bonus Damage:** +2/4/6/8/10
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Sword skill. Swipes your sword against the ground, creating a gust of sharp wind that will travel up to 5 tiles, starting behind the tile directly in front of you. If it hits an enemy, they take Slash physical damage. In addition, if there is an enemy directly in front of you in the direction you use this skill in, you will perform a basic attack on them. If no enemy is attacked, the projectile's base Range is doubled. (Bonus damage is given to both based on Rank.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Shinken)

### Enchant Shield

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Duration:** 3/4/5
- **Elemental LV:** 5/10/15

Shield skill. Requires you to be enchanted with an elemental (item effects that treat you as if you were enchanted with one will not work). Transfers the elemental from you to your shield, ending the effect for you, but granting you an Elemental Shield for 3 rounds of the same element.

The elemental shield will grant resistance to the same element equal to X% (X = Rank*5) and will deal damage (ignores armor) to enemies who attack you or that you Bash equal to X. Guarding will double the elemental resistance and retaliation damage, but no effect is given when under Guard Break.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Enchant_Shield)

### Protect

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **DEF:** +1/2/3/4/5

Shield skill. Allows you to take basic attacks (or skills that perform them) in the place of an ally within melee range. When you intercept an attack, you gain DEF (based on Rank) until your next turn. Can be toggled on and off.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Protect)

### Watchful Eye

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 5
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 7
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Targets 1 enemy within 7 Range and focuses on them intently (you can only focus one target at a time with this skill) for 5 rounds, granting a bonus +5 Hit against them. When the target uses Movement or most movement skills, or damages you, you will follow and face them, preventing them from getting behind you. (Teleports are too fast to see. You must have a line of sight on the target to follow them. Does not function while Blinded.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Watchful_Eye)

### Charge

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 8/6/4
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Move Bonus/Bash Charge Distance:** 3/4/5

Gather your energy in a charge, which boosts your Move based on rank for 1 turn. If you use Movement and are not within 3 Range of an enemy, the effect ends and this skill is put on a 3 Round cooldown. Ends similar movement effects from class skills.

If you Bash an enemy while under the effect of Charge, you will move along with that enemy in a line, pushing them away and pinning yourself against them in the same motion. (The Bash Charge Distance and the Move Bonus are the same.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge)

### Charging Strike

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Bonus Damage:** +5/10/15/20/25 Damage

Learning to put your momentum behind an attack can have impressive results. When you use Charge and Movement during the same round, in that order, your next basic attack with a melee weapon, or your next Soldier Offensive skill, will end Charge's effect, but deal bonus damage based on Rank, and may have an additional effect.

#### Additional Effects

- Charging Shinken - Increases the range of Shinken's projectile.
- Charging Retreating Swipe - Increases the distance of the retreat.
- Charging Thousand Stab - Ignores Evasion.
- Charging Turnover - Inflicts Knockdown.
- Charging Roundtrip - Increases the reach of Roundtrip. (Based on distance moved.)
- Charging Mad Chop - Becomes a single attack, and knocks the target back. (Based on distance moved.)

#### Unconfirmed Effects

- Charging Incise - Probably treats as if Incise is executed twice. (Bonus effects only.)
- Charging Execute - Unknown.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Charging_Strike)

### Flatfoot

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Evade Penalty:** -5 Evade

A true Soldier knows that there's more to combat than just hitting your opponent until they stop moving, even though that's a big part of it. You need to use tactics, use terrain and your non-weapon body parts to create openings to hit your opponent, because they sure aren't going to do it for you.

When you use Bash on an enemy, infict the enemy with LV1 Flatfoot (-5 evade -1 move) until the start of their next turn. If you are charge bashing, there is a 50% chance to knock them down instead.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Flatfoot)

### Weapon Discipline

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Durability Cost Reduction:** 10/20/30/40/50%

When you're trained to use weapons, and then trained to repair those weapons, you get a feel for using it in a way that won't damage it nearly as much. Reduces the durability cost of using weapons, skills, and casting spells by a percentage, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Weapon_Discipline)

### Bulwark

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

One of the most basic combat rules is 'always have your shield up', and true Soldiers follow this discipline religiously. When you have a shield equipped, all damage you take from enemies you are facing is reduced by 15%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bulwark)

### Fortitude

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Max HP Bonus:** +15/30/45/60/75

A life on the front-line offers some benefits, namely that you learn how to stay alive longer on them. Increases your maximum HP.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fortitude)

### Tenacity

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **HP Regen:** 1/2/3

A type of physical stamina that works well in long, drawn-out battles. You regain HP every round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Tenacity)
