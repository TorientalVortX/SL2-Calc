## Skills

### Air Pressure

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/22/24
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Wind ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (3)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Sylphid

Targets a circular location and pulses powerful wind gusts from it, dealing Wind magic damage to all enemies inside of it. It will also push certain field objects, such as rocks, away. If an enemy is hit by one of those objects, they are stunned, reducing their Momentum to 0 this turn and destroying that object. If you are enchanted with Talvyd, the object pushback is stronger.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Air_Pressure)

### Explosion

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/22/24
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Fire ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (3-6)
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Nerifian

Targets a Circular location, and begins rapidly compressing air inside as a Fuse Field Objective (X turn, +1 per extra circle size, X = numbers of battle participants or 8, whichever is lower.), when it explodes, it deals Fire magic damage to all enemies inside of it. If enchanted with Nerhaven, the Explosion may inflict burn, and will knock enemies airborne, and then away from the center by 3 tiles.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Explosion)

### Famiuga

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/22/24
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Ice ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Star (4)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Aquarian

Deals Ice magic damage to all enemies within the area of effect. All tiles in the area are also covered in ice, which will prematurely end movement by 1 tile for every ice sheet walked upon. These ice sheets will last up to 3 turns.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Famiuga)

### Fir

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/18/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 90/95/100% Fire ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (4)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Nerifian
- **Excel Crash:** Can be enhanced with weapon charges.

Unleashes a fireball in a line in one direction that travels up to 4 Range. First enemy hit takes Fire damage. If enchanted with Nerhaven, the fireball explodes when it hits an enemy or at the end of its travel, dealing its damage to all enemies in an AoE.

This spell can be enhanced with the usage of weapon charges.

- LV1: Ignores Evasion.
- LV2: +3 Range. Pierces enemies. Projectile hitbox and size increased.
- LV3: +25% Damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fir)

### Isendo

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 90/95/100% Earth ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (2)
- **Flags:** Location
- **Bonus Damage:** 4/6/8
- **Domain:** Isespian
- **Excel Crash:** Can be enhanced with weapon charges.

Targets a 2 size circle around the user and deals Earth Magic damage to all enemies inside of it; Enemies within 1 range of the center take bonus damage. All enemies magnetized by the users are drawn one square toward the center before damage is dealt. If enchanted with Galren, the magnetization is stronger, pulling enemies in 3 squares, and increases the area of effect by 2 tiles.

This spell can be enhanced with the usage of weapon charges.

- LV1: +10% Damage per Charge Level.
- LV2: Magnetize pull effect strengthened by 3. Size increased by 1. Enemies who take the close bonus damage are knocked Airborne.
- LV3: Ignores Evasion.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Isendo)

### Miu

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 90/95/100% Ice ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (4)
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Aquarian
- **Excel Crash:** Can be enhanced with weapon charges.

Launches a projectile that pierces enemies in a 4 Range Line. All tiles in the line are covered in ice, which will prematurely end movement by 1 tile for every ice sheet walked upon. If enchanted with Kraken, it also has a chance to inflict Frostbite LV (1 + Rank*3) for 3 rounds, which damages enemies who step on ice tiles.

This spell can be enhanced with the usage of weapon charges.

- LV1: Ignores Evasion.
- LV2: +3 Range. Projectile hitbox and size increased.
- LV3: +25% Damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Miu)

### Rye

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 11/12/13
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 7
- **Power:** 90/95/100% Lightning ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Nature
- **Excel Crash:** Can be enhanced with weapon charges.

Calls down a thunderbolt at a target enemy within 7 Range, dealing lightning magic damage to them. Damage is slightly lowered near maximum range. If enchanted with Redgull, the lightning may critically hit, dealing extra damage.

### Stone Dragon

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/22/24
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Earth ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (4-6)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Bonus Damage Per Tile:** +1/2/3
- **Domain:** Isespian

Send a dragon made of stone flying from the earth in a line from target location. The longer the distance it travels, the more Earth magic damage it will deal to the first enemy hit. If the dragon travels within 1 square of someone under the effect of Astral Belt, it boosts the damage by its LV*4. If enchanted with Galren, the dragon will crumble into dense rocks that last for 2 rounds, after it finishes.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Stone_Dragon)

### Astral Belt

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 8/10/12/14/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally or Self Only
- **Defense Bonus:** +3/4/5
- **Domain:** Isespian

Surrounds yourself or target ally with protective stones, increasing their DEF. If a magnetized enemy is pulled with 1 square of someone under this effect, they take Earth magic damage equal to 4x the DEF bonus and are knocked airborne via polarity reversal. This effect lasts for 3 rounds, but may be extended by effects that lengthen Magnetize. Also grants a special skill called Astral Gravity that uses up the buff to quickly pull nearby enemies closer.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Astral_Belt)

### Auto-Enchant

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Activation Chance:** 20/40/60/80/100%

A toggle skill. When on, at the start of a battle, you have a chance to automatically use the first Mage enchant spell equipped in your skill pool on yourself, for free.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Auto-Enchant)

### Elemental Augment

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Elemental Damage:** 30/40/50% Elemental ATK

Allows you to utilize elementals offensively while enchanted with them. When you (or someone enchanted with one of your Elementals, in which case it will use their Elemental ATK instead of yours.) hit with a basic attack, your basic attacks will deal elemental Magic damage to the target, based on Rank. The damage type depends on the elemental you are enchanted with (for example, you will deal Fire damage if Nerhaven, etc). If applied by a Gun attack, the damage is divided by the Gun's total rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Elemental_Augment)

### Whispering Cant

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Allows you to use the simple Offensive spells offered by the Mage base class even while you are Silenced, but increases their FP cost by 50% while under that effect.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Whispering_Cant)

### Tome Specialist

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Nothing beats a good tome, and there as many as there are Mages. Grants +5 Hit with Tome weapons. When dealing damage with a Tome of a damage type that matches the Tome's, or its material's element, grants an additional +5 Hit.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Tome_Specialist)
