### Altera

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 30/28/26/24/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 80/90/100/110/120% Darkness ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (3) + Farlight
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Huggessoan

Unleash a black fog from your lantern, covering all enemies within 3 Range and dealing Dark magic damage to them. It can also inflict Blind for 2 rounds on affected enemies.

(Damage/Heal and Effect Power/Duration/Chance from Lantern Activations are more effective when close and less effective when far.)

### Eternal Flame

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/14/13/12/11
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Flame Strength:** 1/2/3/4/5
- **Duration Bonus:** +0/0/1/1/2

A magical flame that resides in the lantern of the Lantern Bearer. It causes lantern spells to have a lingering effect that increases certain statistics equal to the Flame Strength. If Lantern Bearer is your Main Class, Eternal Flame is always active as long as it is equipped. If it is your Sub Class, you must activate this skill, and it is active for a certain number of rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Eternal_Flame)

### Bask in Light

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Elemental Resistance Bonus:** 2/4/6/8/10%

At the end of a round, if Eternal Flame is active, and you have not used Movement this turn, your flame glows quietly, causing you and all allies within 3 Range to gain damage resistance to its element for 2 rounds, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bask_in_Light)

### Gentle Embers

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** No longer takes up a skill slot when equipped.

A pacifist's approach to lantern bearing. While toggled on, lantern activations will not deal damage or inflict status effects. Instead, all allies in the lantern's aura will gain immunity to the status it normally inflicts, if any, for 2 rounds. It will also reduce the FP cost of Lantern spells (except General domain ones) by 50%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Gentle_Embers)

### Hard Light

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Elemental Resistance Bonus:** 5/10/15/20/25%

The warmth of your lantern creates a protective barrier around you. While Eternal Flame is active, you gain X% (based on Rank) resistance to the element of the last lantern flame you invoked.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hard_Light)

### Imbue Strike

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Restriction:** Spear only
- **Rank 2:** No longer takes up a skill slot when equipped

Lantern Bearers can cause strikes they make to hold the power of their lantern. Basic attacks with Spear weapons will, while this skill is toggled on, change their damage type to your currently active Eternal Flame's damage type.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Imbue_Strike)

### Raging Flame

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Damage:** 2/4/6/8/10

The light from your lantern becomes hotter, burning those within its light. At the start of every round, enemies within your Lantern's range take unresistable damage (based on Rank) of your last invoked flame's element.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Raging_Flame)

### Farlight

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Additional Range:** +1/2/3

The Lantern Bearer's lantern burns more brightly, increasing the reach of all auras, Lantern Bearer spells, and Lantern Bearer bonus effects.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Farlight)

### Warding Light

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Resistance Boost:** +1/+2/+3/+4/+6

A protection against magic that the Lantern Bearer's lantern leaks, increases RES passively. If at max Rank, RES is further increased by +1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Warding_Light)
