## Skills

### Aliagmato

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Line (2-3)
- **Cooldown:** 1
- **Restriction:** Spear only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Bonus Damage:** +5/10/15
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Spear skill. Elemental compatible. Dash in a 2-3 Range line, performing a basic attack with bonus damage on all enemies in that line. If you have Bonded Youkai summoned, they will duplicate the attack themselves in the direction they are facing. If you have only one Bonded Youkai, the Range of this skill is increased by 3. Must end in an unoccupied square. (Those who do not satisfy this condition will not attack.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Aliagmato)

### Infernal Summon

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 25
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** 90/105/120/135/150% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (3)
- **Restriction:** Axe only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Axe skill. Elemental compatible. Targets a 3 Range line, summons the first alive and unsummoned Bonded Youkai available, and sends it along that line. If the Youkai encounters an enemy along its path, it will stop, deal damage (type based on Youkai) to them. If you have only one Bonded Youkai, the Youkai will also perform a basic attack on that target.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Infernal_Summon)

### Pinball Strike

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/14/13/12/11
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2 Round
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Knockback:** 2 + 3/4/5/6/7 Tiles, Hit + 5/10/15/20/25

Targets an empty tile within 5 range and teleports to it. If there is an enemy within 1 Range of that tile you attack them; they are knocked back 2 + Rank tiles. Any bonded youkai within 1 Range of them while being knocked down automatically attack them,with bonus hit.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Pinball_Strike)

### Riagri

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 30/28/26/24/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** 100% Scaled WPN ATK + 4/6/8/10/12 Bonus DMG
- **Target:** Line (5)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

(Swords, Axes, and Spears can be used as casting tools for this spell.) Can only be used if you have Bonded Youkai. For each Bonded Youkai, a projectile is launched in a 5 Range line in one direction. A projectile that hits the target will deal magic damage (damage type depends on the Youkai). If you have only one Bonded Youkai, the damage is also increased by half of that Youkai's Scaled WIL, and the Range is increased by 2.

Note: Projectile 2 will be fired at a 45 degree turn from your direction. Projectile 3 will be fired from a -45 degree turn from your direction.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Riagri)

### Ritual Sword

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Sword only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **FP Steal:** 1/5/9/13/17

Sword skill. Performs a basic attack on 1 enemy within 1 Range. If the target is successfully hit by the attack, you steal FP from the target, based on Rank. (If the attack was a critical hit, the FP steal is also increased.) If you have only one Bonded Youkai, you also summon that Youkai to your side. (If it is summoned already, it is moved to a random tile next to you. If the Youkai is already within 2 Range of the attacked enemy, this effect will not trigger.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ritual_Sword)

### Contract Tres

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

While equipped, all Youkai except for the first 3 Youkai in your contract list will be ignored. (You can't summon or install them, and they will not count against you for the purposes of determining Bonder benefits. You can rearrange the order of your contracts list in the Companions menu. If Contract Uno is equipped as well, this skill has no effect.)

### Fight As One

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Bonus:** +4/7/10

When you and your Bonded Youkai are within 2 Range of each other, all of you gain a bonus to Hit, Evade, Critical, and Critical Evade, based on Rank. (All of your Bonded Youkai must be summoned for this to take effect.) If you have only one Bonded Youkai, the effect is doubled.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fight_As_One)

### Hell Above

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

When a Bonded Youkai deals damage to an enemy, you inflict Hell Above on that target for 3 rounds. When an enemy with Hell Above takes damage from you, the status is consumed and deals protection-ignoring Akashic damage to the target equal to 5% of the damage dealt. (If you have only one Bonded Youkai, it does 10% instead.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hell_Above)

### Parted Pain

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

When summoned, your Bonded Youkai gain a permanent status that increases their damage dealt by 15% and halves their Maintenance and Skill FP costs, but your Youkai cannot bring enemies below 15% of their maximum HP (Any excess damage that would bring them below that is ignored).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Parted_Pain)

### Shared Joy

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Toggle skill. While active, if you have Bonded Youkai summoned, any source that restores your HP triggers this skill. When triggered, the healing is reduced by 70%, but you and all summoned Youkai will receive that healing together.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Shared_Joy)

### Shared Pain

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Toggle skill. While active, if you have Bonded Youkai summoned, damage taken by you or your Bonded Youkai triggers this skill. When triggered, the damage taken is reduced by 30%, but you and all summoned Youkai suffer that damage together.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Shared_Pain)

### Synchro Summon

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **LV:** 2/3/4/5/6

Synchronize your mind with your Youkai's, granting them bonus stats (based on Rank) for 3 rounds after being Summoned. If you have only one Bonded Youkai, you also gain the effects of this skill when applied.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Synchro_Summon)

### Grand Finale

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Requires Parted Pain. Defeating a monster in melee range that was within Parted Pain's HP threshold restores 3M and resets Repeat Action for you, but your damage against monsters outside of Parted Pain's HP threshold is reduced by 30%.

If Heaven Below is active, triggering this effect increases it's active LV by 3.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Grand_Finale)

### Heaven Below

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Requires Parted Pain. At the start of every round, grants or increases Heaven Below at LV2 (max 10). LV decreases by 1 upon taking non-bonus non-status, non-field effect damage. Increases damage against enemies within Parted Pain's threshold by LV * 2%, and upon defeating them, restores LV * 5 FP, and heals all summoned Bonded Youkai's HP and FP for that same amount. Every enemy you defeat that procs this effect increases the maximum level by 10. If you end the battle with a maximum level of 30 or more, all allies gain 10% bonus EXP and 10% drop rate boost.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Heaven_Below)

### Spiritual Bond

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Bonded Youkai Maximum:** 1/2/3
- **Max Level:** 50/55/60

Allows you to have Bonded Youkai and increases their maximum possible level (max. 60). The following conditions are required to have a Bonded Youkai.

Your total contracts must not exceed the Rank of this skill.

The friendship of that Youkai must be above average (50%).

If you do not satisfy the first condition, no Youkai you have will be treated as Bonded. If you do not satisfy the second condition, only the Youkai with low friendship will not be treated as Bonded.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Spiritual_Bond)

### Symbol of Trust

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **WIL/FAI:** +1/2/3

Being a Bonder means putting your trust in your Youkai allies to cover your weaknesses. As such, this skill increases the WIL and FAI of the character, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Symbol_of_Trust)
