## Skills

### Dive Down

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 25/23/21/19/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Spear only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Hit bonus:** +5/10/15/20/25

Spear skill. Can only be used while airborne. Targets 1 enemy within 1 Range and dives down on them, spear first, performing a basic attack on them with a bonus to Hit. If the target is Airborne, if the attack hits, they are knocked down. If they are not, you inflict Aerial Razor LV X (X = Rank * 2) before the attack hits, which lasts until your next turn. Afterwards, you land.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dive_Down)

### Falcon Strike

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 25/23/21/19/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** 90/100/110/120/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Restriction:** Spear only
- **Flags:** Location

Spear skill. Targets a location within 6 Range that is unoccupied, which you swiftly dash towards in a flexible line of tiles, faster than the eye can see. All enemies in those tiles take Pierce physical damage, which ignores evasion. Afterwards, if 3 Magical Feathers are available, they are consumed, and all of your feathers on the battlefield immediately resonate.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Falcon_Strike)

### Kestrel Dive

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 25/23/21
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1-6
- **Power:** 100/115/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Restriction:** Spear only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Must be Airborne. Dive in a line towards a target location, dealing damage to all enemies you encounter, before landing. If 10 Magical Feathers are available, consumes them and wraps you in an Elemental Kestrel for the duration of the skill, which has a variety of effects.

##### Elemental Kestrel

This is a bonus effect gained by Kestrel Dive for consuming a certain amount of Magical Feathers. It bestows the following benefits to the skill, based on your current elemental (or Fire, if none):

- Skill damage is applied to all tiles within 1 Range every time you move, instead of just the tile you move over.
- Grants an additional hit of elemental damage equal to 100% of your Elemental ATK.
- If Feather Veil is active, and you damage an airborne enemy with the skill damage; they are grabbed and carried along with you, and slammed into the ground at the end of the flight, taking 35 additional armor-ignoring Blunt damage and having an infliction-based chance to be Knocked Down.

Once the flight ends, the Elemental Kestrel explodes in a 2 Range Circle around you, applying the skill's damage to all enemies in that radius, and creating field tiles based on your elemental for 2 rounds, with a LV equal to half of your Magical Feather LV.

Enemies can only be affected by this skill once per use, even if it misses.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Kestrel_Dive)

### Lift Off

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 25/23/21/19/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** 80/90/100/110/120% Fire ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (2)
- **Restriction:** Spear only
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** General

Airborne compatible. Spears can be used as casting tools. Slam your spear into the ground and create a magical burst of elemental energy, which deals elemental damage (based on element enchant - Fire by default) to all enemies in a 2 Range circle around you, knocking them airborne. If 5 Magical Feathers are available, they're consumed and all tiles in the area are covered by magical feathers, and the damage ignores evasion.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Lift_Off)

### Ignite Power

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Range:** 2/3/4

Absorb the power from the nearby elements. At the start of a new round, gain +X Scaled Weapon Power for 1 round, where X is the number of a specific special effect tile in a certain Range around you. The special effect tile depends on your enchanted element, which defaults to Fire if none are present. (Fire = Cinder, Ice = Ice Sheet, Earth = Plants, Wind = Air Shaft, Lightning = Light Shaft)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ignite_Power)

### Kagero

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

When you attack an enemy unit and trigger a counter-attack skill (such as Riposte) while the tile behind them is unoccupied, if 4 Magical Feathers are available, they are consumed. You then create an elemental copy of yourself at your current location, which takes the attack in your place and explodes, dealing elemental magic damage equal to 100% of your elemental ATK to all enemies in 2 Range of that location. Meanwhile, you appear behind your opponent and create an Air Shaft there for 3 rounds. (Element effect is based on your current elemental enchant, or Fire if none.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Kagero)

### Magical Feathers

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2

The Firebird is defined by magical feathers that aid them. You begin with 10 and generate 2 automatically every new round (max 30). Tiles you move on will consume 1 and cover it. At the end of the round, they resonate, dealing magic damage of your enchanted element (treated as Fire, if none) to any enemy in that tile, then expire. (Damage = Firebird LV + 5, ignores armor)

They also destroy special tiles in their location. (Fire = Ice Sheet, Ice = Cinders, Earth = Void Pollution, Lightning = Dark Water, Wind = Blows away instead.)

At Rank 2, this skill does not take up a skill slot.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Magical_Feathers)

### Roc's Rise

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Mainclass Only Skill. When you recover from a knockdown, you unleash a quick burst of magical energy around you, dealing damage equal to your Magical Feather damage and of the same element to all enemies within 2 Range; enemies within 1 Range are also knocked back 1 tile. In addition, if at least 4 Magical Feathers are available, the Momentum penalty of the knockdown is reduced by 1 per 4 consumed. (IE, 4 feathers = -2M, 8 = -1M, 12 = -0M)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Roc's_Rise)

### Boon of Apus

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Stay strong, stay safe, stay close, stay quick. During battle, after using 1 each of Offensive, Defensive, Support, and Utility class skills, gain 30 Magical Feathers immediately. (This effect then resets and can be gained again. Only applies if the skill costs at least 3M or is from Firebird.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Boon_of_Apus)

### Light Fall

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

You can float through the air as if you were your namesake. Using Movement while you are Airborne and have at least 10 Magical Feathers available will change it to flight movement.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Fall)

### Plume Echo

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Mainclass Only Skill. As a true Firebird, your feathers are magical enough to get another go. Your Magical Feathers do not expire after the first time they resonate (but will the second time).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Plume_Echo)

### Preening

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Take your free moments to make sure everything is straight and even. Converts your unused Momentum into Magical Feathers at a ratio of 1:1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Preening)
