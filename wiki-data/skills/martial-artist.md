## Skills

### Geldoren

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 6/8/10/12/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **DEF/RES Penalty:** -1/2/3/4/5

Fist skill. A sweeping tiger strike that cracks through an enemy's defenses. Performs a basic attack on the target with a bonus to damage equal to 2*Rank. If it hits, the target loses DEF and RES for the duration. This lasts for 3 rounds. If the attack is a critical hit, the status power and duration are increased by 1.5x.

Urawaza: Inflicts Fear and knocks back the target on a successful hit.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Geldoren)

### Heaven Kick

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 12/14/16/18/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Your Move
- **Power:** 80/85/90/95/100% Scaled WPN ATK + 2/2/2.5/2.5/3 per distance
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Fist skill. Launch yourself into the air and fall down onto the target enemy, dealing more damage the further away they are. The Range of this skill is equal to your Move.

Urawaza: Enemies within 5 Range of the target are also affected by this skill as if targeted normally.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Heaven_Kick)

### Light Tomahawk

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 80/85/90/95/100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Bonus Damage:** 10/13/16/19/22 - Torso Weight

Fist skill. Use your leg strength to quickly flip yourself into a quick kick at an enemy in 1 Range, dealing Blunt physical damage. Having a low weight Torso equipped will increase the damage. If you use this skill while Airborne, the maximum potential damage is doubled as you drop down on your enemy, ending your Airborne status.

Urawaza: Rank*20% chance to inflict Guard Break on guarding enemies.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_Tomahawk)

### Peddling Wheel

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/105/110/115/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (4)
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Fist skill. Spin your body in a somersault, while simultaneously punching and kicking any enemies you pass. You perform this attack in a line that must end in an unoccupied square, and deal Blunt physical damage to every enemy you pass over, or within 1 cardinal tile of a square you pass through.

Urawaza: All enemies hit are also thrown Airborne.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Peddling_Wheel)

### Meditate

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Bonus Recovery:** +5/10/15/20/25

Cannot be used while Airborne. Enter a meditating state, focusing your ki and increasing the duration of all positive status effects you have by 1 round. At the start of the next round, you recover HP equal to 200% of your Scaled SKI, plus a bonus based on Rank (you also recover FP equal to half of this rank based bonus). Fear and being knocked back will halve the effectiveness of the heal.

Urawaza: Reduce incoming damage by a percentage equal to the bonus heal until the healing effect triggers.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Meditate)

### Cleanse Body

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Self
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Cumulatively Removes:** Slow/Poison/Fear/Immobilize/Blind

Enter a meditative posture and channel ki to remove harmful effects from your body. If you are affected by certain status effects (based on Rank), they are immediately healed. (The listed status removals are cumulative, so you will remove both Slow and Poison at Rank 2, for example.)

Status effects with a LV higher than your WIL will not be cured.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cleanse_Body)

- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 5

### Crane Hop

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 3
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Range:** 2/3/4
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location

Can only be used once per round. A simple jump that causes you to move to a nearby tile and become Airborne in the same motion. Since it's so simple, it has low Momentum and FP costs. If you target a tile with an enemy in it, before you land, you will flip them into the air, causing them to become Airborne instead of you.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Crane_Hop)

### Phoenix Talon

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/14/13
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Light Damage:** 10/20/30

Enchants your kicks for 5 rounds, causing your kick attacks on Airborne targets to deal Light damage (based on Rank) that ignores armor. Being within an allied Lantern Bearer's Phoenix Aura boosts the damage by 50%. When triggered, you recover HP equal to the bonus damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Phoenix_Talon)

### Shukuchi

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/9/8/7/6
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Move Bonus:** +1/2/3/4/5

A special martial art, named such because masters of it appear as if they use magic to get rid of the distance between themselves and their enemy. Increases your Move for 1 round, and causes your movement type to become Teleport.

While under the effects of Shukuchi, Geldoren and Light Tomahawk's Range is increased by the Move bonus. Using one of those skills on an enemy will teleport you next to them.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Shukuchi)

### Combination Strike

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Offensive Skill Bonus Power:** +15%

When it comes to fighting a worthy foe, true Martial Artists know that two things are true; first, if you keep using the same technique over and over, your opponents will undoubtedly create counter-measures against it. Secondly, using various techniques in combination often results in a stronger front.

This skill increases the total Power of all Offensive skills by 15%, provided it is the first time you have used it this round, and only if you've used a different Offensive skill from the same class in the same round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Combination_Strike)

### Evasive Strike

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Damage:** 4/8/12/16/20

When you duck out of the way of a punch, the only proper thing to do is hit them back. Serves them right! When you successfully evade an attack from an enemy within 1 Range, you deal Blunt physical damage to them based on Rank. This damage is not affected by their Defense.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Evasive_Strike)

### Mixed Martial Arts

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

It's not uncommon for users of the sword or other weapons to mix in unarmed attacks in melee combat. For one thing, the 'sharp weapon' element makes them less weary of your fists, and for another, it gives you another avenue of attack, which is invaluable.

As long as you do not have a sub-weapon or shield equipped in your Hands slot, you can use Fist skills without having a Fist equipped (it will use your bare fists instead). Furthermore, if you have access to the Sub-Attack trait, you gain access to a Sub-Attack with your bare fists.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Mixed_Martial_Arts)

### Urawaza

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up any Skill Pool slots.

Toggle skill. What's better than having a special attack? Having a secret special attack. While toggled on, when you use a Martial Artist Offensive skill (or Meditate), you will be prompted to enter a series of directions. If you enter them all successfully, the skill will gain some manner of bonus effect. (Failure has no penalty.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Urawaza)

- **Power/Hit/Critical Bonus:** +3/6/9/12/15

While some Martial Artists prefer to employ gauntlets and other such hand-based weaponry, some still prefer to use absolutely no weapon at all. These users of the empty palm receive a bonus to Power, Hit, and Critical when using their bare fists, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Empty_Palm)

### Weathered Body

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Damage Reduction:** 10%

True Martial Artists treat their body as a weapon and armor, having no use for the over-encumbering metal sheets of other fighters. While you have an Unarmored torso with base Armor + base Magic Armor of 4 or less, are not using a shield, and have a sub-weapon that counts as a Fist weapon (or no sub-weapon), all damage you receive is reduced by 10%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Weathered_Body)
