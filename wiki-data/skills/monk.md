## Skills

### Backflip

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10/5/0
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3 turns
- **Flags:** Ally Only

A simple-looking movement that is actually quite challenging to pull off in practice. Upon using this skill, you can turn yourself in any direction, and when you confirm its usage, you will backflip 3 tiles away from the direction you are facing, without changing direction.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Backflip)

### Dense Thunder

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 110/120/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

A spinning kick that targets an enemy within 1 range, dealing blunt physical damage.

Ki: Knocks them away 1 tile per 3 Ki, to a max of 8, using 1 Ki per tile. Deals Sound magic damage to all enemies in 3 Range equal to half of the original damage if they run into an object. Otherwise, if Shukuchi is active, teleport next to them if the next tile in the same direction is empty, and apply the same effect (half effectiveness).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dense_Thunder)

### Dragon Gale

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1-6
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

A heavy punch followed by a roundhouse kick, dealing damage based on Rank over two hits, to an enemy within 1 Range.

Ki: Gains +1 Range per 3 Ki, to a max. of 6. If you target an enemy outside of melee range, 2 Ki will be consumed per tile, but will launch a ki dragon at the target, applying the effect of this skill, creating air shafts for 3 rounds, but doing Wind magic damage (and bonus damage equal to 100% of Wind ATK).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dragon_Gale)

### Kadouha

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 40/39/38
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5-11
- **Power:** 130/135/140% Light ATK + 130/135/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Consumes Ki, up to a max, and unleashes a ball of it that travels up to X (5+1 per 5 Ki) Range away. ON hit, it deals Light magic damage (+Ki consumed% bonus damage). Gains bonus effects if it consumes enough Ki.

10 Ki: Grows in size, hitting a wider area. Pierces enemies instead of being destroyed. (3 tile width)

20 Ki: Explodes when the projectile ends, dealing 75% of its damage to enemies who weren't hit within 3 Range.

30 Ki: Inflicts Knockdown.

If Ki Awoken is active, this skill will not consume any Ki.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Kadouha)

### Ki Blast

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 5
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 1
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Concentrate your Ki into a small projectile blast. Uses 5 Ki. Targets 1 enemy within 6 Range and performs a basic attack on them with your Fist weapon, which deals magic damage. If Ki Awoken is active, the damage is increased by 15% and is done over 3 hits.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ki_Blast)

### Setting Sun

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 7
- **Power:** 100% Fire ATK + 80/90/100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle
- **Cooldown:** 1 turn
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Targets a 1-X Range circle within 7 Range, and launches a sun-like Ki sphere. It will hover there for 3 rounds until it expires (or this skill is used again), where it will descend, dealing Fire magic damage that ignores evasion to all enemies in its radius.

Ki: Can be resized, +1 maximum size per 5 Ki to a max. of 5. Damage increases by 25% per 1 extra range. Consumes 3 Ki per range, even at the default size. (IE, default = 3, 1+ size = 6, etc).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Setting_Sun)

### Sky Chariot

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 110/120/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

A crashing drop kick that slams into one enemy in 1 range, dealing blunt physical damage to them.

Ki: If used while airborne, and you have 5 or more Ki, 5 is consumed, increasing the damage dealt by 15% and knocking down the target. If the target is already knocked down, the damage is increased by 30% instead and target is inflicted with Immobilize for 2 rounds. Your airborne status then ends.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sky_Chariot)

### Terra Strike

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 110/120/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

You strike the ground with your fist at a location within 1 Range. Any enemy there takes physical Blunt damage and inflicts them with Slow LV3 until your next turn. Also destroys enemy-created tiles in that tile.

Ki: Gains 1 Range per 5 Ki, (max. 6). If the tile is more than 1 Range away, you launch a Ki dragon towards that location, applying its effect at it, dealing Earth damage instead and increasing damage by 100% of Earth ATK. (Costs 1 Ki per range).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Terra_Strike)

### Body of Isesip

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 30/26/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Self Only

Cover your body in a barrier of ki harder than diamond. You gain the 'Body of Isesip' status, which negates all damage, but halves your Evade. If you take damage, the duration is reduced by 1, and if it reaches 0, the status ends. The base duration is 1 round. If 10 Ki is available, it will be consumed and increase the duration by 2 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Body_of_Isesip)

### Aid

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3
- **Flags:** Self or Ally Only
- **Heal Multiplier:** 140/160/180%
- **Ki Generated:** +5/6/7

Targets yourself, or an ally within 5 Range. The target is healed based on your Scaled WIL (based on Rank). If the target was an ally and not yourself, you dash to an empty tile next to them and generate Ki, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Aid)

### Ki Awoken

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self
- **Stat Boost:** +4/7/10
- **Ki Generation:** +3/4/5 Ki

A dangerous skill that boosts Ki Generation and the user's own capabilities by combining their bodies with the Ki itself. Increases all statistics (except VIT) and generates Ki by Rank every round. However, this comes at a risk; it drains HP every subsequent round, starting at 5% of max HP and doubling every round after that (but will never reduce you below 1 HP) while active.

Activate this skill again to end the effect before it's too late (Momentum will be refunded for the second use).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ki_Awoken)

### Power Up

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Monk:** Monk
- **FP Restoration:** +3/4/5
- **Ki Generation:** +1/2/3

Raise your arms and focus your power. Uses up all of your available Momentum, to a minimum of 3, and restores your FP and generates Ki based on the amount consumed, based on Rank. If 6 or more is consumed, both effects are increased by 50%. (The values listed are per point of Momentum).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Up)

### Sun Flare

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 30/20/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Monk:** Monk

You put your hands in front of your face and release a blinding flash of light. Requires and consumes 15 Ki. All enemies within 1 Range are inflicted with Blind for 2 rounds. All other enemies have a luck-based chance to be inflicted with Blind for 2 rounds. All enemies also take armor-ignoring Light magic damage equal to 15 * Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sun_Flare)

### Serpent Strikes

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Restriction:** Fist only

Your spiritual energy augments your strikes. You gain Critical equal to your Ki when using Fist weapons. In addition, at 20 Ki or higher, your Fist basic attacks will get a serpent's bite, dealing Acid magic damage equal to 100% of your Acid ATK on hit.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Serpent_Strikes)

### Suppress Ki

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

The ability to control and contain your ki is highly valuable for a Monk. While toggled on, offensive skills (excluding Kadouha and Setting Sun) that utilize Ki will treat your Ki as 0, and won't consume any Ki when used.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Suppress_Ki)

### Woki

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Range:** 3/3/4
- **STR/WIL:** +2/3/4

Focused, you are a source of positive energy for yourself and allies. When your Ki is 10 or more, you generate an aura that gives all allied units in Range a bonus to STR and WIL.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Woki)

### All Fight

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

True monks know the value of time and being efficient, and therefore try to deal with as many enemies as possible at once. When damaging an enemy, if that enemy has not been damaged by the Monk in same round, the damage is increased by 5% and it generates 2 Ki for the user.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/All_Fight)

### Discipline

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **SKI/CEL Bonus:** +1/2/3

A type of martial arts mastery that instills the importance of patience and precision. Passively increases SKI and CEL.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Discipline)

### Golden Glow

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Required:** Power Up Rank 1
- **Physical/Magical Defense Increase:** 2/4/6/8/10%

A golden glow associated with legends. When using Power Up, if your Ki is increased to the upper limit of 30, you gain Golden Glow for 3 rounds. Golden Glow increases your Phys. and Mag. Def based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Golden_Glow)

### Power Impact

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Required:** Power Up Rank 1

Makes your power up even more of a power up, or maybe a power out. When you use Power Up, all enemies within 2 Range of you will be knocked back by 3 tiles.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_Impact)

### Spirituality

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Ki Per Round:** +1/2/3

You generate a special energy called Ki, which is a type of spiritual energy. You gain Ki based on Rank every round, to a maximum of 30. The rate is doubled if Monk is both your Main Class and your Sub Class.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Spirituality)
