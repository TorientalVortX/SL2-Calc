## Skills

### Complex Shatter

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 14/13/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4/5/6
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Targets an enemy within X range (based on Rank), and performs a basic attack on the target with your whip which deals magic damage. If the target is Crystalized, the attack gains a bonus to Hit, and if it hits, the Crystal shatters into 2 gemstones for 2 rounds, landing at random tiles within 2 range of the target.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Complex_Shatter)

### Despot Drill

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/19/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3 to 5
- **Power:** 100%/115%/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Turn your whip into a spinning drill and rush in a 3 to 5 range line. You move until stopped, each step affecting enemies and natural resources in 1 range. Enemies take Pierce Magic damage, and resources are refined. (Enemies are damaged once.) Can guard-break guarding or crystalized units at X% chance ( X = 10 x Ruler/Mage Buffs), on success digs up a rock near the enemy.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Despot_Drill)

### Rawhide Slash

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Crescent (4) - 3 range
- **Power:** 100%/110%/120% Weapon Scaled ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Flourish your whip wildly. Hitting a 4 size crescent area within 3 range. Enemies in those tiles take Slash physical damage, and up to X ( X = rank) Natural Resources in that area cut up by your whip and refined (Ruler) into Geomaterial.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Rawhide_Slash)

### Ruler's Revocation

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 50/48/46
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 8
- **Power:** 75%/100%/125% Elemental ATK
- **Target:** Friendly
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Targets 1 ally within 8 range, and charge all of the non-permanent mage/ruler enchant spell effect that they been granted by you into arcane missiles. These missiles are elemental homing projectile that target random enemies within 10 range around them, which deal armor-ignoring armor damage with Great Accuracy (Can never be missed). The element is based on the domain of the spell that granted the buff.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ruler's_Revocation)

### Slicing Benten

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Line (3 to 5)
- **Power:** 30%/35%/40% Scaled WPN ATK Base: (5/10/15)
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dance skill. Spin your whip in a wild motion around yourself as you dance in a 3-5 range line that must end in an unoccupied tile. Everytime you move, a random enemy and 1 natural resource within 2 range are affected. Enemies take Slash Magic Damage (based on rank), while resources are Refined.

Trickery - Gain Ranged Attack Deflection Lv. 1 until your next turn.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Slicing_Benten)

### Tyrant's Throne

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Self
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Requires 2 Geomaterial. Create a permanent Tyrant's throne under you (only 1 can be active). While on your throne, you can use the Sit on Throne skill. While sitting on your throne, you gain a bonus to DEF/RES/CEL/LUC/STR equal to it's LV x 2, immunity to knockback/pull, and knocked down and gain LV attack range for whip attack, but you are immobile. This effect lasts until your next turn.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Tyrant's_Throne)

- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** 5%/10%/15% Rare Ore Bonus
- **Target:** Allies and Confused Enemies
- **Cooldown:** 2

Exercise your Authority as Ruler and give the order to dig, which washes over all your other allies (Except Rulers), and confused enemies you have damaged this round. ### Ruler's Carriage

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 9
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 5
- **Flags:** Ally only

Whip skill. Location 1 must be within 3 range, and contain a different allied unit that can move, and location 2 must be an unoccupied tile within 9 range. You whip that ally, and pull yourself to them dealing some kickback damage to them. Afterwards, they carry you up to X steps (based on rank) toward Location 2, and then set you down on an unoccupied tile within 1 range. If no such tiles exist, all enemies within 1 range are knocked back 3 tiles, and you are Knocked Down.

### Enhanced Enchantcraft

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2

The Ruler has studied the basics time and time again, letting their mage enchantment effects become a little more potent. Enhances your mage base class enchant spells. When removed before expiring, they create a 2-range magic burst. Deals (LV x 10) armor-ignoring bonus magic damage. Furthermore, if defensive category, casting it on a single target increases its LV by 2.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Enhanced_Enchantcraft)

### Arcane Whip

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

The sign of a Ruler's power is their scourge, and as a being of Arcane skill. It would be nice to show that power, be it through a horse-driver or a cat's string toy. Allows you to use any whip weapon as a casting tool (Tome-whip are exempted as they're already tomes.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Arcane_Whip)

### Patient Scourge

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Most terrifying is not the ruler quickest to lash out, but the one who strikes hardest. Casting a mage/ruler enchant spell grants or power up Patient scourge LV10 (Max Lv50 for 5 Rounds. Patient scourge boosts the damage, and critical of your next whip basic attack and is consumed on hit.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Patient_Scourge)

### Ruler's Geocraft

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Rulers utilize a special resource called Geomaterial for some of their skills. They can have a maximum of 9 Geomaterials stored at once, and begin battle with 3 geomaterial plus per Geoheart enchantment they have on equipped items. When they score a critical hit with a Whip Weapon, they gain 1 Geomaterial.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ruler's_Geocraft)

### Ruler's Reign

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

True Rulers know how to assert their station, using it both as a shield and as a sword. Increase damage dealt to and reduce damage taken by Subservient enemies (Non-boss enemies, Curate/Soldiers, Pack Follower/Little Cheese) by 7% each. if Ruler is both your main class and sub-class, this is doubled. And all enemies except other Rulers are treated as subservient. In addition, increases your skill pool size by 0.5 for every enchant-class spell you have equipped.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ruler's_Reign)
