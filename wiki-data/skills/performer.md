Performer song skill. More worries than just stepping on toes. ### Bewitching Melody

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 25/23/21
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single (6)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only, Main class skill
- **Convert Success Bonus:** 5/10/15%

Performer Song skill. Let them give into the music. Tagets 1 enemy within 5 Range under the Song of Susceptibility; effect differs if the target is a monster or not.

Monster: Tries to Convert Enemy (Performer) Other: Attempts to inflict Pacify until your next turn. (Skill enters 2 round CD if used this way.)

##### Convert Enemy (Performer)

This skill can make a monster into an ally temporarily. If successful, the monster that is converted will be treated as a member of your party for the rest of the battle, will be cured of Confusion, and it will give EXP at the end of it as if you defeated it normally. Bosses, minibosses, and undead-race monsters are immune. You also cannot convert enemies with a level higher than (your level + 10). Only one Converted Enemy can be in your party at a time.

Your success rate is equal to the following: Scaled GUI + (Galdr talent Rank * 2) + (your level - target's level, or 0 if less than 1) + 25 if target is Charmed by you + 25 if target is Confused = success percent.

##### Pacify

Cannot use basic attacks or Offensive skills. Taking non-status, non-bonus damage will reduce its duration by 1 round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bewitching_Melody)
