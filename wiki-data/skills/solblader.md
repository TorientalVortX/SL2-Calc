## Skills

### Chariot Star

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3-5
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Targets a 3-5 Size Line in front of you. You create a shield of light in front of you and fly forward in that line. The first enemy you encounter slams into the shield and is pushed along with you the rest of the distance, taking damage once the flight is over.

If you have the following effects active, gain bonuses: Charge gives +2 Max Range. Holy Shield cause Guard Break. Holy Aura increases damage dealt by 15%, knocks the enemy back 2 tiles and makes them Airborne.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Chariot_Star)

### Godray Undertow

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Targets a 5 Size Line in front of you. You quickly dash through those tiles until you encounter an enemy in the tile you are facing. That enemy takes weapon-based damage and is knocked Airborne.

Holy Aura increases the amount of tiles you move by 2, and will cause the skill to check all tiles within 1 Range of you every step for an enemy, instead of just the tile you are facing. (Applies even if you gain Holy Aura mid-attack.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Godray_Undertow)

### Light On High

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2 Rounds
- **Flags:** Location

Targets an unoccupied tile within 5 Range. Rise like the sun, flying towards the target tile, then slam a hammer of light into the ground, causing all enemies within 1 Range to take Blunt/Light damage.

Airborne enemies may be inflicted with Knocked Down. Otherwise, they are knocked Airborne.

If you have Holy Aura, airborne enemies are always Knocked Down, and their Light Resistance is lowered by 15% for 2 Rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Light_On_High)

### Luminous Arc

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Light ATK & 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 1 Round
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Mercana

Focus light into your casting tool and release it in an arc-shaped projectile, which travels in a line up to 6 tiles away. If it encounters your Solsphere, the projectile becomes enhanced.

Enemies hit by the projectile take Light magic damage. If enchaned, it explodes into a 3 Size Circle when it ends, dealing its damage to all enemies in that area (only once). If you have Holy Aura, the projectile also creates Light Shafts for 2 Rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Luminous_Arc)

### Lux Relido

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 40/38/36
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** 8
- **Power:** 140/160/180% Light ATK & 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single (Line To)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Mercana

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation.

Targets a tile up to 8 Range away and fires a swift, destructive thread of light through it, in a flexible line. All enemies in the path of the beam take Light magic damage with Great Accuracy, and special effect tiles are razed. Critically hits against Glowing enemies for 25% extra damage.

### Photon Ring

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3

- **Range:** 6

### Radiant Surrender

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 30
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** 100/115/130% Light ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2 Rounds
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Mercana
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Must target your Solsphere within 6 Range, releasing its magic and causing it to end, creating a destructive wave of energy.

In a 3 Size Circle around it, creates special effect tiles (based on elemental, Light Shafts by default, 2 rounds) with a LV of 15 and deals Light magic damage to enemies. If the Solsphere was enhanced, +2 Circle Size and +25% damage dealt. If you are within its effective range, you gain Holy Aura.

##### Enchantments

- Fire - Creates Cinder tiles.
- Ice - Creates Ice Sheet tiles.
- Earth - Creates Flower tiles.
- Wind - Creates Air Shaft tiles.
- Lightning - Creates Light Shaft tiles.
- Dark - Creates Darkness tiles.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Radiant_Surrender)

- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single

- **Flags:** Enemy Only

### Solsphere

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location

The blessed art of the Solblader. Targets 1 unoccupied tile within 3 Range and creates a sphere of light called a permanent Solsphere at it. You can only have 1 active at a time.

##### Field Object

This is a special, sun-like field tile that is created by the Solblader class. Only 1 can be active at a time, it can't be destroyed, and its placement can't be stopped by effects like Priest's Sanctuary.

When you enter the same tile as your Solsphere, you gain Holy Aura until your next turn. When your Solsphere and an enemy encounter each other, they take armor-ignoring magic bonus Light damage equal to X + 50% of Light ATK (X = half of Solblader level). This can only occur once per action.

##### Special Interactions

The Solsphere can be interacted with by many Soldier base class skills. If you target the Solsphere with one, the following effects will occur (this will apply even if an enemy and the Solsphere are in the same tile).

- Shinken - Extends the duration of your Holy Sword effect by 1 round, if active.
- Retreating Swipe - If the Solsphere is in the target area, it will be pulled back along with you.
- Thousand Stab - Also applies the skill's effects in a 4 Size Cone from the Solsphere, dealing Light damage instead of its normal damage type.
- Turnover - Moves the Solsphere in the same way it would an enemy. If Excel Crash was LV3 or higher, it can also enhance the Solsphere (see: Radiant Splendor).
- Roundtrip - If it encounters the Solsphere in its path, it will be pulled along with the weapon, allowing it to contact enemies for extra damage.
- Mad Chop - Creates a strong impact that releases spears of red light in a Star pattern from the Solsphere; the size of this pattern is larger the more HP you are missing. Enemies that they touch (except the original target) cause them to take 75% of the skill's base damage.
- Incise - The stab creates a beam of light that fires behind you, applying the skill's effects to all enemies in a 3 Size line from it, dealing Light damage instead of its normal damage type. Also extends the duration of your Holy Wings effect by 1 round, if active.
- Execute - Divine bolts of judgmental lightning will strike all other enemies in 4 Range, dealing Lightning magic damage equal to the skill's base damage to them. If you target an enemy, and the Solsphere is within 2 Range, it is teleported to that enemy's location first. If you defeat an enemy with it, the lightning's damage and range increase by 50%.
- Bash - Moves the Solsphere. Also extends the duration of your Holy Shield effect by 1 round, if active.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Solsphere)

### Star Parade

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location

Requires 3 Solcharges. Targets the tile in front of you, which must be unoccupied. Creates a Fading Solsphere at that location, which moves forward up to 3 tiles, activating if it encounters an enemy.

Fading Solspheres are the same as regular Solspheres, but last only the round they are created. You can have 1 Solsphere and 1 Fading Solsphere active at the same time.

### Divine Radiance

- **Type:** Passive
- **Light Damage:** 5/10/15

The holy light cleanses all it touches.

While you have Holy Aura active, enemies you pass over take protection-ignoring Light magic bonus damage, based on Rank. (Only applies once per action, per enemy.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Divine_Radiance)

### Divine Spark

- **Type:** Passive
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a SP slot.

While equipped, this skill changes all Solblader effects and skills that deal Light damage and scale with Light ATK to deal Lightning damage and scale with Lightning ATK respectively, and changes the appearance of the Solsphere.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Divine_Spark)

### Exceeding Sol

- **Type:** Passive
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a SP slot.

Toggle skill. Utilizes your Solcharges in destructive ways. While on, allows them to be consumed when you use a Soldier base class skill as if they were Weapon Charges for Excel Crash effects.

Weapon Charges will be prioritized and cannot be used with Solcharges. Skills enhanced by Solcharges gain Great Accuracy instead of being evasion ignoring.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Exceeding_Sol)

### Flash of Light

- **Type:** Passive

Solblader Style skill (only 1 can be equipped, doesn't take up a skill pool slot). A spell and blade type of Solblading.

Passive: Soldier base class Offensive skills can use any weapon type. Soldier base class Offensive skills and weapon attack skills that target your Solsphere grant a stacking buff that reduces the Momentum cost of your next Mercalan domain spell by LV (max: 6). Holy Aura Bonus: +5 to all Elemental ATK and -15% FP cost for spells. Holy Aura Triggered: Recover 15 FP and deal protection-ignoring Light bonus magic damage to all enemies within 3 Range. The damage is equal to your Solblader level.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Flash_of_Light)

### Speed of Light

- **Type:** Passive

Solblader Style skill (only 1 can be equipped, doesn't take up a skill pool slot). A quick and tricky type of Solblading.

Passive: Allows Solblader and Soldier Offensive skills (except spells) to utilize Combination Fighter, successively reducing Momentum costs but also damage for those skills. Holy Aura Bonus: Also increases your Evade by 15. Holy Aura Triggered: Immediately gain 1 stack of Combination Fighter (max 2).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Speed_of_Light)

### Wall of Light

- **Type:** Passive

Solblader Style skill (only 1 can be equipped, doesn't take up a skill pool slot). A slow and sturdy type of Solblading.

Passive: Reduces the Range of Solblader Offensive skills that move you by 1 or 2 Range, but increases the damage they deal by 15%. Holy Aura Bonus: Also increases Armor and Magic Armor by 5. Your Protect skill gains +3 activation Range. Reduces redirected damage you take by 10%. Holy Aura Triggered: Gain Swift Guard LV 20 until your next turn.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Wall_of_Light)

Passively increases FAI and GUI, based on Rank. If you are monoclassing Solblader, you start battles with Solcharges equal to Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Illuminating_Sol)

### Unerring Light

- **Type:** Innate

The holy light pierces all it reaches.

While you have Holy Aura active, your Light damage pierces Absorb, Reflect, and Immunity affinities.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Unerring_Light)

### Unerring Light

- **Type:** Innate

Solmancy and Solblading go hand in hand. A rather natural talent, only used by certain spellblading types.

Once per round, casting a Mercalan domain spell from a non-Solblader class, using Pray, or scoring a critical hit, automatically grants 1 Solcharge.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Faithful_Sol)
