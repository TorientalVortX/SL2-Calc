### Dhessence

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10/11/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 80%/90%/100% Dark ATK, 100% Scaled WPN atk

### Solar Lance

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 26/24/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 120/125/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Spear only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Spear skill. Unleashes a piercing spear of solid light at a target enemy within 5 Range. On impact, it deals Pierce physical damage to the target, and the spear will remain in the target for up to 3 rounds. If the target takes Fire or Light damage, the spear will glow red and explode when the status expires, dealing Fire magic damage (100% Fire ATK).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Solar_Lance)

### Brighten

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 12/11/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location
- **RES Bonus:** +4/7/10
- **Other Effects:** LV 5/10/15
- **Domain:** Mercalan

A spell that causes a light of protection or debilitation to be created. Can target an ally, an enemy, or a battlefield tile. All effects last for 3 rounds.

Ally: Grants Brighten status. (An ally's light is protecting you. Increases RES by 1 + LV*3, Light resistance by LV*5%, and deals unresistable Light damage to attackers equal to LV*5.)

Enemy: Inflicts Glowing on the enemy. (Evade is reduced by LV.)

Location: Creates a light shaft for 3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Brighten)

### Calm

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 25/23/21/19/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only, Main class skill

True Curates know that violence isn't always the answer. This skill targets 1 enemy, but has differing effects.

Player Character: Luck-based chance to reduce the duration of anger-based effects, such as Rage Energy, Soul Rage, etc.

NPC/Monster: Luck-based chance to inflict Pacify for 3 rounds.

### Convert

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 25
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only, Main class skill

True Curates know that the path they follow is the right one, and are capable of convincing others of that. Targets 1 Pacified monster within 4 Range and attempts to convert them to your side. (Bosses, minibosses, and undead-race monsters are immune.) If successful, the monster joins your party for the rest of the battle. (It will still give EXP as if it were defeated.)

Your success rate is equal to the following:

Total FAI + (Devotion talent Rank * 2) + (your level - target's level, or 0 if less than 1) = success percent.

You can only have one converted enemy at a time.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Convert)

### Pray

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Max FP Restore:** 6/12/18/24/30

Cannot be used while silenced. Drop to your knees and offer prayers for help to the divine while forsaking all other actions that round. First, this prayer restores FP Equal to your FAI + Rank (max: Rank*6). Second, the duration of all positive status effects you have caused for all combatants are increased by 2 rounds. Third, it increases your LUC by half of your FAI for 4 rounds. This skill can then no longer be used while the LUC bonus is active. Finally, there is a chance based on your FAI that a bonus effect might occur.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Pray)

### Antithesis

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Damage Bonus:** +5/10/15%

The Curate represents life in all of most of its forms. As such, they serve as the polar opposite to unlife; things that cling to the living realm unnaturally. Specifically, undead, and as such, all damage dealt to Undead and Ghost monsters is increased by a percentage, based on Rank. It is also effective against members of the Vampire and Possessed race, but only at half effectiveness, as they tip-toe the line between alive and undead.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Antithesis)

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

It's a miracle you weren't hurt. When you evade a basic attack, you gain Grace of God for 3 rounds. When you Pray, Grace of God is consumed to guarantee it will activate a bonus effect.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Grace_of_God)
