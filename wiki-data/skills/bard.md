## Skills

### Fire Dance

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 12/14/16/18/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1-3
- **Power:** 40/45/50/55/60% Fire ATK, 5/10/15/20/25 Base DMG
- **Target:** Line (1-3)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dance skill. You target an unoccupied tile within 4 Range and move in a line to it. Every time you move through a tile, you deal Fire magic damage (based on your Fire ATK and Rank) to a random enemy within 2 Range. Enemies hit multiple times take 50% damage from additional hits.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire_Dance)

### Ice Slide

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 12/14/16/18/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4 (2)
- **Power:** 50%/55%/60%/65%/70% Ice ATK +5/10/15/20/25
- **Target:** Line to (4)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dance skill. Dash along the ground like a figure skater with chilling grace. You target an unoccupied tile within 4 Range and move in a line to it. Every time you move across a tile, you deal Ice magic damage (based on your Ice ATK and Rank) to a random enemy within 2 range. Enemies hit multiple times take 50% damage from additional hits.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ice_Slide)

### Quaking Tap

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 7
- **Power:** 100/120/140/160//180% Earth ATK, +5/10/15/20/25
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dance skill. Elegantly tap your feet on the ground below you, perhaps more strongly than expected. You will tap your feet 3 times, and every time, you deal Earth magic damage (based on your Earth ATK and Rank) to a random enemy in 7 Range (however, each enemy can only be damaged once).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Quaking_Tap)

### Sound Wave

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 8/10/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 90%/100%/110% Sound ATK/Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (4)
- **Cooldown:** 1
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Must be used by an Instrument weapon. Unleashes a wave of sound in a 4 Range line as a projectile, which will pierce through enemies and deal Sound magic damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sound_Wave)

### Thunder Steps

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 12/14/16/18/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4 (2)
- **Power:** 110%/120%/130%/140%/150% Lightning ATK
- **Target:** Line (4 - 2)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dance skill. Leap into the air and crash down with the awe-inspiring effect of thunder. Targets 2 unoccupied tiles; you will leap to the first one, and then the second one. Each time you land at one of these locations, you deal Lightning magic damage (based on your Lightning ATK and Rank) to a random enemy within 2 Range.

If there is not an enemy within 3 range of you after using this skill, it is put on a 2 round cooldown.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Thunder_Steps)

### Wind Pirouette

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 8/9/10/11/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Diamond (4)
- **Power:** 50%/55%/60%/65%/70% WIND ATK, 5/10/15/20/25
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dance skill. A rapid spinning dance move while staying in place. You will spin in place 3 times, and every time, you deal Wind magic damage (based on your Wind ATK and Rank) to a random enemy within 3 Range.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Wind_Pirouette)

### Anthem of Enthusiasm

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 12/14/16/18/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** +2/4/6/8/10 Elemental ATK, Critical Damage%
- **Target:** Diamond (6)
- **Flags:** Ally Only

Bard Song skill. A cheery, forceful tune to get allies excited. Play your instrument, gaining the Anthem of Enthusiasm aura (+2 WIL, +2 GUI, 5 Range), and giving all allies in 5 Range bonuses to Elemental ATK and Critical Damage for 3 rounds. (based on Rank).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Anthem_of_Enthusiasm)

### Volume Up

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 9/10/11
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** 5/10/15 +Sound ATK, 1/2/3 +Bard Range
- **Target:** Self
- **Flags:** Self

Crank up the volume. For 3 rounds, boosts your Sound ATK, and the range of your Bard class Song skills (based on Rank).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Volume_Up)

### Auto-Tune

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Rank 1:** Auto-starts the battle with first equipped bard support skill.

You know just when to kick in the music. At the start of battle, if you have a Bard base class Song skill equipped, you will automatically use the first skill of that type equipped in your Skill Pool. (You must still pay the FP cost and satisfy the conditions of that skill.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Auto-Tune)

### Light-Handed Playing

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

It's so much easier to perform when your hands are as empty as your mind. When you only have 1 weapon equipped, and that weapon is an Instrument, FP costs for Bard base class skills are reduced by 10%

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Light-Handed_Playing)

### Musical Prodigy

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Momentum:** 0
- **Power:** 1/2/3/4/6 +SAN
- **Target:** Self
- **Flags:** Self

Some Bards just have the talent. Passively increases SAN, based on Rank. At Rank 5, SAN is further increased by +1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Musical_Prodigy)

### Bard's Armory

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

Besides a good voice and quick feet, what does a Bard need? An instrument.

Allows you to equip any Instrument weapon, regardless of weapon type.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bard's_Armory)

### Charmer's Wit

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Rank 1:** 10% additional status infliction if Charming.

With a reputation for being more charismatic than reliable, the Bard is a natural charmer, increasing their Status Infliction when inflicting Charm status by 10%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Charmer's_Wit)

### Never Quiet

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Rank 1:** 15% additional resist vs Silence

With a reputation for never shutting up, perhaps to the benefit or anger of others, the Bard is a natural talker, increasing their Status Resistance versus the Silence status by 15%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Never_Quiet)

### Repeat Sign

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Rank 1:** Gains 2 FP for every enemy defeated by a party-member you buffed.

There's nothing quite like the satisfaction of good music while you fight. When an ally under the effect of one of your Bard song status effects defeats an enemy, you regain 2 FP for each of your song effects active on them.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Repeat_Sign)

### Trained Ears

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Rank 1:** Gains 10% Sound Resist.

A Bard is used to loud noises - music, singing, the crowd cheering (or booing...). As such, sound has a lesser effect on them, increasing their Sound damage resistance by 10%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Trained_Ear)
