## Skills

### Hirazuki

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 18/20/22
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 3-5
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (3-5)
- **Restriction:** Katana, Swords only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Kensei Combo Skill. Stab your target and run with them in a line, carrying them with you. Run forward, dealing pierce Physical damage to the first enemy you hit, pushing them along until you reach the end of the line. If the sword you are using is a Katana-type, the Momentum cost is reduced by 1. The size of the line can be changed using the +/- keys.

Sacred Art Effect: Fire physical damage.

Combo Effect: Skewer the target several times, inflicting Fray LV X (X = Rank * 3) on them until your next turn, before damage is dealt.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hirazuki)

### Kagekiri

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 18/20/22
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 3-5
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (3-5)
- **Restriction:** Katana, Swords only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Kensei Combo Skill. Dash forward in a 3-5 Size line, and slash all around you faster than the eye can see, dealing Slash damage. If the sword you are using is a Katana type, the Momentum cost is reduced by 1.

If Voltiger is available, it can be consumed to change the pattern into flexible line.

Sacred Art Effect: Darkness physical damage.

Combo Effect: Ignores evasion. After the dash, you turn around towards the way you came.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Kagekiri)

### Raijinken

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 18/20/22
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Katana, Swords only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Kensei Combo Skill. Slash upwards at your target, and follow them into the air. Deals Slash damage to an within 1 range, making you both airborne. If the sword you are using is a Katana-type, the momentum cost is reduced by 1.

If the Voltiger is available, it can be consumed to target an enemy within 5 range, teleporting you to an unoccupied tile next to them before other effects.

Sacred Art Effect: Lightning physical damage.

Combo Effect: All enemies within 3 Range of the target are also hit by the skill.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Raijinken)

### Sharenzan

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 18/20/22
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Katana, Swords only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Kensei Combo Skill. Strike your target with forceful sword blow. Deals Slash damage to 1 enemy within 1 range, and knock them back 3 tiles (this knockback ignores immunity effects.) If the sword you are using is a Katana-type, the Momentum cost is reduced by 1. If you are airborne, the damage is increased by 1.25x. If the target is airborne, they are also knocked down. (Airborne Compatible)

Sacred Art Effect: Earth physical damage.

Combo Effect: The target is swung around, hitting all enemies within 1 Range for half damage and knockback, then the normal skill effect takes place.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sharenzan)

### Toiken

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 18/20/22
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 3-5
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Crescent (3-5)
- **Restriction:** Katana, Swords only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Kensei Combo skill. Draw your sword with such precision and speed that it kicks up razor-sharp wind that deals Slash damage to all enemies in a crescent pattern. If the sword you are using is a Katana-type, the Momentum cost is reduced by 1. The size of the pattern can be changed using the +/- keys.

Sacred Art Effect: Wind physical damage.

Combo Effect: Affected tiles that have field objects are blown away 3 tiles away from you.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Toiken)

### Wazabane

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 18/20/22
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Katana, Swords only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Bonus Hit/Critical:** +4/+7/+10

Kensei Combo skill. An overhead slash at a target within 1 range, performing a basic attack on them with bonuses to Hit and Critical. If you are airborne, and your target is not, the Hit and critical bonuses are doubled. Afterwards, your airborne status ends. If the sword you are using is a katana-type, the momentum cost is reduced by 1.

Sacred Art Effect: Water physical damage.

Combo Effect: Damage cannot be parried. Gains Guard Break properties. (When used, will cause Guard Break if the enemy is Guarding.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Wazabane)

### Sheath Sword

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 5
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Katana, Swords only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Bonus Hit:** +10/20/30
- **Bonus Damage:** +2/4/6

Sword skill. Targets 1 tile in 5 Range. Put your sword in your sheath after facing target location; if it is a Katana, you also gain Quick Draw (LV X, until your next turn). If an enemy is in the target tile, you inflict them with Watchful Eye (1 round).

Quick Draw will be expended at the start of your next turn or after successfully triggering. If it doesn't trigger, you will gain Sacred Art at the start of the next round, if it is equipped.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sheath_Sword)

### Absolute Death

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 5
- **Momentum:** 0
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Self
- **Cooldown:** 1
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **FP cost per round:** 10

Kensei Stance Skill. Grants you Absolute Death Lv 10 (Permanent duration, but requires 10 FP every round.) When active, enemies damaged by Kensei Offensive skills that are already affected by your Sakki will cause you to swiftly move to slash all other enemies within 5 range of that target, dealing Protection-ignoring Bonus Slash damage equal to your kensei class (or 10, whichever is higher). Enemies can only be damaged this way once per skill use.

Small Note: There is an in-game typo in the word 'targeting'; it is instead written as 'targetting'.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Absolute_Death)

### Blade Barrier

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Success Rate:** Scaled CEL + 30/40/50%

Deflection Skill. You become capable of spinning your katana so fast that it deflects bullets. When attacked by a Gun weapon by an enemy you are facing, there is a percentage chance based on Rank to negate the attack and reduce your Katana's durability by 1. This skill will fail if your katana's durability is 0, and can only successfully activate once per round. (Only one Deflection Skill can apply at a time.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Blade_Barrier)

### Counter Edge

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Bonus Damage:** +15/20/25

Attacks made through Riposte and Sheath Sword receive bonus damage based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Counter_Edge)

### Hidden Cut

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Required:** Sheath Sword rank 1
- **Damage:** 10/20/30 + 25/50/75% Scaled Weapon ATK

Sometimes your enemies don't realize they've been cut until after you put your sword away. When you use Sheath Sword, all enemies you have attacked and hit with a Katana-type Sword, or used a Kensei Sword skill on this turn take Slash damage that ignores armor, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hidden_Cut)

### Reflect Shots

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Range:** 3/4/5

You skillfully manipulate any deflected Gun attacks into bouncing back towards your enemies. When you successfully block a Gun attack with Blade Barrier, rather than cancelling the attack, it is reflected to a random enemy within X Range, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Reflect_Shots)

### Sacred Art

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Kensei often utilize multiple techniques in succession, turning their sword play into an art rather than a skill. When you deal damage to an enemy using a Kensei Offensive Skill, the next Kensei Offensive Skill this turn does not require any FP to use, and if it hits an enemy, it will deal elemental damage based off of your elemental ATK (each offensive Kensei skill uses a unique element; if you have a Starsign, its element will be used instead).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sacred_Art)

### Tekagen

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Toggle skill. A term used in Oniga for someone who is holding back. While toggled on, damage you deal will not reduce an enemy below 1 HP.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Tekagen)

### Zen Blade

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Does not take up a SP slot.

Calm your center, and restore strength. When using a Sheath sword with more than 3 momentum, you recover 15 FP per momentum point exceeding 3 (IE, 4 Momentum would restore 15, 7 Momentum would restore 60.) After activating, this effect have a 3 round cooldown, unless Kensei is BOTH your main class, and sub-class.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Zen_Blade)

### Channel Spirit

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP Regeneration:** 4/5/6

Kensei perform better when not burdened by things like armor. When you are using an Unarmored torso, you regain X FP every round, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Channel_Spirit)

### Katana Master

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Boost:** +5 Power/LV

Needless to say, Kensei are masters of the katana. Increases your Power with Katana-type weapons.

At the start of battle, If you only have a Katana Weapon(s) equipped, You gain a permanent status that boosts the power of your Katana Weapons, Touki, Sakki, and Kenki. This boost is lost if you swap weapons.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Katana_Master)

### Kenki

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Hit Penalty:** -5

Kenki is an aggressive type of spiritual energy that most Kensei possess. It is used offensively, projected towards enemies, to try and intimidate them, or trick them by confusing it with Touki or Sakki. When applied by Sharenzan or Wazabane, it reduces the target's Hit versus you for 3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Kenki)

### Peerless

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **STR/SKI Bonus:** +1/2/3

The Kensei are both respected and feared for their immense prowess with a blade, but also their incredible speed and skill with it. Passively increases STR and SKI.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Peerless)

### Sakki

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Critical Evade:** -5

Sakki is a type of spiritual energy that gives off the intent to kill. Kensei possess the ability to suppress this energy, hiding their killing intent from enemies. When applied by Kagekiri or Hirazuki, it reduces the target's Critical Evade versus you for 3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sakki)

### Touki

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Evade Penalty:** -5

Touki is a type of spiritual energy that shows the will to fight. Hiding it from your enemies will make them feel less threatened just before your attacks, causing their evasion to be sloppy or mistimed. When applied by Toiken or Raijinken, It reduces the target's Evade versus you for 3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Touki)

### Yomidori

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Parry Acivation Rate:** +4/7/10%

Yomidori is the act of predicting your opponent's movements with such precision that it becomes akin to mind-reading. When you are attacked by a person who has already attacked or damaged you this turn, your parry skills have a higher chance to activate, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Yomidori)
