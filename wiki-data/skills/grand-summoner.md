### Judgement Blade

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 30/28/26/24/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** 100/105/110/115/120% Light ATK, 100% Scaled Weapon ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** General
- **Install Bonus Damage:** If the target is a Youkai, or installed with one, the damage is increased by Rank*5%.

Cut your target with a dimensional blade. Deals Slash magical damage to target enemy within 3 Range. If the target is a Youkai, or installed with one, the damage is increased by Rank*5%. Additionally, if the target is a summoned unit, the damage is increased by Rank*5% (stacks additively if both conditions met).

### Soul Chains

- **Type:** Offensive
- **FP cost:** 30/28/26/24/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **FP Recovery:** Target cannot recover FP for the 2/2/3/3/4 Rounds
- **Install:** Target is treated as if they were Installed with a Youkai. For the same duration, you cannot summon the Youkai

Can only be used if you have a Youkai installed. The Youkai's astral form jumps to target enemy within 3 Range for X rounds (based on Rank), ending the Install. During this time, that enemy cannot recover FP, and is treated as if they were Installed with a Youkai. For the same duration, you cannot summon the Youkai.

### Chain Unsummon

- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/15/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally Only

### Astral Aegis

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Effect::** +5 DEF/RES

A strengthening of the spirit and body that is caused by summoned Youkai. If you have a Youkai summoned, you gain a bonus to DEF and RES. (This bonus is treated as a class bonus.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Astral_Aegis)

### Auto Summon

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

One of the greatest flaws of being a summoner is that it is very taxing to have a Youkai summoned at all times. But if you are caught off guard, summoning a Youkai to protect you can take time you don't have. Sometime it's good to have a contingency.

While this skill is equipped, when a battle starts, you automatically summon the first available Youkai from your contracts (it must have at least 1 HP however).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Auto_Summon)

### Dimension Resonance

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Required:** Impact Dismiss Rank 1

Attack enemies with powerful dimension shifts. Every time a Youkai you control is unsummoned, after the effect of Impact Dismiss applies, you gain Dimension Resonance LV1 (stacks up to LV2+Rank). The damage of Impact Dismiss is multiplied by Dimension Resonance's LV. However, the damaged caused by it will become resistable.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dimension_Resonance)

### Evoke Mastery

A mastery of utilizing evoking skills. While not on the level of the Miashima, it does reduce the FP cost associated with the art.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Evoke_Mastery)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Impact_Call)

### Impact Dismiss

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Damage Dealt:** When you unsummon a Youkai manually, it creates an elemental explosion that damages enemies within 1/2/3 Range for damage equal to the Youkai's level. The damage type varies based on the Youkai

When you unsummon a Youkai manually, it creates an elemental explosion that damages enemies within X Range (based on Rank) for damage equal to the Youkai's level. The damage type varies based on the Youkai, and has Great Accuracy.

### Misty Dimension

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

The mystic qualities of summoning sometimes make foes less aware. Increases the FP cost of using the Summon skill by 5, but causes it to give you Sneak status for 2 rounds, which lowers your chance to be targeted by enemies.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Misty_Dimension)

### Ripple Dimension

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Required:** Impact Call Rank 1

Abuse the ripples of dimensions to speed up your summonings. Hitting at least one enemy with Impact Call's effect will grant Ripple Dimension LV1 (stacks up to LV3) for 1 round, which lowers the momentum cost of Summon by LV.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ripple_Dimension)

### Spirit Regeneration

- **Type:** Passive
- **HP/FP Regeneration:** Every round, Youkai who are unsummoned and not at 0 HP regain 2/4/6/8/10 HP and FP.

As beings that live in the ether, youkai are more quickly able to recover when in it. Every round, Youkai who are unsummoned and not at 0 HP regain HP and FP based on the Rank of this skill.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Spirit_Regeneration)

### Spirit Well

- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP Boost:** +3/6/9/12/15

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Melee Weapon Bonus Damage:** Melee weapons gain +5/10/15/20/25 Power if the target is a Youkai (or treated as one).

As a being who must always interact with Youkai, the Grand Summoner is well versed in the ways to discipline them. Melee weapons gain a bonus to Power based on Rank if the target is a Youkai (or treated as one).
