### Blowback Cannon

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 25/24/23
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** Line (5) Wide
- **Power:** 130%/140%/150% Scaled WPN Atk
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Bow/Rifle only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Knockback:** 4/5/6

Arbalest Cannon skill.

Launches your Special Armament in a powerful burst. Targets a 5 size wide line in cardinal Direction from you, scatters certain field objects in those tiles, and all enemies in those tiles take Pierce damage (can critically hit), and are knocked back several tiles (based on Rank). ### Special Armament

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Allows the Arbalest to equip any Sword, Axe, or Spear as a sub-weapon. When a weapon of one of those types (or any type if monoclassing Arbalest) is equipped a sub-weapon, it is fired when using Cannon skills. See Arbalest Cannon Skills for more details.

##### Arbalest Cannon Skills

These skills have special requirements in order to be used; you must have a Bow or Rifle weapon in your main hand, and a sub-weapon of certain types (Sword, Axe, Spear) equipped.

After using one of these skills, you enter a Reloading state for 4 rounds. This is essentially a global cooldown for all Arbalest Cannon skills; they cannot be used until the cooldown ends. You may end it prematurely by using Shore Up.

While on cooldown, your sub-weapon will be inaccessible, as if it weren't equipped; you won't be able to use it with skills, and its item effects won't apply.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Special_Armament)
