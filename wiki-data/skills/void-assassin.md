## Skills

### Black Bolt

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 45/44/43/42/41
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** ?
- **Power:** 100/110/120/130/140% Scaled Darkness, 100% Scaled weapon.
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Dark Damage:** 6/10/14/18/22
- **Bonus Effect:** Rank 2: Silences for 2 roundsRank 3: Teleports to random squareRank 4: Inflicts Interference for 3 roundsRank 5: Covers ground near target in Void Pollution, doubling spell costs
- **Domain:** Huggessoan

Target enemy takes Dark magic damage equal to your current Void Energy. This will consume all Void Energy you have accumulated. Gains bonus effects based on Rank if enough Void Energy is consumed (10*Rank Effect). Can also be used with Veil Off active; It'll use its LV instead, and then end the effect.

### Cutthroat

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Dagger only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Hit Penalty:** -20/15/10/5/0

Dagger skill. Can only target an enemy that you are directly behind. Performs a basic attack on the target with a modifier to your Hit, which if successful, may inflict Silence on the target for 2 rounds (3 rounds on Critical Hit). If Veil Off is active, the effect is guaranteed.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cutthroat)

### Detogate

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 24/23/22/21/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 7
- **Power:** 100/115/130/145/160% Dark ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Huggessoan

Targets one of your Voidgates within 7 range, and causes it to explode, dealing Dark magic damage to all enemies within 4 range of it. They will also be teleported to a random battlefield tile.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Detogate)

### Eliminate

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 30
- **Range:** 1/7
- **Power:** 100/115/130/145/160% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3
- **Restriction:** Dagger only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Dagger skill. Uses up 3+M and consumes all Void Energy. Targets 1 enemy within 1 Range (or 7 Range if Airborne; you move next to them) and deals Slash damage, and then Pierce damage, which ignores evasion. Damage is increased by (Void Energy consumed)%, plus 40% if you are Sneaking. If the target is a non-boss, non-player character, it also has a chance equal to the damage bonus to instantly kill the target (if the target's level is equal or less than your level +10).

If a target is defeated by this attack, all enemies will be inflicted with Fear for the rest of the battle.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Eliminate)

### Fray

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/13/16/19/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** ?
- **Power:** 80/88/96/104/112% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Dagger only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Voidgate Damage:** 100/110/120/130/140% Dark ATK

Dagger skill. Targets 1 Enemy or 1 Voidgate in Range.

Enemy: You slash the target wildly, dealing physical Slash damage. Their Phys. DEF is also reduced by 2*Rank for 3 rounds. If Veil Off is active, it also reduces Mag. Def by same amount.

Voidgate: The Voidgate becomes unstable, preventing teleportation, but causing it to release bolts of void energy (on activation and every round) which deal Dark magic damage. If you are in the path of a bolt, you will gain Void Energy equal to half its damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fray)

### Vanishing Strike

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16/14/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** ?
- **Power:** 100/110/120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2 turns
- **Restriction:** Dagger only
- **Flags:** Location

Dagger skill. Consumes 10 Void Energy. Targets 2 locations. In Location 1, there must be an enemy, and Location 2 must be an unoccupied tile. The enemy in L1 takes Pierce damage, based on Rank, and is teleported to your current location. Afterwards, you teleport to L2.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Vanishing_Strike)

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5

- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** ?
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location
- **Rounds:** 3/4/5/6/7

Targets an empty location or Voidgate in Range. Location: Creates a Voidgate at the target location for X rounds (based on Rank). Voidgate: You leap into the Void and instantly reappear at the Voidgate's location. Teleporting refunds 2M and half the cost of the skill.

If, instead. The Voidgate is Frayed, you manipulate it into firing, but also reduce its duration by 1.

### Advancing Flow

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Void Energy per Evade:** +1/2/3

Toggle Skill. Evasive Reaction skill (only one takes effect at a time). While toggled on, when you evade an attack, you briefly fade into the void, causing you to move 1 tile towards the attacker. If you moved, you also generate Void Energy, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Advancing_Flow)

### Blind Spot

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Leave your foes wondering where you went. Teleporting to a Voidgate that is not in the line of sight of an enemy will grant you Sneak for 2 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Blind_Spot)

### Charred Void

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Taint fire with darkness. When you create a Cinder tile, if you have at least 1 Void Energy, it will be consumed and turn it into a Dark Cinder. (Happens on a per-tile basis.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Charred_Void)

### Dangerous Dip

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

By exposing yourself to the void while teleporting, you can gain valuable Void Energy, but it's a risk. When you teleport to a Voidgate, you generate 3 Void Energy, but take 5 unresistable Null damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dangerous_Dip)

### Deadly Arms

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Critical Damage Boost:** +10%

The Void Assassin is particularly adept at using daggers, capable of hurting people with them like almost no one else. Increases Critical Damage of Daggers by 10%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Deadly_Arms)

### Impure Element

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Use the power of the void to boost your magic. Upon taking Fire, Ice, Wind, Lightning, or Earth damage, gain Impure Element of the same element. Impure Element increases your Elemental ATK of that element by 10 (if monoclassing Void Assassin, by your Void Energy instead). However, using any skill that uses that Elemental ATK causes you to take damage equal to half of that amount. (Taking damage will reset the status to a new element.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Impure_Element)

- **Max rank:** 1

### Void Flight

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

### Void Refraction

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

When an allied projectile encounters one of your Voidgates, it disappears, but a copy of that projectile will shoot from every other Voidgate you own in a cardinal direction towards the nearest enemy.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Void_Refraction)

### Void Reverb

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Retain your energy as much as possible, at any cost. If you would consume at least 2 Void Energy, reduce the amount by half, but take Null damage equal to the amount reduced.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Void_Reverb)

### Voidveil

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Magical Damage Reduction:** 5/7.5/10/12.5/15%
- **Max Void Energy:** 20/30/40/50/60

While wearing an armor made of a Cloth material, it is covered in an almost invisible mesh called the Voidveil. When you take magic damage, you reduce the damage by X% of the damage and gain a special Void energy equal to twice that amount (based on Rank; if Void Assassin is not your main class, no damage reduction is applied). Void Energy is used by other Void Assassin skills, and it persists through multiple battles.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Voidveil)
