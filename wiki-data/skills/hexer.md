## Skills

### Black Bubble

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 35/32/29/27/25
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 100% Scaled WPN ATK + 20/25/30/35/40% Dark/Water ATK
- **Target:** Circle (3)
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Aquarian

Targets a 3 Range circle area within 4 Range. All enemies within the area of effect take magic damage split between Water and Dark. All damaged enemies may be Silenced for 2 rounds.

All tiles in the radius will be covered in Dark Water for 3 rounds (which reduce the Faith of those who stand in it to 0.), but it will take until the start of the next round for it to puddle and take effect.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Black_Bubble)

### Death Knighting

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 70/65/60/55/50
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Self
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Huggessoan

Requires a Rank D Invocation. After casting, you will temporarily change into a Spectre Knight for 5 rounds. While you are a Spectre Knight, you gain access to the skills Calling, Hellspike, and Fallcall. You are also treated as the Possessed racial type for the same duration.

### Earthbound Fog

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 70/65/60/55/50
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Huggessoan
- **Duration:** 5/5/6/6/7

Mist Skill. This sill requires a Rank D Invocation. When cast, the battlefield is covered in a purple mist that seems to induce uneasiness. The purple mist lasts for 5 rounds, and while active, enemies cannot use Attack (or Sub-Attack) or any Offensive-class skill during the same turn they use Movement. Boss enemies do not have actions prevented, but instead have -15% Dark Resistance. (Only one Mist skill can be activate on the battlefield at a time.)

#### Invocation

"Stand your ground and fight into the grave..."

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Earthbound_Fog)
