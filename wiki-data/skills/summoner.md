## Skills

### Energy Transfer

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally Only
- **FP Bonus:** +0/4/8/12/16

Supplement a summoned youkai's focus with even more of your own. By targeting a Youkai within 5 Range with this skill, you restore the FP of it by the FP cost of this skill plus a bonus (based on Rank). In addition, if you have the appropriate Affinity skill, the FP restore will be increased by the growth bonus it gives that Youkai.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Energy_Transfer)

### Cracked Mirror

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Required:** The Contract rank 1
- **FP Regen:** +1/2/3/4/5

Weakens the wall between your youkai and yourself, letting energies from the astral plane flow into you. Recovers FP based on Rank every round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cracked_Mirror)

### Install

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Required:** Sync-Mind rank 1
- **Bonus Duration:** +0/1/2

Grants access to Install summons. The cost of an install is equal to twice the cost of summoning the youkai and lasts 2 rounds ( +1 for Mechanations). Installed Youkai cannot be summoned, but the following happens: * Your race's base stats are replaced with the Youkai's racial base stats, excluding FAI, SAN, and APT. * You gain access to all of that Youkai's skills.. * Affinity statistical boosts are effective for you (max +1). Some Youkai may have special skills that apply if they're installed.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Install)

### Sync-Mind

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Required:** The Contract rank 1
- **Rank 1:** Allows you to use the Youkai's Evoke-Active skill.
- **Rank 2:** Allows you to use the Youkai's Evoke-Passive skill.

An advanced summoning method called 'Evoking', whereby a Youkai is only partially summoned, and its powers are channeled through the Summoner. This allows the Summoner to utilize some of the Youkai's power themself. (You do not gain any benefits while the Youkai's HP is 0.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sync-Mind)

### The Contract

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Rank 1:** Allows you to enter summoning houses and converse with youkai.

An agreement with an unknown entity, stating that you choose this fate of your own free will. It grants you the title and abilities of a 'Summoner'.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/The_Contract)
