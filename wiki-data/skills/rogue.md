## Skills

### Fire Trap

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 8/10/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Fire ATK + 150% GUI
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location
- **Trap Size:** 2/2/3
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Places a fire trap at an unoccupied square within 2 range for 10 rounds. If an enemy walks within 2 cardinal squares of the trap, it triggers, dealing Fire physical damage to them. They may also be inflicted with Burn LV X (X= Rank * 10) for 3 rounds.

Traps become stronger every round they are active.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fire_Trap)

### Gust Trap

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 8/10/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Wind ATK + 100% GUI
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location
- **Trap Size:** 2/2/3
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Places a gust trap at an unoccupied square within 2 range for 10 turns. If an enemy walks within 2 cardinal squares of the trap, it triggers, deals Wind physical damage, causes the triggerer to stop their movement, and knocks them away from the trap 1 square. They may also be inflicted with a Poison LV X (X = Rank * 5) for 3 rounds.

Traps become stronger every round they are active.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Gust_Trap)

### Ice Trap

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 8/10/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Ice ATK + 150% GUI
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location
- **Trap Size:** 2/2/3
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Places an ice trap at an unoccupied square within 2 range for 10 rounds. If an enemy walks within 2 cardinal squares of the trap, it triggers, which will cause all tiles in its Range to be covered in Ice Sheets for 3 rounds, as well as dealing Ice Physical damage to the one who triggered it. They may also be inflicted with Frostbite LV X (X = Rank * 10) for 3 rounds.

Traps become stronger every round they are active.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ice_Trap)

Traps can be effective in certain situations. Even if your enemy knows there's a trap, that doesn't mean they can ignore [it]. Hidden traps are even better. When you place a trap, after a short arming animation, the traps disappears from the view of everyone.

### Thorn Scratch

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Max DMG:** 4/8/12

Leave a little mark when you pass, just like bumping against a thorny vine. While you have at least one Dagger weapon equipped, when you move over a tile, any enemy located in that tile will be slashed by you, dealing up the X damage (based on Rank) equal to your Dagger's Power, of your dagger's damage type. This damage ignores protection.

If both of your classes are Rogue classes, the damage cap is doubled.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Thorn_Scratch)

### Throwing Daggers

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Starting LV:** 1/2/3

Toss your daggers as if they were projectiles. You can utilize 1 LV of Throwing Daggers to increase the basic attack range of your Dagger weapons by 3. You start battle with LV X (based on Rank), and gain 1 at the start of a new round, up to a maximum of 3. (If both classes are Rogue classes, you gain 2 at the start of a new round instead.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Throwing_Daggers)

### Twin Dance

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **2nd Attack Damage:** 10/15/20%

On the battlefield, true Rogues tend to prefer to strike quick and often. When you Attack or Sub-Attack while you have two non-Bow weapons equipped of the same weapon type, you will attack with the other, but this attack deals significantly reduced damage if it hits. (Attack target must be in 1 Range unless using Shuriken weapons. Mutated weapons may not qualify.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Twin_Dance)

### Flank

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Additional Damage:** +10

Attacking a target from a vulnerable angle is quite beneficial, letting you hit them harder in softer spots. When attacking an enemy from the side or behind, you gain damage based on Rank. (Guns do not receive a damage bonus from Flank.) If the attack is from a Dagger weapon, you also ignore up to 6 Armor from the target.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Flank)

### Quick Rise

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

A true Rogue always takes any small opportunity and turns it into an advantage for them, if possible. When you are Knocked Down, if you successfully Evade an attack, you are cured of Knock Down for free by quickly jumping to your feet.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Quick_Rise)

Being flexible is usually a good thing. It's beneficial to anyone, most of all adventurers, to always have new tricks up their sleeve, and a resourceful rogue is just that. Flexible. This inate skill will increase your maximum possible equipped skills.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Resourceful)
