## Skills

### Agile Androcobra

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 30
- **Power:** 110/120/130% Acid ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy
- **Domain:** Isespian

Arcane skill. Fires a slithering arrow that deals Acid magic damage to the first enemy hit and applies Acidity LV X (X = 2+Rank). If it passes over an allied Rain Trap, it will apply its damage & effects in its range, create LV 20 Poison Pools for 3 rounds, and destroy the trap. (Enemies can only be damaged once per skill use.)

Annorum: The arrow will continue even after hitting an enemy and the Acidity LV is doubled.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Agile_Androcobra)

### Cherry Blossom

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 7
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Earth ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy
- **Domain:** Isespian

Arcane skill. A cherry blossom blooms at a tile in 7 range, which boosts the stats of those near it (only one can be active). Enemies beneath it will take Earth magic damage, be knocked airborne, and one tile away. An enemy damaged by Cherry Blossom while within 3 range of it are covered in Cherry Petals for 3 rounds.

Annorum: Targets a Cherry Blossom. Destroys it, but deals this skill's damage to all enemies, and restores all allies' HP by 50+(50*Rank.)

Cherry Petals

Additionally treated as plant race, due to being covered in petals. Because that's all it takes.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cherry_Blossom)

### Deos Danzai

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** ?
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Light ATK, 50% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Diagonal (Double) (1)
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy
- **Domain:** Mercalan

Creates four arrows of light in 2-long lines of tiles on either side of you. After a turn of priming, they will launch at the nearest enemy every round there after, one at a time, dealing Light magic damage. They can be attacked and destroyed, however; if so, the attacker recovers 20 FP.

Annorum: Danzai arrows gain an additional hit of durability and have no priming time.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Deos_Danzai)

### Forest Fiend

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16

### Frigid Arrow

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 110/120/130% Ice ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy
- **Domain:** Aquarian

Arcane skill. Deals Ice magic damage to an enemy within 5 Range. It can also target a Rain Trap instead; doing so will apply this skill's damage & effects in its range, create ice sheets for 3 rounds, and destroy the trap.

Annorum: Inflicts Frozen LV X (X = Rank *75) for 3 rounds, and Heavy Frost for 2 rounds.

Heavy Frost

### Ghost Arrow

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Yes
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Dark ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy
- **Domain:** Huggessoan

Arcane skill. Fires a ghastly arrow which will pass through enemies for 3 tiles, before becoming tangible and traveling until it hits an enemy. An enemy hit by it takes Dark magic damage. If it passes over an allied Rain Trap, it will apply its damage & effects in its range, create delayed Dark Water tiles for 3 rounds, and destroy the trap. (Enemies can only be damaged once per skill use.)

Annorum: If the enemy hit is reduced to 0 HP, summons a Ghost ally.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ghost_Arrow)

- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 110/120/130% Sound ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK

- **Restriction:** Bow only

Arcane skill. A fast, magical arrow that screeches painfully past enemies. Deals Sound magic damage to 1 enemy within 5 Range. Allies affected gain Sound ATK Up LV (Rank * 5, x1.5 if Annorum enhanced) for 3 rounds.

### Annorum

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 25
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3 turns
- **Flags:** Self Only

A magic sigil that is inscribed on an arrow, filling it with enhanced power. Rangers have an Annorum gauge in battle, which is filled with charges when casting a Spell (3 for Ranger spells, 2 for other spells) up to a maximum of 9. When 9 are available, the next Arcane-class Ranger spell will be enhanced with a bonus effect (see specific skills for those). After activating a bonus effect, charges cannot be regained automatically during the same round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Annorum)

### Rain Trap

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 22/18/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location
- **Diamond Size:** 2/3/4 Range
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Places a Rain Trap at a location within 2 range for 10 rounds. ### Expert's Aim

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Required:** Longdraw Rank 1

A steady hand makes for a steady shot. If you have Longdraw equipped, it activates automatically (for free) at the start of a round. However, using any movement skill will cause it to end (while this skill is equipped).

Most Ranger Arcane spells benefit from Longdraw's Range increase.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Expert's_Aim)

### Spellshot Sigil

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up any Skill Pool slots.

Turning the shot into spell energy, the Ranger is capable of using their well placed shots to amplify the Arcane. When you score a critical hit with Bow weapons, you generate 1 Annorum charge.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Spellshot_Sigil)

Rangers are skilled at moving swiftly and unpredictably, even while attacking. Grants a bonus to Evade until your next turn after using a Ranger Offensive skill that causes you to move.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Acrobatic_Shooting)
