## Skills

### Detonation Rift

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 25/22/19
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** 100% Scaled WPN ATK, 50/60/70% Sound/Fire ATK
- **Target:** Circle (3)
- **Cooldown:** 1 Turn
- **Flags:** Enemy or Ally

Dark Bard Song skill. Play your instrument, creating an explosion at a 3 size Circle within 3 Range, dealing Fire and Sound damage to all units inside of it. Enemies may be inflicted with Burn LV X (X = Rank * 10) for 3 Rounds.

Enemies take full damage, while allies take Kickback Damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Detonation_Rift)

### Fatal Finale

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 40/35/30
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/125/150% Darkness ATK, 100/115/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3 Turns
- **Flags:** Location

Dark Bard Song skill. An end to the show that they'll never forget. All of your Shadow Moshes explode with dark energy, dealing Dark magic damage to all units within 1 Range of them. (Allies take Kickback Damage. Enemies can only be hit once per use.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fatal_Finale)

### Hard Note

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 50
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Sound ATK, 100/105/110% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (6)
- **Cooldown:** 5 Turns
- **Flags:** Location

Dark Bard Song skill. The hardest of notes leaves the deepest impression. In a size 6 Circle around you; destroys Ice Sheets, deals Sound magic damage (allies take Kickback damage), and breaks Invocations (of allies and enemies).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hard_Note)

### Hell's Beating

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/16/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 60/90/120% Sound ATK
- **Target:** Enemy
- **Cooldown:** 2 Turns
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Critical Damage Boost:** +35/40/45%

Instrument skill. Take a swing at an enemy within 1 Range with your instrument, with greatly increased Critical Damage. If it hits, all other units within a 3 Range Circle take Sound magic damage (allies take Kickback Damage), and enemies may be inflicted with Hesitation LV X (X = Rank * 5) for 3 rounds, as that probably really hurt.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hell's_Beating)

### Kick Play

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/13/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2-5
- **Power:** 40/50/60% Sound ATK, Base DMG: 10/20/30
- **Target:** Line (2-5)
- **Cooldown:** 1 Turn
- **Flags:** Enemy only

Dance skill. Play your instrument while you hop forward, kicking your foot outwards. Every time you move, you deal Sound damage to a random enemy within 2 Range. Stops upon encountering an occupied or dense tile - if an enemy is in that tile, you apply the Kick skill to them automatically.

Trickery - You play the first Bard Song skill you have equipped automatically for free.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Kick_Play)

### On A Leash

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/16/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 7
- **Power:** 100/105/110% Scaled WPN ATK, 80/90/100% Fire ATK
- **Target:** Single in 7 Range
- **Cooldown:** 1 Turn
- **Flags:** Enemy or Ally Only

Instrument skill. Put an enemy or another ally within 7 Range on a leash of fiery chains, dealing Fire magic damage to them (allies take Kickback Damage), and pulling them 2 tiles towards you. Enemies may be inflicted with Immobilize until your next turn. Allies are instead inflicted with On A Leash for 5 Rounds (if the target was an ally with this effect already, the effect ends and you regain 2M).

On A Leash: This is an effect given by the skill of the same name to allies. While active, the ally is treated as if they were Immobilized, and at the start of a new round they take Kickback Damage, but they also gain or power up Freakish Frenzy by 1 LV/3 rounds (max: LV 5, 3 rounds).

Freakish Frenzy increases Move by LV, Critical by LV * 10, and Critical Damage by LV * 5%.'

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/On_A_Leash)

### Pyrotechnics

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/17/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2-4
- **Power:** 100/105/110% Sound ATK, 100/105/110% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (2-4)
- **Flags:** Enemy or Ally

Dark Bard Song skill. Play your instrument, dealing Sound magic damage in a 2-4 size circle around you, then all tiles in a ring outside of the circle launch the pyrotechnics, dealing Fire magic damage (based on SWA + Fire ATK).

Enemies take full damage, while allies take Kickback damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Pyrotechnics)

### Raid the Rafters

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 30/25/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/105/110% Sound ATK, 100/105/110% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 1 Turn
- **Flags:** Location

Dark Bard Song skill. Play your instrument, creating projectiles in all directions around you that travel up to 6 Range away. Enemies hit by the projectiles take Sound magic damage and are pulled two tiles towards you. (Allies the projectile passes through take Kickback Damage, but do not stop the projectile and are not pulled towards you.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Raid_the_Rafters)

### Unholy Diver

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/17/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 50/55/60% Darkness ATK, Base DMG: 20/25/30
- **Target:** Single (Line To) in 4 Range
- **Cooldown:** 1 Turn
- **Flags:** Location

Dance skill. Slide along the ground, on your knees (or whatever is appropriate, if you don't have knees), playing your instrument as you do. You target an unoccupied tile within 4 Range and move in a line to it. Every time you move across a tile, you deal Dark magic damage (based on your Dark ATK and Rank) to a random enemy within 2 Range.

Trickery - You play the first Bard song you have equipped automatically for free.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Unholy_Diver)

### Dark Stage

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/17/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Circle (3)
- **Flags:** Location
- **Dark Stage LV:** 3/4/5

Give yourself the perfect place to play. In a circle around you, creates Dark Stage tiles, with a LV based on Rank (infinite duration). (This will end other Dark Stage tiles you currently have active).

Dark Stage: This is a special effect tile created by Dark Bard skills. After playing a Song or using a Dance skill, if you are on a Dark Stage tile, you create 2 Shadow Moshes at random unoccupied locations near your Dark Stage tiles (if you are at the edge of a Dark Stage, it will prefer to spawn them within 1 Range of you.)

Furthermore, allies standing on a Dark Stage tile boosts their own Fire, Dark, and Sound ATK by (LV*3), and DEF & RES by LV.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Stage)

### Crazy Riff

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Circle (5)
- **Flags:** Ally Only
- **Kickback (per):** 4/7/10

Dark Bard Song skill. Use up all of your Momentum to really shred your instrument. All allies (including yourself) within a 5 range Circle around you take Sound Kickback Damage and restore FP based on the amount of Momentum used (based on Rank).

If at least 3+ Momentum was used, Trickery: Automatically activate Pyrotechnics if the skill was equipped.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Crazy_Riff)

### Second Set

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 40/35/30
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 6 Turns
- **Flags:** Ally Only
- **Bonus Heal:** 50/100/150

Dark Bard Song skill. Play a refreshing tune with your instrument, giving all allies in the same battle enough energy for a second set. All allies with Darkness Hype consume it to restore HP equal to its LV plus a bonus based on Rank, and for every 50 LV, they gain one (or more) of the following buffs in order: +5 HP Regen, +5 FP Regen, +5 Scaled Weapon Attack, +5 Hit, +5 Critical, and +5 to all stats, for 5 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Second_Set)

### Obey Your Master

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** 100/105/110% Sound ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2 Turns
- **Flags:** Location

Dark Bard Song skill. Play your instrument towards two different locations up to 5 Range away. Applies to all tiles in a line from your position to those locations, depending on if there is a unit or not in it:

Unit - Deals Sound magic damage. (Enemies take full damage and may be inflicted with Confusion for 2 rounds, allies take Kickback Damage.)

No Unit - Creates a Shadow Mosh (if none exist in that tile).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Obey_Your_Master)

### Dark Revelry

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

A little bit of pain just makes the music more rockin'.

Allies who take damage from your Dark Bard skills gain Darkness Hype LV 10 (max LV: 300, max 10 rounds).

Darkness Hype: This is a stacking buff given by taking damage from allied Dark Bard skills. On its own, the effect grants +1 to all Elemental ATK per 10 LV. At the end of a battle, allies with Darkness Hype recover HP equal to its LV.

In addition, Darkness Hype may be used or consumed by other Dark Bard skills to gain bonus effects.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Revelry)

### Power to the People

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a SP slot.

Parry skill (but only active in certain conditions). Allied units, including yourself, within 1 Range of one or more Shadow Moshes, gain a chance to parry enemy damage, reducing it by 35% (50% if the attacker is Confused). The chance is equal to 15% per Shadow Mosh in 1 Range (+25% for the owner of this skill).

When triggered, a random Shadow Mosh in 1 Range will be blown away.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Power_to_the_People)

### Bard's Dexterity

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

A bard has to be dexterous to play their instruments well. Whether that be with fingers, teeth, or tongue depends on the situation.

Passively boosts Hit for Axe and Instrument weapons by 5.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bard's_Dexterity)

### Dark Bard's Pledge

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **SAN/STR Bonus:** +1/2/3

Pledge allegiance to the state of rock and roll.

Passively increases SAN and STR. Also grants you immunity to damage dealt by Dark Bard skills from other non-Confused allied units. (Effects like Darkness Hype will still be given, even if the damage is ignored.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Bard's_Pledge)

### Distorted Beat

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a SP slot.

There's just something a little different about this song...

Bard base class Song skills now deal Kickback Damage to allies when played, but those allies also gain Dark Distortion LV5 for 3 rounds, 1 attack.

Dark Distortion: +LV*2 Evade, +LV Armor and Magic Armor. Expires after being attacked.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Distorted_Beat)

### Elemental Shredding

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Rip apart all defenses and let the songs flow like blood.

While you are under the effect of Volume Up, skills from Bard classes that deal Fire, Dark, or Sound damage ignore up to 15% of enemies' elemental resistance. (This does not reduce those resistances below 0%.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Elemental_Shredding)

### Getting Into it

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a SP slot.

An enemy absorbing, reflecting, or being immune to your Fire, Dark, or Sound damage triggers the following effects for 2 Rounds (3 if Volume Up is active):

1) Disables absorb, reflect, and immunity for that element.

2) Inflicts Lingering Damage LV X (X = half of damage) of the same type, and Battle Rush LV 5.

3) Infliction-based chance to apply Confusion.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Getting_Into_It)
