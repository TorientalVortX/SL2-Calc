## Skills

### Deploy: Medibot

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 50
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 8 turns
- **Flags:** Location
- **Machine Stats:** +0/2/4/6/8

Deploys a robot carrying special medical supplies on it at a location within 5 Range, and is most effective when used while Total Control is on.

When a Bot is destroyed, it will leave behind Scrap at its current location. You can pick it up to reduce the FP cost of the next Deploy skill you use.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Deploy%3A_Medibot)

### Deploy: Metalaegis

- **Range:** 5

### Flamethrower

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 50/60/70/80/90% Fire ATK + 80/90/100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (4)
- **Restriction:** Gun only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Gun skill. Shoot a stream of fire from your gun in a line, dealing Fire physical damage to all enemies hit, and melting all ice tiles in the path. Any enemies hit may also be Burned and/or Feared for 3 rounds (LV = Rank * 5).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Flamethrower)

### Grenade Launcher

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/11/12/13/14
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** 2/3/4/5/6
- **Power:** 30/35/40/45/50% Fire ATK + 10/15/20/25/30% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Gun only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Gun skill. Fires a grenade, which functions like a mini-bomb, to target location within X Range (based on Rank). At the end of the round, it explodes, dealing Fire damage to all enemies within 3 Range. This bomb gains the effects of all skills that affect bombs, but only at 75% effectiveness.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Grenade_Launcher)

### Electro Shield

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/18/21/24/27
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Shield LV:** 12/24/36/48/60

Generate a shield of electricity around you for rounds equal to 2+Rank. While active, the shield will shock any enemy who attacks you with a melee weapon, dealing Lightning magic damage to them, based on Rank.

You can reactivate this skill while it's active to overload it, which will deal Lightning damage to all enemies in a 3 Range circle around you equal to 3x the level of the shield, but will also put it on a 3 round CD.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Electro_Shield)

### Upgrade (Electro Shield)

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally Only
- **Required:** Electro Shield Rank 1

Upgrade skill. Targets an ally within 2 Range. Equips that target with a shield module, which allows them to use the Electro Shield skill. ### Overclock

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 30
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** ?
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally Only
- **Lightning Damage:** 40/80/120/160/200

Targets an Engineer device and sends it into overdrive. Upon activation, the target immediately gains 2 Momentum, and will gain that much more every round. However, after every action it takes, it will lose 10% of its maximum HP. If destroyed while Overclocked, it will burst into a lightning explosion that leaves no scrap behind, dealing Lightning damage to all enemies in 3 Range. You can undo this effect by using the skill again.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Overclock)

- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** ?
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally Only
- **Salvage LV:** 10/20/30/40/50

### Reuse and Recycle

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Don't let your goodies go to waste. If an Upgrade skill of yours would end prematurely (for example, the battle ending) you recover the 15 FP you spent on the skill. Similarly, if an Engineer Bot is alive at the end of a battle, you will recover 50 FP for each.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Reuse_and_Recycle)
