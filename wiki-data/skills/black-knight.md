## Skills

### Board Shaker

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 25/26/27
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 110/125/140% Earth ATK + 50/60/70% Scaled WPN ATK + Torso Weight
- **Target:** Circle (4)
- **Cooldown:** 4 turns
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Slam something heavy, be it your weapon or foot, into the ground, causing it to shake. All enemies in a 4 Range Circle around you take Earth magic damage (this damage is increased by your Torso's weight). Enemies who take damage may also be inflicted with Clumsy for 2 rounds. (Clumsy prevents the use of movement skills besides basic Movement.)

Does not affect flying or airborne units.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Board_Shaker)

### Bright Bishop

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/14/13
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Diagonal (4)
- **Power:** 80/100/120% Light ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Sword, Spear or Axe only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Sword, Axe, or Spear skill. Fires a cross of light in a diagonal line, travelling up to 5 Range. The first enemy hit takes elemental magic damage (based on enchanted element, or Light if none), and is inflicted with Glowing LV X (3 * Rank). It will also leave behind Light Shafts with LV X for 3 rounds on each space it passes over (unless an Elemental Impact effect leaves behind a different special tile).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bright_Bishop)

### Checkmate

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2 turns
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

A heavy attack iconic to the Black Knights, using their heavy armor to crush their opponent with power. On use, attacks a nearby target. If the attack hits, you deal bonus damage equal to your Scaled DEF.

If the target is Immobilized or Flatfooted, the attack cannot miss. If they are defeated by this attack, the unit is inflicted with Certain Defeat for 6 rounds. (Certain Defeat prevents defeated enemies from being revived.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Checkmate)

### Crescent Rook

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/14/13
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Line (6)
- **Power:** 80/100/120% Wind ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Sword, Spear or Axe only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Sword, Axe, or Spear skill. Unleashes a projectile in a line that deals elemental magic damage to the first enemy hit (based on enchanted elemental).

If you have invoked the Black Wind this round, the target may also be inflicted with Interference for 3 rounds. Otherwise, you invoke the Black Wind to appear either nearby the enemy hit, or if none, the tile the projectile expired at.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Crescent_Rook)

### Hanging

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/19/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Hit Bonus:** +1/2/3 per square
- **Range (Black Wind):** 3/4/5 squares

Can only target an enemy with no adjacent allied units. Exploit your target's lack of protection, performing a basic attack with a bonus to Hit. (This bonus is higher the further away you were from the enemy.)

If you have invoked the Black Wind this round, gains +15% Critical Damage for the attack. Otherwise, invokes the Black Wind to change skill Range and teleports you near the target.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hanging)

### Castling

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally Only
- **Defense Bonus:** +3/5/7

A protective move to get an ally out of danger. Targets 1 ally within 2 Range and switches place with them. You and that ally also gain a Defense buff based on Rank for 3 rounds.

If the Black Wind has been invoked this round, the duration of the buff is doubled. Otherwise, the Range of the skill increases by 5, and you invoke the Black Wind to utilize it.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Castling)

### Prophylaxis

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Round (1)
- **Cooldown:** 5 turns
- **Flags:** Location

Prepare yourself to fend off the worst. Creates a group of counter-attack tiles at all tiles within 1 Range of you. When an enemy steps on one and you are within 1 Range of that tile, you perform a basic attack (treated as a counter attack) with a bonus to your Critical Damage equal to X (X = Rank * 10%). If the attack hits and the enemy was using basic Movement, they stop.

The tiles will last for 2 rounds or until triggered.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Prophylaxis)

### Forced Move

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Circle (3)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Size:** 3/4/5
- **Push/Pull:** 2/3/4 tiles

Utilize sheer force to manipulate your enemies into making unwise moves. Inflicts Forced Move LV X (X = Rank) on all enemies within a circle until your next turn, reducing their Move by LV.

If you have invoked the Black Wind this round, all those enemies are also pushed back 1+X tiles. Otherwise, you invoke the Black Wind to draw them closer, pulling them 1+X tiles towards you.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Forced_Move)

### Unmoving

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only

Every move a Black Knight makes should be deliberate. Until your next turn, inflicts Immobilize and Unmoving on yourself. Unmoving prevents pull and push effects from affecting you, and grants immunity to Vydel's movement effects (though you still take damage).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Unmoving)

### Black Deflection

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Black Knights are capable of turning their foe's momentum against them. When you successfully trigger your own Parry skill, you gain +5 Hit for 2 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Black_Deflection)

### Black Drain

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Sap the strength from your foes using the mysterious power of the Black Knight's gale. If you have invoked the Black Wind, physical damage dealt from Soldier class skills drains the target of their Scaled Weapon ATK, as a stacking status effect (1 LV, 3 rounds per damage instance, max LV 1+(Rank*3), 10 rounds). You gain the same amount of Scaled Weapon ATK. (The drain effect can apply to multiple enemies, but you only gain this status once.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Black_Drain)

### Stalemate

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Base Chance:** 5/10/15/20/25%

Parry skill (only one takes effect at a time.)

Grants the ability to parry attacks and skill damage (excluding spells) from Sword, Axe, Spear, or monster weapons, at a chance equal to half of your Scaled DEF + a bonus based on Rank. When triggered, damage is reduced by 50%. If the attacker was within 1 Range, and you have a weapon with 15 Weight or more equipped, they are also inflicted with Flatfoot LV 1 until their next turn.

You can only parry attacks you can see.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Stalemate)

### Sudden Death

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Trigger:** Scaled SKI + 5

It is painfully obvious when a Black Knight is unaffected by a strike. When an enemy deals physical damage equal to or less than your Scaled SKI+5, they are Feared for 3 rounds. In addition, enemies who you have Feared suffer -15 Critical Evade versus you.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sudden_Death)

### Grandmaster

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

A sharp mind is a deadly weapon. Increases your Hit with Greatsword, Greataxe, and Greatlance weapons by half of your scaled GUI.

Additionally, your Tactician's class's Tactics Rank boosts the range of Black Knight projectiles and skills that can invoke the Black Wind to gain increased range (by half of its value). Furthermore, Tactics Rank cannot decrease due to taking damage during a turn your Parry skill triggered, or have invoked the Black Wind.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Grandmaster)

### Indomitable

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Damage Reduction:** 4/7/10%

An unrelenting will that numbs pain. If you are wearing Heavy Armor with 10 or more Weight: Reduces physical damage taken and your total Battle Weight by 1+(Rank*3)% (10% at max rank). Against enemies that you have inflicted with Fear, the reduction is doubled.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Indomitable)

### Iron Wall

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Defense Bonus:** +1/2/3/4/6

A master of defense can fend off attacks no matter what the condition. Passively increases your DEF. If at max Rank, further increases DEF by +1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Iron_Wall)

### Knight's Intimidation

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Black Knights have a reputation for being intimidating figures, even saying to give those who know no fear a taste of it. You can inflict Fear on enemies who are normally immune to it (excluding Boss monsters). In addition, defeating a unit will attempt to inflict Fear on all other enemies for 3 Rounds.

Main Class only.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Knight's_Intimidation)

### Negation

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Critical Evade Bonus:** +6/12/18

An otherworldly quality possessed by the user of the Black Wind. Blades and blows seem to veer off their course to lethal spots against the Black Knight. When wearing Heavy Armor, Passively increases your Critical Evade, lessening your chance of taking critical hits. Does not work against Tome weapons.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Negation)

### Zugzwang

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

A tactic that forces enemies to make moves they don't really want to. Enemies affected by your Forced Move, Flatfoot, or Fear status effects have their Parry skill's reduction effectiveness halved and suffer -5 Evade (versus you only).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Zugzwang)
