## Skills

### Blood Spike

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17/18/19
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** 100% Scaled WPN ATK + 50/60/70/80/90/% Ice/Dark ATK
- **Target:** Single (2)
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Costs 8% of your maximum HP. Targets a 2 Range circle up to 3 Range away. All enemies in that circle take ice and water damage (split evenly) and have claret call applied to them twice.

The range of the spell is increased based on your Rising Game level. Sword and Axe weapons are valid casting tools.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Blood_Spike)

### Cut Down

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17/18/19
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Axe Skill. Two quick swings from your axe on an enemy in 1 Range. The first swing applies Claret Call, and the second consumes it, deals damage, and inflicts Cut Down LV X (X = half of Claret Call's level) for 3 rounds. Cut Down reduces the targets Phys and Magic Defense by LV%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cut_Down)

### Death Gaze

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 25/22/19/16/13
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 100/125/150/175/200% Dark ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

A piercing gaze that seems to come from the grim reaper itself. The target of this skill must be marked by Claret Call and be Feared, and both must be caused by you. The target loses the Claret Call marking and becomes Unmarkable for 2 rounds, but is Silenced for 2 rounds. They also take Dark damage that ignores armor and evasion equal to 100% (+25% per additional Rank) of your Dark ATK.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Death_Gaze)

### Ether Invitation

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 35/32/29/26/23
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** -
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2 Rounds
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Cursed Wounds Duration:** 1/2/3/4/5 Rounds

An invitation to the underworld. Targets an enemy within 1 Range and pulls all Claret Calls on all enemies to them, stacking them together. Afterwards, you perform a basic attack on the target, which deals bonus damage equal to 20% of your missing HP. If it hits, and they are marked with Claret Call, the Claret Call explodes in the wound, consuming it and making them Unmarkable for 2 rounds, but giving them Cursed Wound LV X (X = damage dealt by the attack).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ether_Invitation)

### Ghastly Strike

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17/18/19
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Sword skill. Attack an enemy in 1 range with a quick sword strike, then fake a follow up. Deals Slash physical damage to the target. If you have inflicted Fear on them, they may try to protect themselves from the fake hit, having the chance to inflict Guard LV 1 on them. The overall success rate is increased by the LV of you Claret Call the target has (if any).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ghastly_Strike)

### Reaper Scythe

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 24/26/28/30/32
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** 110/120/130/140/150% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Crescent (2)
- **Cooldown:** 1 round
- **Restriction:** Axe only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Axe or Scythe skill. A deadly blade that cannot be protected against. Deals Slash magic damage to all enemies in a crescent pattern. If any of those enemies are marked by Claret Call, they can be guard broken, but become Unmarkable until your next turn.

(Guard Break: If the target is Guarding, it's removed and they take 15% more damage for 1 round.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Reaper_Scythe)

### Scarlet Twister

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 20/21/22/23/24
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3-12
- **Power:** 100% Scaled WPN ATK + 50//60/70/80/90% Wind/Dark ATK
- **Target:** Circle (2-11)
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Sylphid
- **Pull:** 2-11 tiles

Costs 10% of your maximum HP. Targets a 2 range circle up to 3 range away. All enemies in those tiles are pulled 2 tiles towards the centre, and then all enemies in the circle take Dark and Wind damage (split evenly), and have Claret Call applied (twice if in 2 range of the center).

Size and pull strength are increased based on Rising Game level. Sword and Axe weapons are valid casting tools.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Scarlet_Twister)

### Wraithguard

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/15/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 5 rounds
- **Flags:** Location, Main class skill
- **Wraith HP:** 100/200/300

Creates a ghostly spirit that is tied to you at a target location within 3 Range. When you take damage, it is reduced by 30% and that reduction is subtracted from the Wraith's HP instead. The Wraith can still take damage normally and gets no turn, so try to keep it away from the action.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Wraithguard)

### Dark Imbue

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16/14/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only
- **Hit Bonus:** +3/6/9/12/15
- **Dark ATK:** 10/20/30/40/50%

Axe skill. Causes your Axe to extend with a dark blade similar to a scythe, increasing its Hit with Axe weaponsand dealing Dark magic damage when you hit an enemy with a basic attack with an Axe weapon, or with a Ghost Axe skill.

This effect lasts for 4 rounds. At Rising Game LV 3, it lasts for an additional round. At Rising Game LV 6, its Momentum cost is reduced by 2.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dark_Imbue)

### Gravestone

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 100/150/200% Earth ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location

Creates up to one impassable but attackable Gravestone at target location for 3 rounds. Enemies in that tile take Earth magic damage and are knocked airborne. Enemies within 3 range of a Gravestone take double bonus damage from Claret Call, and Wraiths within 3 range of a Gravestone take no damage from enemies.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Gravestone)

### Haunting

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/11/7
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5/6/7
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Flags:** Location

Disappear into the mist and reappear at target empty square. Any enemies you have marked with Claret Call within 1 Range of that location are Feared for the duration. Lasts 2 rounds.

If Rising Game level is 3 or higher, the cooldown of this skill is reduced by 1 round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Haunting)

- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --

Draw back and consumes all of your Claret Calls on all enemies, which sap HP and FP from those enemies equal to their LV, restoring your HP and FP for each (HP recovery is increased by X% of your Scaled RES).

### Red Rain

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Circle (2)
- **Cooldown:** 3 Rounds
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Duration:** 3/4/5 Rounds

Costs 5% of your Maximum HP. Creates a localized rainstorm of blood at a target location up to 5 Range away. When created, and at the start of every round, enemies in the rainstorm have Claret Call applied to them. At the start of a new round, if no enemies are in the area of effect, the rainstorm will creep towards the nearest enemy.

The rainstorm lasts for 2+Rank rounds, but can be dissipated by certain effects.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Red_Rain)

### Afflicted Spectre

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

The Ghost thrives on drawing out hidden power from situations where they are at a disadvantage, but sometimes that doesn't always mean 'near death'. While this skill is equipped, the Ghost's maximum HP is increased by 5% for every negative status effect that are suffering from.

Small Note: There may be a spelling mistake in the skill's in game description. It might be 'that they're suffering from' as opposed to 'that are suffering from'.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Afflicted_Spectre)

### Last Chance

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

As a last ditch effort, the Ghost can absorb the Claret Calls from their attackers, leaving them one step above actually being dead. When the Ghost takes damage that leaves them at 0 HP or less from an enemy that is marked by the user's Claret Call, they instead remain at 1 HP. However, the attacker has their Claret Call marking removed, and cannot be remarked for 3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Last_Chance)

### Anti-Curse

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Resistance Bonus:** +1/2/3/4/5

An innate property of the Ghost that wards off supernatural and magical effects. Passively increases your RES.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Anti-Curse)

### Claret Call

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

A red mark that increases pain. When Claret Call is applied to an enemy, it gives them or powers up a status by LV X (X = 1, or 2 if below 50% HP) that lasts for 3 rounds. Claret Call boosts the damage of basic attacks, as well as the damage of Ghost skills.

Claret Call is applied by dealing physical damage to an enemy, or by various Ghost skill effects. (Max LV is equal to 5 + Ghost level.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Claret_Call)

### Fitting Form

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5

Ghosts, as in the spectres, do not typically carry a lot of weight in their appearance, so it is only fitting when Ghost duelists follow suit. Increases the base LV of Claret Call applications for each weapon and torso you have that is 5 Weight or less.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fitting_Form)

### Pain Tolerance

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **HP Bonus:** +8/16/24/32/48

Ghosts are used to having to deal with shedding blood - sometimes their own. They can operate effectively in less ideal circumstances (and with less blood) than others, increasing their Hit Points. At max Rank, further increases maximum HP by 8.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Pain_Tolerance)

### Rising Game

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Affected Stats:** SKI/CEL/STR/WIL/LUC

The true strength of the Ghost that causes their combat parameters to significantly increases as death creeps closer. Something inside of them is honed with each step into the grave. Fortune smiles upon the reckless. This skill causes specific statistics to increase based on your missing HP. For every 15% of your HP that is missing, the affected stats increase by +1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Rising_Game)
