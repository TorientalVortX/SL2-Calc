## Skills

### Aerial Razor

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** 80/90/100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (3-6)
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **DEF Reduction:** -2/4/6/8/10
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Bow skill. Fire a razor sharp arrow with great force in a line. Deals Pierce physical damage and inflicts Aerial Razor for 3 rounds. An enemy at the very end of the line takes 1.25x damage.

If Dynamic Shooting is at Rank 2 or higher, the length of the line can be adjusted.

#### Elemental Impact

Fire - Creates Cinder tiles. Ice - Creates Ice Sheet tiles. Earth - Increases damage by Rank * 2. Wind - Increases range. Lightning - May lightning critical. Dark - Creates Darkness tiles. Light - Creates Photon tiles.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Aerial_Razor)

### Cripple Arm

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 20/22/24/26/28
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Weapon
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 5
- **Restriction:** Bow, Gun only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only, Main class skill
- **STR/SKI Penalty:** -1/2/3/4/5

Bow/Gun skill. Performs a basic attack (only 1 Round for Guns), aiming at the arms (or facsimile) of a target within your weapon's attack range with a 20% penalty to Hit. If it hits, the target's STR and SKI are reduced by X (X = Rank), and have the use of any Hands slot item they have disabled for 3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cripple_Arm)

### Entangle Trap

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 8/10/14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2/2/3/3/4
- **Power:** 100% GUI, 50/60/70/80/90% Earth ATK
- **Target:** Single in 2 Range
- **Flags:** Location
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Places an entangle trap at an unoccupied square within 2 range for 3 turns. If an enemy walks within 2 cardinal squares of the trap, it triggers, which halts the triggerer's Movement action (if any), will cause all tiles in its Range to be covered in Thick Vines for 3 rounds, as well as dealing Earth physical damage to the one who triggered it.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Entangle_Trap)

### Pulling Shot

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Bonus Damage:** +5/10/15
- **Pull:** 2/3/4 Tiles

Bow skill. Fire an arrow tied to a rope, and use that rope to pull your target/fish in. Performs a basic attack on the target that deals bonus damage; if successful, the target is pulled towards you a number of squares based on Rank.

If Dynamic Shooting is at Rank 5 or higher, if the target is suffering from any status that decreases Move, they are inflicted with Knocked Down.

### Snake Shot

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 14/15/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Poison Level/Acid Damage:** 5/10/15

Bow Skill. Fire an arrow coated with poison at the target, performing a basic attack on them. If it hits, the target takes armor-ignoring acid magic bonus damage and may be inflicted with Poison LV X for 3 rounds (based on Rank).

If Dynamic Shooting is at Rank 4 or higher, if the target is already Poisoned, this skill instead inflicts Snake Wrap by twisting a rope around their legs, which halves Move until your next turn.

### Starbow

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 10/12/14/16/18
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** 80%/90%/100%/110%/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Diamond (3)
- **Restriction:** Bow only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Excel Crash:** Can be empowered by Weapon Charges.

A trickshot, launching several arrows into the air and raining them down long-range onto the targets. Deals Pierce physical damage to all within the Area of Effect. Additional ranks in the skill will increase its range.

If Dynamic Shooting is at Rank 3 or higher, the damage is increased by 35% of your Scaled SKI.

#### Excel Crash

LV1 - +10% Damage per Charge Level

LV2 - Arrows explode after a short delay, dealing Fire magic bonus damage to all enemies in those tiles equal to 100% Fire ATK. You also attack any attackable field objects in those tiles.

LV3 - Ignores Evasion.

Any weapon that uses this skill can consume active Weapon Charges to gain these effects, depending on the amount consumed.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Starbow)

### Fortune Wind

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/15/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally Only
- **Evade Bonus:** 5/10/15 + 50% caster's Scaled LUC
- **Domain:** Sylphid

A spell that protects the target in a wrap of wind. The target gains Evade equal to X (Based on Rank) + 50% the caster's Scaled LUC. Lasts for 5 rounds. If used on a target that already has the effect, the effect will end, but all enemies with 4 Range will take Wind magic damage equal to Wind ATK + 3x the Evade bonus and be knocked back 1 square. ### Marked Target

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up any Skill Pool slots.

Toggle skill. A two fold ability.

1) If toggled on, Pulling Shot will pull the nearest target towards the nearest trap (relative to them) you have set instead of you.

2) Enemies who trigger and/or are damaged by your traps become Trap Marked for 3 rounds. The mark is consumed when you hit the enemy with a basic attack, but causes the attack they're hit with to deal 15% extra damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Marked_Target)
