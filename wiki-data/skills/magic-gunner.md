- **Max rank:** 5

- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only

Overloads the Gun with Focus energy, dramatically increasing its power output. ### Akimbo

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

If you have a Handgun-type Gun as both your Main Weapon and Sub Weapon, when you use Attack or Sub-Attack, enables the Twin Dance skill from Rogue base class, even if you do not have it active. In addition, the sub-weapon will use all its rounds instead of just 1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Akimbo)

### Safety

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

By turning on the safety of your gun, you will not consume Charge Shot or Overcharge when you attack. This skill can be toggled on and off during your turn for free.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Safety)

### Spark Drive

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Required:** Charged Shot rank 1
- **Activation Chance:** 20/40/60/80/100%

A special modification to an arcane gun that allows it to steal focus from enemies. At a percentage based on rank, when you land a Critical Hit on an enemy with a Gun weapon, you will automatically become charged as if by Charge Shot. Once activated, this skill is put on a 2 round cooldown.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Spark_Drive)

### Stand Off

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Defensive Knowledge skill. Used to holding their ground, the Magic Gunner's confidence allows them to intercept attacks made at them, increasing Phys. Def by 1% by 20% of your Scaled WIL. (Multiple Defensive Knowledge skills will not stack).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Stand_Off)

### Thunder Drive

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Required:** Spark Drive rank 1, Overcharge rank 1
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

A further augment to the Spark Drive that causes it to draw too much energy from the target. If Spark Drive would trigger while you already have a Charge Shot prepared, you become Overcharged.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Thunder_Drive)

### Arcane Gun

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Rank 1:** +5 FP Regen

The Magic Gunner utilizes a special gun that has been modified with certain magical parts, letting them utilize what are called 'Magic Bullets'. Magic Bullets have three different effects listed: Shell, Bonus, and High Charge. You can only have 1 Bullet selected at a time. When you attack with a Gun weapon, on-hit, the Shell effect is applied. See Charge Shot and Overcharge for info on the other two effects.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Arcane_Gun)

### Exposure Tolerance

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **CEL/RES Boost:** +1/+2/+3

When you're constantly exposed to things like danger and magic, you tend to build up a resistance to them. Passively increases CEL and RES based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Exposure_Tolerance)

### Focused Mind

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Hit Bonus:** +3/6/9/12/15

When firing a critical shot, it's important to be completely accurate. While the Magic Gunner has a Charged Shot prepared, they gain a bonus to Hit while attacking with a Gun. If they are Overcharged, the bonus is doubled.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Focused_Mind)
