## Skills

### Anchor Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Engrave: Attempts to inflict the target with Immobilize until your next turn. (Cooldown 1 turn longer when used this way)

Trigger: Attempts to inflict Immobilize on all enemies within effective range for 2 rounds.

Allies within the effective range of this rune are immune to most push and pull effects.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Anchor_Rune)

### Chilling Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Placed: Covers tiles in effective range (+1) in Ice Sheets for 2 rounds.

Triggered: Covers tiles in effective range in Ice Sheets for 3 rounds. Enemies in those tiles may be inflicted with Frostbite LV X for 3 rounds. (X = Rune Level * 3)

Engrave: Attempts to inflict Frostbite LV X (X = Rank * 5) for 3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Chilling_Rune)

### Chronos Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Linked Runes do not trigger normally. Rune Level increased by Chronos count. When Chronos count is 4, effective range is increased by 3.

Engrave: Attempts to inflict the target with Slow LV X (X = Rank) for 2 rounds.

Resonate: Increased Chronos count by 1 if not at 4. If it was at 4, the rune will trigger, unless a Null rune was linked with it.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Chronos_Rune)

### Destruction Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Placed: Destroys enemy-created special effect tiles with a Hardiness of 1 or less in it's effective range (+1).

Linked runes that deal damage have +30% increased damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Destruction_Rune)

### Elemental Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** 100% Scaled Weapon Attack (+100% Elemental attack based on tome)
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Engrave: Deals elemental magic damage to the target.

Triggered: Deals elemental magic damage to enemies in range, increasing with Rune Level.

Damage dealt is based on your currently tome (Or fire, by default),(100% of SWA + 100% Elemental Attack).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Elemental_Rune)

### Haste Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Resonate: All allies in X range gain +2 Move for 1 round.

Engrave: Target Ally gains + Rank Move until your next turn.

Linked Runes that deal damage are given Great Accuracy.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Haste_Rune)

### Illuminated Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Resonate: Attempts to inflict all enemies within effective range (+1) with Glowing LV X for two rounds.(X = Rank * Rune Level)

Engrave: Attempts to inflict the target with Glowing LV X for 2 rounds. (X = Rank * 5)

Trigger: Attempts to inflict Glowing LV X on all enemies in effective range for Y rounds. (X = same as Resonate LV, Y = higher of 3 or 1 + half the rune level)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Illuminated_Rune)

### Magnet Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Placed: If any Runes that can be linked are within 3 Range, draws them in and links them. (Only triggers on move effects once.)

Resonate: Moves towards the nearest enemy.

Engrave: Attempts to inflict Magnetize LV X (X = Rank * 5) for 2 rounds.

Trigger: Attempts to inflict enemies in effective range with Magnetize LV X (X= Rune Level * (Rank * 2)) for higher of 3 or 1 + Rune Level/2 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Magnet_Rune)

### Razor Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** 100%/110%/120% Scaled Weapon Attack
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Resonate/Trigger: Deals X Slash magic damage to all enemies in effective range (+1), which ignores armour. (X = 5 * Rune Level)

Engrave: Deals Slash magic damage to the target.

Resonate effect will trigger every time the Rune moves.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Razor_Rune)

### Smoking Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Placed: Creates Smokescreen tiles LV X for 2 rounds in effective range (+1) if none exist. (LV = Rank x 3 + Rune Level).

Engrave: Attempts to inflict the target with Blind for 2 rounds.

Trigger: Attempts to inflict Blind for (1 + half of Rune Level) rounds to all enemies in effective range.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Smoking_Rune)

### Warding Rune

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Place/Resonate: Creates stone pillars on certain tiles without units on them for 1 round within it's effective range (Max range: 4). At Rune Level 2 or higher, the tiles are not created in the same column as the rune.

Engrave: Knocks the target back in the direction they're facing X times. (X = Rank).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Warding_Rune)

### All Rush

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 5
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 1 turn
- **Flags:** Location

By snapping your fingers, you command all of your active Runes to fly forward 5 tiles. Moving this way can cause the rune to link with other runes, as well as trigger on enemies in it's effective range.

Deals 10 damage to the rune after forcing it to move.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/All_Rush)

### Designia: Alpha

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Momentum:** 0
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 1 turn
- **Flags:** Single in 5 Range

The Rune Magician follows through on simple pre-planned patterns at a target location within 5 Range, using equipped Rune spells in order at that location. These uses all require the proper FP cost, use the appropriate amount of Momentum, and so on - this simply serves as a quick 'shortcut' method. The effect will continue until you have 1 Momentum left, or when no more Rune spells can be used.

Order: Same order as Rune spells equipped.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Designia%3A_Alpha)

### Arcanum: Rune

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Rune Magicians are masters of runes, essentially arcane writings and patterns that can evoke predictable magic spells and effects. Runes can have a variety of effects; Placed effects activate when the rune is created on the battlefield. Resonate effects activate around the Rune at the start of a new round. Trigger effects activate when an enemy steps into the effective range of a Rune. Engrave effects activate if the Rune spell targets a unit instead of a location.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Arcanum%3A_Rune)

### Salvage Rune

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Letting no rune go to waste, you quickly recover them if they're left unused. At the end of a battle, for every Rune left untriggered, you recover FP equal to 10 times it's rune level, effectively refunding the cost.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Salvage_Rune)

### Sturdy Rune

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Rune HP Bonus:** +10 (+20 if your Main and Sub Class are both Rune Magician)

Having refined your rune crafting skills, your runes are sturdier than normal, granting them +10 HP (Or +20 HP if both your Main and Sub Class are both Rune Magician)

Runes start with 10 HP and each attack on them takes away 10 HP.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sturdy_Rune)
