## Skills

### Akihono Tobitoro

- **Type:** Offensive _(wiki: Offensive Skill)_
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/20/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Crescent (2) in 4 Range
- **Power:** 80%/90%/100% Fire ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK, LV: 5/9/13
- **Target:** Enemy Only
- **Cooldown:** 1 Round
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Nerifian

Ninjutsu. Throw flaming shuriken at a 2 Zie Crescent up to 4 Range away, dealing fire magic damage that can critically hit (25% damage) over 2 hits. Damaged enemies may be inflicted with Lingering Damage (Fire) (2 Rounds), or Burn instead (if critically hit). Size, total hits and status LV increase based on total Shuriken equipped (+1, +1, +3 respectively)

Furinkazan: Gain 1 Fire Mark

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Akihono_Tobitoro)

### Arashi Sanseiken

- **Type:** Offensive _(wiki: Offensive Skill)_
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/20/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Diamond (3)
- **Power:** 100%/110%/120% Wind ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** enemy Only
- **Cooldown:** 3 Rounds
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Sylphid

Ninjutsu. Targets all enemies in a 3 Size Diamond (+1 Size per 25 Ice ATK, max 5) around you, increasing your aerodynamic qualities with ki for an instant. Teleport next to each enemy you can and slash with a gusting blade, dealing Wind magic damage.

Trickery: If you have Kagebunshin active, each time before you teleport, you leave behind a Kagebunshin.

Furinkazan: Gain 1 Wind Mark.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Arashi_Sanseiken)

### Bone Breaker

- **Type:** Offensive _(wiki: Offensive Skill)_
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/20/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Single in 1 Range
- **Power:** 50% Scaled WPN ATK, LV: 1/2/3
- **Target:** Enemy Only
- **Cooldown:** 3 Rounds
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Kenpo. Targets 1 Knocked Down or Grappled (by you) enemy in 1 Range. Perform a painful joint strike, dealing evasion-ignoring Blunt damage. If target is Knocked Down, inflicts Cripple Leg (LV X, 2 rounds). If Grappled, inflicts Cripple Arm (LV X, 2 rounds). Regardless, they're cured of the effect (except non-boss monster or summoned units).

Shukuchi: Range increases by its LV and teleports you next to the target.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bone_Breaker)

### Kagebunshin

- **Type:** Defensive _(wiki: Defensive Skill)_
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/15/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Single (Line to) in 5 Range
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Location
- **Cooldown:** 1 Round
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Genpo skill. You manipulate your shadow and create a Kagebunshin at your current location, gain Sneak (2 rounds), and then swiftly dash towards another unoccupied tile within 5 range in a flexible line.

Kagebunshin: Kagebunshin are shadow clones of the user. They are illusion units, but do not receive turns, and will fade away at the start of the cloned unit's next turn (unless otherwise stated). Primarily, they are used as decoys.

If an enemy within 2 Range of a Kagebunshin defeats it, and you have the Evasive Strike skill equipped, that enemy will receive its damage.

### Assassination Art

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Specializing in striking targets before they know what hit them is the shinobi way.

On Battle Start: If your party back attacked the enemy, all enemies take protection-ignoring damage equal to your Shinobi class level. (Only the highest Assassination Art takes effect, but all other units with the same skill increase the damage by 10. Damage dealt this way cannot defeat enemies.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Assassination_Art)

### False Target

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Parry skill. If you can use movement skills, at a chance of 25% + (higher of 25% CEL & 50% DEF) + 15% (if enemy is under your Genpo), you can parry incoming physical damage (35% reduction).

Once per round, when this triggers, you teleport to an empty tile within 3 Range (except in 1 Range of an enemy) and leave behind a Ninja Trick.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/False_Target)

### Furinkazan

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Toggle Skill. While on, the shinobi can gain elemental marks; Fire, Forest, Mountain, and Wind. A maximum of 4 total marks, and 2 of each type, can be accumulkated by utilizing various Shinobi skills. Once 4 marks have been acquired, an Elemental Art based on the combination of marks automatically activates (no marks can be gained during this round after).

Marks can be gained by casting spells gained via Scrolls; the type gained depends on the spell's domain.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Furinkazan)

### Hand Signs

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

One of the shinobi's hidden arts. Using hand signs and ki, the Shinobi can sidestep the need for verbal components. As long as they aren't immobilized and have at least 40 CEL, they can cast Spells (excluding invocations) and Ninjutsu even while Silenced.

Additionally, Shinobi can utilize fist weapons as casting tools.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hand_Signs)

- **Max rank:** 1

The shinobi is a master of unarmed combat, capable of flowing one blow into another like droplets of rain forming a stream.

Allows your Shinobi class Knepo skills, and Martial Artist base class Offensive skills to build and utilize Combination Fighter.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Kenpo_Master)

### Formless Form

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

The shinobi is a shadow; a shape without form, ever changing and ever moving.

The Shinobi has 10% FP Efficency for any movement skill. They also passively gain 10% Status Resistance versus Immobilize, Frozen and Slow effects.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Formless_Form)

### Gear Mastery

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Damage:** +5/10/15%

Because of the circumstances, limitations, and available resources for any mission are wildly different, Shinobi are trained in the use of a variety of tools.

As such, item-based skills (such as bombs), most damaging skills give nby their equipment (except basic attacks), and Item Potentials gain a damage bonus when used by them. This bonus is also applied to the EXP gained towards Item Potentials.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Gear_Mastery)

### Shuriken Wrap

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

A shuriken wrap, when unrolled, allows a shinobi quick access to a valuable throwing weapon.

For Shinobi skills and Ninjutsu subtalents that can use a Shuriken, if no other valid item is equipped, a Shuriken in the Item Belt can also be used instead (if possible). Additionally, Shuriken in the Item Belt also count for Shinobi effects that care about the number of them equipped.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Shuriken_Wrap)
