## Skills

### Perfected Tolerance

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

Exposure to certain hazards has made the Chemist a little better at dealing with them. Grants +15% Poison Resistance, and +15% effectiveness to potions you use (including Mixtures). If Monoclassing Chemist, both effects are doubled.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Perfected_Tolerance)

### Specimen Containment

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Uh, they weren't supposed to get a hold of that... +10% Damage and Grapple success VS enemies with a beneficial Mixture, Mutagen, Vaccine or Perfume effect caused by you (5% damage versus non-monsters). Grappling such a target will reduce one of those effects by 3 rounds, and may Pacify non-boss monsters (until your next turn) if this ends the effect. (3 round CD per unit)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Specimen_Containment)
