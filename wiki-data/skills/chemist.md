### Alchemic Barrage

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 35
- **Range:** 2
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Required weapon:** Bow, Gun, Fist, Strings
- **Tags:** Alchemic

Requires an unused Magic Stone in your item belt. Prepares a barrage, up to 1 per chemical type (A, B, etc.), up to a max of your Momentum. Then, launches them at an enemy in 2 Range as Alchemic Bullets. Momentum cost equals the number of Stones used.

Damage against your Marked Targets consumes the mark to increase damage by its LV% and gain Great Accuracy. +2 Range under Longdraw and consumes it.

| --- | --- |
| 1 | 0M, 35 FP, 100% Elem ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK |

### Eight-Pointed Weaving

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 24/22/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Octagon (1-3)
- **Cooldown:** 1 Round(s)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Required weapon:** Strings

Targets a 1-3 Size Octagon around yourself, weaving a complex shape of strings, which last until your next turn, which deals Slash damage to enemies in those tiles or who cross over it (once).

If you have Coated Ammo active, 1 LV is used to coat the strings; any enemies that trigger them will also have its effect applied to them (once, doesn't use further LVs).

- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Momentum:** 2
- **Power:** --
