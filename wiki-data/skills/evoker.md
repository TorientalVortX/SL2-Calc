## Skills

### Exgalfa

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 43/42/41
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** 140/160/180% Fire ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Nerifian

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation (see the Invocation talent for more details).

Targets 1 enemy within 6 Range and fires 6 fireballs at them, dealing Fire magic damage to them. Every fireball has an even chance of dealing plus or minus 5*Rank extra damage. (Positive effect chance increases with LUC.) If you are enchanted with Nerhaven, two extra fireballs are fired.

#### Invocation

"Burn the body, count the ashes..."

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Exgalfa)

### Intensify Cold

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 43/42/41
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** 140/160/180% Ice ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Aquarian

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation (see the Invocation talent for more details).

Targets 1 enemy within 6 Range and rapidly freezes the air around them, dealing Ice magic damage. The target also gains a 50% Weakness to Ice for 3 rounds. If enchanted with Kraken, the target is also inflicted with Frostbite LV (20+ 10 * Rank) for 3 rounds.

#### Invocation

"Be scarred by this arctic claw's embrace..."

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Intensify_Cold)

### Isenshi

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 43/42/41
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 140/160/180% Earth ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (6)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Isespian

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation (see the Invocation talent for more details).

Raises sharp stones in a 6 Range Circle around yourself, dealing Earth magic damage. Airborne enemies within 3 range that are also magnetized by the user are slammed into the ground taking +(Rank*5)% damage, become knocked down, and the Damage has Great accuracy.

If enchanted with Galren, all enemies Magnetized by the user are drawn in 3 tiles prior to damage.

#### Invocation

"Rage and pierce, spears of earth..."

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Isenshi)

### Libegrande

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 25/27/29
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Ice ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (4)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Aquarian

Freeze a sword made of ice at target location and launch it in a line, dealing Ice magic damage to the first enemy hit. Each tile passed over will also be covered in an Ice Sheet that lasts for up to 3 rounds. If enchanted with Kraken, the enemy hit with this spell will suffer from Guard Break (if guarding) before damage is applied.

Excel Crash: Level 1 - 10% Damage per Charge level. Level 2 - Upon hitting an enemy, the sword dances to all other enemies within 3 range circle around the target, dealing slash and Ice damage to them using the spell's normal power. Level 3 - Ignore Evasion.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Libegrande)

### Magaisendo

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 36/34/32
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 100/110/120%% Earth ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (5)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Isespian

Targets a 3-tile wide, 5 tile long line in one direction from you. Causes earthen spikes to erupt in that line, dealing Earth magic damage to all enemies hit. If enchanted with Galren, the tremor will also destroy Hardiness 1 Special effect tiles in the area of effect.

Excel Crash: Level 1 - 10% Damage per Charge level. Level 2 - Enemies you have magnetized, who are also in the tiles at your sides, are pushed 1 tiles in the direction you are facing before damage. Rock tiles in the area of effect will explode, and any enemies within 1 range of them will have a chance to be inflicted with Lingering Damage (Earth) Lv X (X = Charges*10) for 1 rounds. Level 3 - Ignore Evasions.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Magaisendo)

- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Lightning ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK

### Sear

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 26/24/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Fire ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (1)
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Nerifian

Create a firestorm at the target area, dealing Fire magic damage to all in the area. It will also create Cinders for 3 rounds in the affected area, with a LV equal to 50% of your Fire ATK. If enchanted with Nerhaven, the AoE size is increased slightly.

Excel Crash: Level 1 - 10% Damage per charge level. Level 2 - rapidly melts ice sheets in the area of effect, granting a chance to inflict Burn Lv X (X = 10 + Charges*5) for 2 rounds on any enemy standing on one. Level 3 - Ignore Evasion.

### Vydel

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 43/42/41
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 140/160/180% Wind ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (12)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Sylphid

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation (see the Invocation talent for more details).

Creates a tornado that rampages through a 3 tile wide, 12 tile long line. Enemies in the path of the tornado are sucked up and taken along for the ride. Afterwards, they are spit out to a random, unoccupied tile, and take Wind magic damage. If enchanted with Talvyd, all enemies are knocked down when they are thrown from the tornado.

#### Invocation

### Wind Slasher

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 24/26/28
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Wind ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (2-5)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Slash Damage:** 30/40/50
- **Domain:** Sylphid

Creates a sharp blade of wind in a line that cuts through all enemies, dealing Wind magic damage to all enemies hit. The first enemy hit also takes Slash physical damage based on Rank that ignores armor. The size of the line can be resized.

If enchanted with Talvyd, the first enemy hit may be inflicted with Lingering Damage (Slash) with LV equal to the Slash damage, for 3 rounds.

Excel Crash: Level 1 - 10% per charge level. Level 2 - Increases minimum and maximum size by amount of Charges. Level 3 - Ignore Evasion.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Wind_Slasher)

### Charge Mind

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 20
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only

Rev up your mind into a magical battery, gaining Charge Mind LV 3 for 2 rounds. When casting spells from Mage classes that have Excel Crash capabilities, Charge Mind will be expended as if it were Weapon Charges equal to it's LV, then its LV will decrease by 1. (For example, at LV3, it will grant one spell its 3 Charge effect, then become LV2. he next spell will gain its 2 Charge effects, etc. Doesn't stack with Weapon charges.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Charge_Mind)

### Elemental Overtime

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Mind over matter, the saying goes. Why not both? After using Charge mind, you also gain Elemental overtime for the same duration, which increases the damage dealt by Elemental Augment by 100%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Elemental_Overtime)

### Ephemeral Insight

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

Focus your mind to the highest level possible. While equipped, when you use Charge Mind while you already have Charge Mind active, you gain Ephemeral Insight for 2 rounds (your Charge Mind duration also becomes 2 rounds). Ephemeral Insight causes the next spell to deal Akashic damage instead of its original damage type and then ends. When Ephemeral Insight ends, you gain Worn Out LV 10 for 2 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ephemeral_Insight)

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Lightning Critical Increase:** +5/10/15/20/25

Causes lightning to behave more aggressively, increasing your Critical rate if it is triggered by a lightning effect (such as Redgull Elemental Impacts, or Ryemei).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Highwrath)

### Pure Power

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Power per slot:** +10

The truly powerful don't need items. In fact, they just get in the way. For every equipment slot that isn't occupied, you gain a Power bonus to all spells you cast.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Pure_Power)

### Arcane Tattoo

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

A special kind of mage marking on the skin that invokes an incantation when supplied with focus, these tattoos are often found on battle-oriented magicians. This skill will reduce the FP cost of the first equipped Mage or Evoker spell in your Skill Pool by 25% if you are using Unarmored or Light Armor torso items. Unarmored torso items will instead reduce it by 40%.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Arcane_Tattoo)

### Evocation

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Evocation)
