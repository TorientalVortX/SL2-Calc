### Couloir

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 6/7/8/9/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Polearm only

Spear skill. Surprise an enemy with a spear strike from a distance. You perform a basic attack from 3 Range, gaining bonus Hit (based on Rank) due to the swiftness of the strike.

### Crystal Rose

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 6/8/10/12/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** 50/55/60/65/70% Ice ATK + 80/85/90/95/100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (1)
- **Restriction:** Sword only
- **Flags:** Location
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Sword skill. You charge the edge of your weapon with focus and cut in the air, channeling ice magic and causing the air to freeze and shatter, dealing Ice physical damage to all enemies in the area of effect. Freezing variation also has a chance to inflict Frozen LV 20 for 2 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Crystal_Rose)

### Cyclone Spear

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 15/14/13/12/11
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 80/90/100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (3)
- **Restriction:** Polearm only
- **Flags:** Location
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Spear skill. Throw your spear high into the air, where it will fall at a nearby location within 5 Range. Upon landing, it creates a cyclone, dealing Wind damage to all enemies within a 3 Range circle.

All enemies within 5 Range of the spear will be pulled towards it one tile, and all battlefield objects in the damage range will be blown 3 tiles away. If they impact with enemies, they may have special effects.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cyclone_Spear)

### Eclair Lacroix

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 50
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** 130/140/150/160/170% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single (6)
- **Cooldown:** 5 rounds
- **Restriction:** Sword only
- **Flags:** Location

Sword skill. An attack requiring a great amount of focus, you release a golden X-shaped energy projectile that pierces enemies and travels 6 tiles away, which deals Slash magic damage to all enemies it touches. Upon reaching the end of its path, it will grow and explode, dealing Light magic damage to all enemies within a 4 Range Circle of that location.

Cannot be used during the same turn as basic Movement. Enemies hit by the projectile will take 50% damage from the explosion.

#### Excel Crash

LV1 - +10% Damage per Charge Level

LV2 - Tiles the projectile passes over also receive the explosion effect.

LV3 - Ignores Evasion.

Any weapon that uses this skill can consume active Weapon Charges to gain these effects, depending on the amount consumed

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Eclair_Lacroix)

### Lance de Lion

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 50/51/52/53/54
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 130/140/150/160/170% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (2)
- **Cooldown:** 5 rounds
- **Restriction:** Polearm only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Spear skill. An extremely powerful single strike that is unavoidable. Deals physical Pierce damage to all enemies in a 3x3 grid (directly in front of you), but requires an extreme amount of focus, both mentally and literally. - **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 70/80/90/100/110% Scaled WPN ATK

- **Restriction:** Polearm only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Spear skill. Targets 1 enemy within 1 Range and stabs at them in a unique manner, dealing Pierce physical damage. ### Eviter

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Base Chance:** 5/10/15/20/25%

Toggle skill. Parry skill (only one takes effect at a time).

While on, you will begin using one of your melee weapons (sub by default; else main) as a parrying tool. At a chance equal WPN durability (unbreakable = 40) + a bonus, you can parry attacks and skills (excluding spells) from monster, Sword, Axe, and Spear weapons. Success reduces the damage taken by 50% and WPN durability by 1. Protection from Arrows will let you parry Bows (easier at longer range). You can only parry attacks that you can see, and only if the parrying weapon isn't broken.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Eviter)

### Fleur

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

A flowery style that cuts the opponent apart like rose thorns. When you successfully score a Critical Hit with a weapon for the first time in a round, you gain an additional +1 Momentum. (This effect can only trigger on a specific enemy once per round.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fleur)

### Flottement

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Voltiger Bonus Range:** +1/2/3/4/5
- **Voltiger Bonus Damage:** +5/10/15/20/25%

Toggle skill. Evasive reaction skill (only one takes effect at a time).

While on, you will step one tile away from attackers you can see within X Range (X = Rank) who miss you with attacks. Upon triggering, you gain Voltiger LV X (or power up an existing status). Voltiger increases the Range of Sidecut and Sillcut by LV and increases the damage they deal by a percentage.

(Max: 25% bonus damage, 10 duration)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Flottement)

### Riposte

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Activation Chance:** Scaled SKI+0/3/6/9/12%

Accepting attacks and then returning them is a basic form of swordplay. When you are attacked in melee range, there is a chance that you will perform an automatic attack on your attacker (but this attack will deal 50% of its normal damage). This skill will not trigger for attacks made directly behind you.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Riposte)

### One-on-One

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Bonus Damage/Reduction:** 10%

True Duelists excel at just that; dueling. While they are still competent fighters in larger battles, they are better trained against single opponents. While there is only one enemy in the battle, you gain a bonus to all damage you deal. While you are the only living unit in your party, you reduce all damage taken by the same amount.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/One-on-One)

### Poise

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Critical Bonus:** +3/6/9/12/15
- **Damage Threshold:** 10/20/30/40/50

To have poise means to have the upper hand and it requires quick reflexes. When you are attacked, you gain a bonus to your Critical for your next attack within 2 rounds.

If you evaded the attack, the duration is increased by 1 and you gain Perfect Poise, which doubles the bonus and keeps it active for multiple attacks. Perfect Poise will end if you are hit by a basic attack, and both will be reduced in duration and level (1/3 respectively) if you take any damage exceeding a threshold, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Poise)

### Vent Petale

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 3
- **SKI/DEF/FP Reduction:** +3/4/5

A flowery, two-weapon style using both sword and spear. When you have a Sword and Spear equipped at the same time (must be two weapons), you gain a bonus to SKI and DEF, and reduce the FP cost of all Duelist base class skills by 2+Rank. (Min. FP Cost: 0)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Vent_Petale)
