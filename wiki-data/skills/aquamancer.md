### Cold Splash

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 20/22/24/26/28
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 80/90/100/110/120% Ice ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy only
- **Domain:** Aquarian

An extra cold burst of water wraps around an enemy within 5 Range, dealing Ice magic damage to them.

Aquae Crest: If LV 9 or higher, has an infliction-based chance to cause Frozen LV100 for 3 rounds. If successful, consumes 9 LV.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cold_Splash)

### Dancing Water

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10/11/12
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** 100/120/140% Water ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy only
- **Domain:** Aquarian
- **Duration:** 6/7/8 Rounds

Animate water in a tile within 6 Range, creating an attackable Dancing Tentacle there (LV based on duration and Rank).

Aquae Crest: If LV 5 or higher, +2 duration, +20 HP.

Dancing Tentacle

This is a special effect tile created by Aquamancer skills. At the start of a new round, it will attempt to attack an enemy within its attack range (1 Range if not on a Flooded Water or Dark Water tile, 2 Range otherwise), dealing damage to them that ignores evasion equal to its LV (Water damage by default, Dark damage if on Dark Water). It also has a chance to inflict Slow LV 2 (if not on water) or Immobilize (if on water) to the damaged enemy for 2 rounds.

When created, if there are any monsters in its attack range, it will attack one.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Dancing_Water)

### Ring of Pearls

- **Type:** Defensive

- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Self
- **Flags:** Self Only

Surround yourself with an enchanted ring of magical pearls for 5 rounds. ### Call Storm

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 30
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy
- **Domain:** Nature
- **Water ATK Increase:** +2/4/6/8/10

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation.

Creates a localized Rainstorm on the battlefield for 5 rounds. If your Rainstorm is already active, its duration is extended by 5 rounds, and it gains additional effects.

Aquae Crest: If Invoking the skill and LV 6 or higher, consumes 6 levels and refunds 3 Momentum.

Rainstorm

This is a weather-based battlefield effect. While active, Flooded Water tiles do not lose duration when a new round occurs, and allies of the caster gain Water ATK. In addition, Aquamancer Support spells that target an ally will affect all allies instead (applies to all Aquamancers - not just the caster). The Rainstorm can also be powered up twice by casting the spell again:

- When powered up for the first time, it will cause lightning to strike a random enemy at the start of a new round. This lightning strike deals Lightning magic damage that ignores protection equal to the caster's Lightning ATK.
- When powered up for the second time, it will cause violent winds to kick up, causing the caster to automatically cast Vyd on a random enemy for free at the start of a new round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Call_Storm)

### Aquae Crest

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

A magical crest of governing water. The crest fills with magical energy by casting Aquamancer spells, increasing its LV by 1 (max. 9). Aquamancer skills interact with the Aquae Crest by gaining bonus effects if its LV is high enough, sometimes by consuming some of its levels.

Your Water ATK is increased by the crest's LV.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Aquae_Crest)

### Aquae Mastery

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Starting LV:** 1/2/3

A true display of aquatic mastery. If Aquae Crest is equipped, you begin the battle with Aquae Crest levels equal to Rank. If Aquamancer is both your main class and sub class, you start with double the amount instead.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Aquae_Mastery)

- **Max rank:** 2

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Healing_Aqua)

### Servant of a Dark Authority

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

The Aquamancer is not necessarily a force of purity; sometimes, they may worship or serve something 'darker'. While equipped, changes the functionality of most Aquamancer spells:

- Instead of Flooded Water, creates delayed Dark Water tiles.
- Depending on the spell, Water damage may change to Dark damage or Dark/Water split damage. (Elemental ATK scaling unchanged.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Servant_of_a_Dark_Authority)
