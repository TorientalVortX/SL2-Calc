## Skills

### Bellowing Stag

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/13/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 50% Dark ATK + 100/110/120% Scaled Weapon ATK + 15% Rage Energy
- **Target:** Circle (2)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Matador stance. Requires at least 10 Rage Energy and consumes 15% of your total Rage Energy on use. You let out a mighty bellow, inflicting Dark magic damage based on Rank + Rage Energy consumed to all enemies in a 2 Range circle. If a damaged enemy is Airborne, they are knocked away 3 tiles and knocked down by the roar.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bellowing_Stag)

### Chaser

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/13/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled Weapon ATK
- **Target:** Line (2-5)
- **Restriction:** Sword only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Sword skill. Targets a 5 Range line in a cardinal direction from you and you move along it. The first enemy hit takes weapon-based damage, and on-hit effects are applied to them.

If used while Airborne, movement will continue until you hit an Airborne enemy (you still damage enemies). Airborne enemies are knocked back by the full range of the skill and knocked down.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Chaser)

### Crashing Bull

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/13/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 50% Dark ATK + 100/110/120% Scaled Weapon ATK
- **Target:** Circle (2)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Matador stance. Requires 15 Rage Energy. Can only be used while Airborne. You come crashing down to the ground, dealing Dark magic damage to all enemies within a 2 Range Circle, as well as launching them Airborne.

If you are Grappling an enemy: You throw that enemy to the ground insteaad, increasing damage to all enemies by 15%, and damage the Grappled target takes ignores evasion.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Crashing_Bull)

### Desperado Stance

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only

The Desperado is a man with nothing to lose, whose life and death is determined by how quick he draws his gun. They masterfully manipulate the art of gunplay and can take on large quantities of enemies effortlessly.

When you use this skill or a Desperado stance skill, your active stance becomes Desperado. Desperado stance increases your Hit by 5.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Desperado_Stance)

### Double Down

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/13/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Sword only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Wear Down LV:** 3/4/5

Sword skill. Can only be used while Airborne. Swing down your weapon on a foe in a downward arc, using your fall to build up momentum. Performs a basic attack on a target within 1 Range; if it hits, inflicts Wear Down LV X (X = 2 + Rank) for 2 Rounds (3 Rounds on critical hit). If the target is Airborne, it also has a chance to inflict Knocked Down.

This skill will bring you and the target down to the ground, ending Airborne.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Double_Down)

### Elemental Rave

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/13/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled Weapon ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Sword only only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Enchant Augmentable:** Can be empowered with Enchants.

Sword skill. Airborne Compatible. Deals physical Slash damage over 4 hits to one enemy within 1 Range.

If you and your target are both Airborne, the damage becomes magic damage and alternates between Fire, Ice, Wind and Lightning, and is increased by 10% of those respective ATKs.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Elemental_Rave)

- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled Weapon ATK
- **Target:** Circle (3)

### Nitrogen Drop

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 16/13/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/115/130% Scaled Weapon ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 2 Rounds
- **Restriction:** Gun only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Desperado stance. Unleash a volatile bullet as a projectile in a line that will travel up to your Gun's attack range away. Upon contacting an enemy or at the end of its range, the bullet will burst into a massive explosion, dealing Blunt physical damage that ignores evasion. All damaged enemies are also launched Airborne. If you are in the explosion radius, you also become Airborne.

### Retaliate

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 25
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1-10
- **Power:** 100% Rage Energy
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Matador stance. Give one enemy cabrone within 1 Range the horns, dealing unresistable Dark damage to them equal to your accumulated Rage Energy. Afterwards, your Rage Energy resets to 0.

Gains 1 Range per 10 Rage Energy available, to a max of 10 Range. Using this skill on an enemy outside of 1 Range will spend 5 Rage Energy (per tile) to move you next to them, before triggering its effect.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Retaliate)

- **Power:** --

### Matador Stance

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 0
- **Momentum:** 1
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Self Only

The Matador is a wild berserker more like a bull than a tamer, storing Rage Energy from damage they take and unleashing it back on their enemies.

When you use this skill or a Matador stance skill, your active stance becomes Matador. Matador stance increases Armor/Magic Armor by 2, and 30% of damage you take is stored as Rage Energy.

#### Rage Energy

This is a special resource generated for the Demon Hunter class, while in the Matador stance. Certain Matador skills will interact with it, or require a certain amount to use.

Rage Energy expires when battle ends, and if you are not in Matador Stance when a new round starts, you lose 5.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Matador_Stance)

Cobra Stance. Can only be used while Airborne. Targets 2 tiles in 3 Range; in Location 1, there can be an airborne enemy (optional), and Location 2 must be an unoccupied tile. You move towards Location 2, landing and regaining FP based on Rank.

### Bonus Bloodshed

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Flags:** Main class skill
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Turn slashing into style. At the start of a new round (excluding the start of battle), if you are in Reaver stance and do not have an elemental effect active, you gain a 1 round Elemental status based on your Starsign. (Nerhaven, if none of that element doesn't have an enchantment.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bonus_Bloodshed)

### Bullet Barrier

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Parry skill. The ultimate counter to bullets will always be more bullets; swords be damned. While you are in Desperado Stance and have a Gun equipped, you gain a chance to parry Gun and Bow weapons (skills & attacks, excluding spells). (25% + half of Scaled GUI chance to activate, 50% damage reduction.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bullet_Barrier)

### Clay Pidgeon

Make your enemies into little more than targets to shoot. While you are in Desperado Stance:

- Gun attacks against enemies who are Airborne deal extra damage.
- Shotgun basic attacks will knock down enemies in 1 Range if they are Airborne or on critical hit.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Clay_Pidgeon)

### Combination Fighter

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Make a masterful dance of blows that leave your enemies bewildered and jealous. Toggle skill. While toggled on, using Demon Hunter Offensive skills (excluding skills with a base Momentum cost of 1, Retaliate, and Wild Ride) or a Soldier Offensive skill reduces the Momentum and FP cost of all skills of those types that you have not used this round by 1, but reduces their damage by 25%. This effect can stack up to 2 times (to a minimum of 1 Momentum and a maximum penalty of 50%.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Combination_Fighter)

### Danger Sense

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Instantly tell where the enemy is hitting you. While you are in Cobra Stance, if you take damage from an enemy you are not facing, you turn to face them immediately afterwards.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Danger_Sense)

### Goring Horns

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **Cursed Wounds::** 1/2/3 Rounds

Leave a mark your foes won't forget. Enemies damaged by Matador skills have their maximum HP reduced for X Rounds (based on Rank). This effect can stack infinitely, but maximum reduction per damage instance is capped at 50 (excluding Retaliate, which has no cap).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Goring_Horns)

### Know No Pain

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Block blows with your forearms, letting your enemies know you mean business. While you are in Matador Stance, you gain a chance to parry monster weapons & non-ranged weapons (skills & attacks, excluding spells) (as long as you are facing the attacker). (25% + half of Scaled DEF chance to activate, 50% damage reduction.) Successful activation generates 15 Rage Energy.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Know_No_Pain)

### Martial Lawbreaker

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** This skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

The rules are so boring. Toggle skill. While toggled on and you are in Reaver Stance, all Demon Hunter Sword skills, and all Soldier Offensive skills will use your Main Weapon, whatever that may be, instead of its intended weapon type. (For example, you could use Thousand Stab with a Sword, or Roundtrip with a Spear, etc.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Martial_Lawbreaker)

### Cobra Stance

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Become a blur, switching between multiple stances with beautiful ease. All Stance changing skills become Airborne Compatible, and their first use in a Round will restore 1 Momentum.

When Auto-Stance Swapping, you gain a stacking buff for 1 Round that increases Scaled Weapon Power by 2.

### Coyote Pack

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Unleash the wild cries of the swift hunters of the night; coyotes hunt in packs, after all.

While you are using two Shotgun weapons, you gain the ability to Twin Dance with them, even at range. If you have the Akimbo skill active, you can also gain the effect while wielding a Shotgun and a Handgun.
