## Skills

### Axe Kick

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Hare Discipline technique. Targets 1 enemy within 1 Range and deals physical Blunt damage, plus bonus Ice magic damage if Ice Point Guard is active (50% Ice ATK).

If you are airborne and have Ice Point Guard, the target is knocked down and knocked away 1 tile. The Ice Point Guard is then deployed in front of you.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Axe_Kick)

### Cold Front

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 120/130/140% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Bear Discipline technique. Targets an enemy in 1 Range, or a tile in 1 Range (if you have Ice Point Guard, or one exists in it), launching it up up to X tiles (2 + Rank) or until it encounters an enemy. The enemy hit by this skill takes Blunt damage and is knocked back based on how close they are when hit.

Bonus effects may occur while Ice Point Greaves, or if an enemy collides into an Ice Point.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Cold_Front)

### Crawling Spikes

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 35/33/31
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Ice ATK, 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (5)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Fox Discipline technique. Targets a line; spikes travel along that line. If it encounters an Ice Point, it will move in another line to another Ice Point within 5 Range, or an enemy within 5 Range.

*Tiles crossed are covered in Ice Sheets (2 rounds).

*Enemies hit take Ice magic damage (+50% if hit more than once, to a max of +200%).

*Can jump a maximum of 5 times.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Crawling_Spikes)

### Expanding Ice

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 35/33/31
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** Self
- **Power:** 80/90/100% Ice ATK, 80/90/100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location

Fox Discipline technique. Targets all areas in a cardinal direction from any Ice Sheets you or allies have created. Any enemies within the affected area take Ice magic damage. (Enemies cannot be damaged more than once). Furthermore, all affected areas are covered in Ice Sheets for 3 rounds, and the duration of your existing Ice Sheets becomes 3 rounds.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Expanding_Ice)

### Face Stomp

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** 140/160/180% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Hare Discipline technique. Targets 1 enemy within 3 Range that is Knocked Down and has an unoccupied tile next to them, and deals Blunt physical damage to them.

If Ice Point Guard is active, it is deployed to the target's tile. Otherwise, the target is cured of Knocked Down (without paying Momentum).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Face_Stomp)

### Icicle Spear

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 35/33/31
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Ice ATK + 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Fox Discipline technique. Targets 2 locations; Location 1 must contain an Ice Point or Ice Point Guard (or you with IPG active), Location 2 must be 1 tile adjacent to that location.

Transform the target Ice Point into a spear of ice that travels up to 5 tiles (tiles with Ice Sheets do not count), dealing Ice magic damage to all enemies it encounters and may inflict Frostbite LV25 for 3 rounds.

If the target is an Ice Point Guard, travels infinitely, the skill's damage is increased by 50%, but stops upon hitting one enemy, and inflicts them with Frozen LV 200 for one round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Icicle_Spear)

- **Target:** Single

### Rapid Kick

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 15/16/17
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Hare Discipline technique. Targets an enemy in 1 Range, or a tile in 1 Range (if you or that tile have Ice Point Guard) and kicks it X times (X = 3+Rank).

If the target is an enemy, it deals Blunt physical damage. Each kick applies half of Ice Point Greaves' damage. Otherwise, each kick launches three projectiles that deal Ice magic damage which ignore armor (50% Ice atk + bonus kick damage) and puts the skill on a 3 round CD. Reduces the duration of the Ice Point Guard it targets by 1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Rapid_Kick)

### Ice Point Guard

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location

Targets an Ice Point or Ice Point Guard within 5 Range and turns it into a shield of ice, granting several bonuses (5 rounds):

*30+(Scaled SKI/2)% chance to parry martial weapon attacks/skills (30% reduction).

*Can use Guard.

*Can enhance certain Verglas skills.

*Using this skill while you have 4 Momentum or less grants Guard LV 30 for 1 round.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ice_Point_Guard)

### Ice Skate

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Knockdown Distance:** 5/4/3

Must target an ice sheet in 1 Range. You then skate along the ice, stopping only when you fail to move or encounter a tile without an ice sheet on it, knocking aside all battle participants in your way, friend or foe. If you have skated for at least X (based on Rank) tiles, enemies knocked away will also be knocked down.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ice_Skate)

### Insulate

- **Type:** Support
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/15/10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Ally or Self Only
- **Ice Resistance:** +10/20/30%

Targets 1 ally within 3 Range and protects them from the harshness of the cold, increasing their Ice Resistance (based on rank) for 3 rounds. For the same duration, they do not trigger any Ice Sheets by walking on them. It will also cure Frostbite and provide immunity to it for the duration.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Insulate)

### Ice Point

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 4
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location

Create an Ice Point (3 rounds) at target location in 4 Range. Enemies at that location are pushed back away from it and take Ice magic damage equal to your Ice ATK. Afterwards, all tiles within 1 Range are covered in Ice Sheets (3 rounds).

If there is already an Ice Sheet at that location and no enemy is hit, Ice Sheets spawn in 3 Range instead and 2 Momentum is refunded.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ice_Point)

### Ice Point Greaves

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 1
- **FP cost:** 10
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Ice Point
- **Flags:** Location

Targets an Ice Point within 5 Range, leaps to it, and turns it into a pair of icey boots (5 rounds). For the duration, the user does not trigger Ice Sheets, leaves behind Ice Sheets for 2 rounds while moving, and deals bonus Ice magic damage (50% of Ice ATK) that ignores armor (based on Rank) when using a Verglas skill that kicks, or the Kick maneuver.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Ice_Point_Greaves)

### Winter's Bite

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 4
- **FP cost:** 30/29/28/27
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 3
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 8
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Rank 1:** Negates: Resistance
- **Rank 2:** Negates: Immunity
- **Rank 3:** Negates: Absorb
- **Rank 4:** Negates: Reflect

Targets an enemy within 3 Range. For 3 rounds, any properties that grant certain effects against Ice damage are negated. The types negated are based on Rank. In addition, the target takes additional Ice damage equal to 1+Rank for the duration.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Winter's_Bite)

### Brain Freeze

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

The icy chill of your movements locks down your enemies. When you evade a basic attack, a random skill the attacker has equipped is put on a 1 round cooldown (or 2 rounds if you have an Ice Point Guard active).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Brain_Freeze)

### Chimera Style

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 1

When you use a Verglas discipline skill, the Momentum cost of all skills of a different discipline are reduced by 1 for the rest of the round. (You can only gain this effect once per round.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Chimera_Style)

### Bear's Might

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

The discipline of the bear teaches that there is little one can do against overwhelming power. While in Bear Stance, when you deal Ice damage, you gain a stacking status that grants +5 Power and +5 Critical Damage (up to +30) for 10 rounds. (This status loses 5 LV every round you are not in Bear Stance. When it reaches 0, it ends.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Bear's_Might)

### Fox's Cunning

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

The discipline of the Fox teaches that knowledge can act as a wedge. While in Fox Stance, when you deal Ice damage, you gain a stacking status that grants +5 Ice ATK (up to +30) for 10 rounds. (This status loses 5 LV every round you are not in Fox Stance. When it reaches 0, it ends.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Fox's_Cunning)

### Hare's Agility

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

The discipline of the Hare teaches that not every battle is worth fighting. While in Hare Stance, when you deal Ice damage, you gain a stacking status that grants +5 Evade (up to +30) for 10 rounds. (This status loses 5 LV every round you are not in Hare Stance. When it reaches 0, it ends.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Hare's_Agility)

### Permafrost

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Duration Increase:** +1/2/3/4/5

A type of mastery that increases the quality of ice created through spirituality. Increases the duration of all Ice Point skills by Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Permafrost)
