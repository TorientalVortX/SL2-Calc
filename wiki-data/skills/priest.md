## Skills

### Divine Judgement

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 40/35/30/25/20
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** 6
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Mercalan

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation (see the Invocation talent for more details).

Targets 1 enemy within 8 Range. For 5 rounds, that enemy's Faith is used instead of Defense/Resistance when calculating Phys/Magic Def for Mercalan domain spells respectively. In addition, the target's Light resistance is also negated, they become vulnerable to Antithesis, Blessed and Holy enchants, and lost elemental ATK equal to LV * 2. This effect cannot be dispelled.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Divine_Judgement)

### Divine Shower

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 50/48/46/44/42
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** All
- **Power:** 100/120/140/160/180% Light ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Mercalan

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation (see the Invocation talent for more details).

Targets all enemies on the battlefield and deals Light magic damage that ignores evasion to them equal to WIL + a bonus based on rank. At Rank 5, all enemies damage also have a chance to be Blinded for 2 rounds. After being successfully casts, goes on a 3 round Cooldown.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Divine_Shower)

### Gentle Torrent

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 30/29/28/27/26
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 7
- **Power:** 80/92/104/116/128% Water ATK, 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3 turns
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Mercalan

Sends out a bubble of water as a projectile in a 7 Range line, which washes over enemies and allies alike. If it passes over an ally, all negative status effects the ally has are reduced by 1 round in duration. If it hits an enemy, they take Water magic damage, and the projectile will stop.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Gentle_Torrent)

- **Max rank:** 5

- **Momentum:** 3

- **Power:** 100% Scaled WPN ATK, 50/55/60/65/70% Light/Lightning ATK

### Mercalan Mist

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 60/58/56/54/52
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Range:** All
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Self
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Mercalan
- **Regen (min):** +2/4/6/8/10
- **Evade:** +5/10/15/20/25

This skill requires a Rank D Invocation (see the Invocation talent for more details).

Mist skill. Upon being cast, the battlefield is covered in a light blue mist for 5 rounds, which grants all allies bonus HP regeneration equal to 25% of your Light ATK (or a Rank-based minimum, whichever is higher) and increases their Evade (based on Rank). Enemies gain a 3% weakness to Lightning damage (per Rank) while the mist is active. (Only one Mist skill can be active on the battlefield at a time; the last one cast will take priority and nullify previous ones.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Mercalan_Mist)

### Needle

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 35/34/33/32/31
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 5/6/7/8/9
- **Power:** 8/16/24/32/40% Light ATK per hit
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 3 turns
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Mercalan

Unleashes a barrage of needles as projectiles in a line (4+Rank Range); one projectile per Rank. Each projectile that hits an enemy pierces them with needles and deals Light damage that ignores armor (based on Rank). The target remains pierced by the needles for the rest of the round, and if they take Light damage from any source except Needle, the needles will explode, dealing Light damage to the target equal to half the original amount, and releasing a healing pulse that recovers the HP of any ally within 2 Range by the same amount.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Needle)

### Shine Knights

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 35
- **Momentum:** 6
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Mercalan
- **Level:** 20/30/40/50/60
- **Rank 5:** Summon 2 Shine Knights instead of 1.

This skill requires a Rank C Invocation (see the Invocation talent for more details).

Summons a Shine Knight is summoned next to the caster. At Rank 1, they have the Protect skill. At Rank 2, they boost stats for nearby allies. At Rank 3, they reduce the Hit of nearby enemies. At Rank 4, basic attacks receive damage and range. (Shine Knights last until defeated.)

Rank 1: LV:20

Rank 2: LV: 30, Gains immunity to Dark/Light

Rank 3: LV: 40, gains Blinding aura.

Rank 4: LV: 50, Alondite weapon upgrade.

Rank 5: LV: 60, summons 2 Shine Knights instead of 1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Shine_Knights)

### Sanctuary

- **Type:** Utility
- **Max rank:** 5
- **FP cost:** 20/19/18/17/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 2-5
- **Target:** Area, Self
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Domain:** Mercalan
- **Stat LV:** 2/4/6/8/10

Creates a Sanctuary in a 2-5 Range circle around your current location. At the start of a new round, if you are not within the Sanctuary, it is dispelled. While active, all allies within it receive a bonus to DEF and RES, and all enemies within it receive a penalty to STR and WIL. In addition, special effect tiles cannot be created within the Sanctuary's confines. (The Sanctuary will be last for 5 rounds or until dispelled. Casting this spell again will remove the first Sanctuary.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sanctuary)

### Blackened Faith

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2

Sometimes, we put our faith in unorthodox sources. For priest spells, spells that utilize Light ATK and deal light damage are changed to use Dark ATK and deal Dark damage respectively (there is no change to the scaling values). Some invocation may be altered in similar way if they deal with Light ATK or light damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Blackened_Faith)

### Detailed Care

- **Type:** Passive
- **Range:** 1
- **Increased Effect:** 10/20/30/40/50%

Being up close and personal with someone you're treating often allows for a better level of care. When you are within 1 Range of a target you are using a Healing or Treatment skill on (Curate and Priest only), the power of the heal or cure is increased by 10% per Rank. (This does not apply when you are the target. This will affect the maximum LV healed by Treatment skills.)

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Detailed_Care)

### Devocation

- **Type:** Passive
- **Max rank:** 2
- **Rank 2:** Equipping this skill does not take up a Skill Pool slot.

Let the power of faith see you through. While this skill is equipped, whenever you are invoking a Mercana spell, any chance for the Invocation to break upon taking damage is reduced by half of your Scaled FAI.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Devocation)

### Longterm Cure

- **Type:** Passive
- **Duration:** 1/2/3/4/5

Treating an illness is important, but it's also just as important to prevent it from happening again. When you use a Treatment skill on an ally (except yourself), they also become immune to the status type(s) it cures for X rounds (X = Rank). At Rank 5, when you use a Treatment skill on an ally or yourself, you also become immune to the same status type(s) for half the normal duration (rounded down).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Longterm_Cure)

### God Rod

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

A staff is a common symbol attributed to priests and bishops, and through it, they channel their holy power. Lets Staff weapons be used as casting tools for Mercalan domain spells.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/God_Rod)

### Mercalan Tenants

- **Type:** Innate
- **FP Recovery:** +3/6/9/12/15

The way of Mercala teaches that healing magic should not be abused, because mankind would come to take it for granted, spurring on more aggressive or reckless pursuits. As such, her followers are taught that it is best to treat with non-magical methods when possible, and to use the magic only in emergencies or situations that do not act against this desire. When you use a healing item (except a Staff) on an ally, you regain FP, based on Rank.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Mercalan_Tenets)

### Piety

- **Type:** Innate
- **Faith:** +1/2/3/4/6

A good priest isn't good nor a priest without faith guiding them. Increases FAI based Rank. At Rank 5, FAI is increased by an additional +1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Piety)

### Staff Mastery

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

Utilizing staves gives the Priest a unique source of power, which causes their WIL and FAI to increase by 1 for every Staff Battle Item they have equipped in their Item Belt.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Staff_Mastery)
