## Skills

### Asura Fist

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/21/22
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Darkness ATK + 100% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Line (4)
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Location
- **Domain:** Huggessoan
- **Schwarz Sturm Generation:** +1

Unleashes a blow of darkness in a 4 Range line. The projectile will deal Dark magic damage to the first enemy hit, and will increase your Schwarz Sturm level by 1 on use. Fist weapons are treated as valid casting tools for this spell.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Asura_Fist)

### Felhook

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 18/16/14
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 110/120/130% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Cooldown:** 1 Round
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Schwarz Sturm Generation:** +1

Fist skill. A weighty hook punch that sets the target off-balance.

Deals Blunt Damage to the target and inflicts them with Flatfoot. If you are Swaying, this skill can critically hit (25% damage).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Felhook)

### Felsmanege

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Earth ATK + 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Circle (3)
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Location

Finisher (resets Schwarz Sturm to 0 when used). Deal a crushing blow to the ground in a 3 Range circle around you, dealing Earth magic damage to all enemies inside of it.

It will also create pillars of rock at the edge of the circle, which last for rounds equal to your Schwarz Sturm level.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Felsmanege)

### Grandupper

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 42/38/34
- **Momentum:** 4
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Flags:** Enemy Only

Finisher (resets Schwarz Sturm to 0 when used). The strongest punch a Boxer has. Deals Blunt damage to one enemy within 1 Range. If your Schwarz Sturm level is 8 or higher, an additional effect occurs:

If the target is a non-Player Character and non-boss, it is instantly reduced to 0 HP. If they are, they are knocked into the air and removed from the battle for 1 turn; afterwards, they land in the battle again, and take Blunt damage equal to half of the original damage.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Grandupper)

### Sturm Shreik

- **Type:** Offensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 24/22/20
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** 100/110/120% Scaled WPN ATK
- **Target:** Single
- **Restriction:** Fist only
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Schwarz Sturm Generation:** +1

Fist Skill. Deals a hard blow to the gut of an enemy within 1 Range, inflicting Blunt damage and reducing their FP by your Schwarz Sturm level. It can also inflict Silence for 2 rounds at a rate of 30% of your normal Status Infliction, plus 10% per Schwarz Sturm level. If the target is Flatfooted, this skill can critically hit (25% damage).

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Sturm_Shreik)

### Geist Schritt (Eins)

- **Type:** Defensive
- **Max rank:** 3
- **FP cost:** 20/18/16
- **Momentum:** 3
- **Range:** 1
- **Power:** --
- **Target:** Line (3)
- **Flags:** Enemy Only
- **Schwarz Sturm Generation:** +1
- **Follow-Up Dash:** 3/4/5 tiles

A common type of dash used by close-range fighters. You dash in a 3-6 Range line that must end in an unoccupied tile. Only 1 Geist Schritt skill can be used per turn.

Eins style. The dash also attempts to bait out basic attack skills; if you are targeted by one, it is negated, and the follow-up dash activates, moving you towards the attacker, restoring 3 Momentum and increases your Schwarz Sturm level by 1.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Geist_Schritt_(Eins))

### On The Ropes

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1
- **Flags:** Main class skill

True Boxers will take every advantage they can get when the enemy has their back to the wall.

While not at the edge of the battlefield, any enemy who is, or has a dense object behind them, takes 15% extra damage from you.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/On_The_Ropes)

### Schwarz Sturm

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 5
- **Schwarz Sturm Limit:** 4/5/6/7/8

The Boxer is an entity that relies on momentum and prediction, to which they have a unique, authentic quality. Once a Boxer gets started, you need to stop them as soon as possible, or things will just get worse.

Schwarz Sturm is a gauge that will increase when using most Boxer Skills, up to a maximum. Using a non-Boxer skill, even ones like Movement (except End Turn), will reset it to 0. Boosts Boxer skill power by 5% per level, and some skills will change based on it.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Schwarz_Sturm)

### Wind Weave

- **Type:** Innate
- **Max rank:** 1

When you fight up close and personal all the time, it's important to be able to move quickly, especially if you're on the offensive.

Increases your Evade by your Schwarz Sturm level * 2.

[Wiki page](https://sl2.miraheze.org/wiki/Wind_Weave)
