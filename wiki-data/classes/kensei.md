## Wiki notes

Oniga is famed for its swordsmanship, and true masters of the art have even earned the title "Kensei" from outside nations. They are described as an almost supernatural force, moving faster than eyesight, and capable of thinking several steps ahead of their opponents.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Kensei's Eviter, Riposte, Counter Edge, Sheath Sword, Blade Barrier, and Reflect Shots culminate to make their front-facing lethal beyond compare.
- Kensei's innate skills provide several statistical buffs and debuffs that add up quickly.
- Can nullify bullets with Blade Barrier and even reflect them back with Reflect Shots.
- Kensei Combos provide a wide variety of useful effects.
- Innately high Critical with katana-subtype weapons.

### Drawbacks

- Requires Katana-subtype weapons to be effective, limiting weapon choice.
- Limited defensive options when struck from the sides or behind.
- Very high FP costs.
- Becomes significantly weaker against a target with high Critical Evade, as with most other Duelist promotions.

### Tips

- Once the effects of Katana Master are lost, they cannot be regained in a fight.
- Multi-hit strikes into your front or sides can provoke a flurry of Ripostes, dealing very large amounts of damage.
- Being enchanted with Talvyd or equipping the applicable badge extends the range of your abilities significantly. Kidnap isolated opponents with an enhanced Hirazuki!
- End your turns facing where you think an opponent will try to attack you. Sheath Sword and Eviter can be difficult to use, but your hardest hit is the former's counterattack - plus bonus Riposte.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Duelist Class.

- Win any battle while you are equipped with a Katana-subtype sword in your main hand and nothing equipped in your torso (or Unarmored type armor).
