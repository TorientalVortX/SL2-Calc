## Wiki notes

After coming back from a near-death experience, some Duelists take up the mantle of Ghost. These fighters draw on supernatural endurance and tenacity to harrow their foes, and only become more dangerous and ferocious as their injuries mount.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Claret Call is a Ghost's bread and butter, steadily (or rapidly) increasing the physical damage they deal to a target.
- Ghosts becomes stronger and more dangerous to fight against the less HP they have due to Rising Game, and when really low on health, Ether Invitation.
- Wraithguard, Painproof and Rebound provide major survivability.
- Haunting is a powerful mobility tool with a 1-turn cooldown when a ghost is under 50% health.
- Ghost's offensive spells, Scarlet Twister and Blood Spike, do very large amounts of damage without the restrictions of a conventional casting tool.
- Scarlet Twister turns from minor grouping tool to massive displacement zone depending on how low a ghost's health is.

### Drawbacks

- Despite their high damage and utility, some of Ghost's strongest abilities consume chunks of HP to cast.
- Both Wraithguard and Fleur are main-class only, limiting class choices for non-caster Ghosts.
- Stacking Claret Call without Fitting Form can be very difficult, encouraging the class to avoid heavier and stronger armor.

### Tips

- Wraithguard counts as an unit for your team, disabling the effects of One-on-One.
- Failing to finish off a target with Ether Invitation will reset your Claret Call stacks on ALL targets and prevent the survivor from gaining them for a few turns.
- Death Gaze is an evade-ignoring, Status Resistance-ignoring silence with reasonable damage. Fulfilling the conditions with L'au-Dela, Haunting near a target with Claret Call, or a weapon enchanted with haunted soul makes it a potent tool.
- Ghost is the only class without statistical bonuses for equipping it. Build accordingly.
- Painproof can turn even the most sickly dodge build into a tank for a brief period, especially if spirited - but only against targets marked by their Claret Call.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Duelist Class.

- Win any battle with your HP under 10% (When your health bar becomes red). Healing from food occurs before the unlock condition.

or

- Have the History Trait - Ghost and win any battle.
