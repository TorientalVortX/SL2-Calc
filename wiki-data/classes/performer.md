## Wiki notes

Life's a show, and you're the main player. That's what all the rest of those talentless hacks think, right? But you, the Performer, are the real king. With a lute in your hand, you can make even the most stuck-up wallflower hit the dance floor like it owes them money.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Supports allies with a plethora of enhanced buffs, cleanses, and heals.
- Inflicts enemies with debilitating debuffs and can directly lower a target's Status Resistance.
- Unique passive that increases EXP gained, Murai found, and the chance to find items by 10%.
- Pied Piper.

### Drawbacks

- Limited damage output.
- Reasonably high FP costs, with no way to regain it on its own.
- Limited to instrument weapons.
- Oddly high weapon durability consumption.

### Tips

- Song of Susceptibility reduces a target's Status Resistance without a check against it. Use it on targets you or your allies would have difficulty inflicting.
- Pied Piper applies confusion and a level 40 charm to all enemies in range. Use it to greatly reduce the damage enemies deal to you and make them hit each other. Also affected by Volume Up!
- Dawr of Defense applies Swift Guard when used by a Performer. Don't underestimate how much damage it can reduce on its own.
- Rain-Caller Rondo applies to all enemies when the battlefield is affected by Aquamancer's Call Storm.
- Use Bloody Ball in conjunction with Pied Piper to create your own mosh pit. No Dark Bard required.
- Every Song skill you use consumes weapon durability. Be mindful of how your weapon is doing in longer fights.
- Beat it lets you flee from any non-boss monster fight.
- Luck increases your chances of receiving more cleanses from Cantus of Consideration.

### Unlocking:

Use all 5 Bard songs in a single fight as a level 20+ Bard.
