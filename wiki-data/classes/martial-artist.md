## Wiki notes

Martial Artists are skilled in hand to hand combat. Likewise, their main power comes from a wide array and understanding of combat techniques. Martial Artists are disciplined and intelligent, believing that a sharp mind wins in battle over a sharp blade.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Puts Fist weapons to great use, including your bare hands.
- Martial artists have a significant freedom of movement compared to most other base classes, with skills such as Heaven Kick or Crane Hop to move around with, or skills that enhance your movement such as Shukuchi.
- They can recover themselves with Meditate and Cleanse Body.
- Offsets the drawbacks of Unarmored Torsos with extra damage reduction, and enhances them with extra Evade.
- Martial Artists can entirely ignore the main drawback of the Knocked Down status with Kip-Up, and cleanse the effects of Immobilize and Slows with Cleanse Body. This makes them very difficult to control in a fight.

### Drawbacks

- Martial Artists will find themselves running out of FP quickly, especially with higher ranked skills.
- Extra damage reduction doesn't mean you're invincible either, with the low armor stat of most Unarmored torsos, Martial Artists can die very quickly when caught out of position.
- Many of it's promotion classes define each of the Martial Artists with much better tools than the base class, making this class a poor pick for a Normalcy playstyle.

### Tips

- Meditate can be used to extend statuses for an additional round. Try using Shukuchi before Meditate if you have the FP to spare to carry it over into the next round without spending additional momentum.
- Geldoren is effective at cracking open sturdier targets and fearing them, which reduces their hit rate.
- Crane Hop is most effective when paired with effects that would grant you an additional point of momentum, such as critically hitting with a basic attack, or hitting a weakness. Used this way it can make Crane Hop your best way of moving around.
- Mixed Martial Arts allows you to equip a weapon in your main hand that isn't a fist weapon and still utilize your Martial Artist skills, and even utilize talents or traits not normally allowed while Dual Wielding such as Two-Hand.

### Promotions

Class LV20+ Martial Artists can promote to:

- Monk
- Verglas
- Boxer
