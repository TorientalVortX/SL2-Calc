## Wiki notes

With a brief incantation and a slight flick of the wrist, a proper Mage can invoke destruction. Charged with arcane forces, these individuals can control and manipulate the battlefield like a conductor leads an orchestra.

Magic has its roots everywhere, but nowadays, Karaten and its Mage's Guilds are the most well known practitioners.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Mages have a large variety of offensive, supportive and utility spells to choose from, making them quite versatile.
- Their accessibility to various elements can allow them to exploit enemy Elemental Weaknesses.
- Blink is a solid movement option, as it ignores disabling status effects that cancel movement skills and allows you to move while casting an Invocation.
- Whispering Cant allows a mage to continue casting most of their spells even when Silenced.

### Drawbacks

- Casting spells require considerable amounts of FP, and the class lacks the means to recover it on its own.
- No source of physical damage.

### Tips

- Several Mage spells can consume excel charges, changing them in powerful ways.
- Elemental enchants such as Nerhaven have drawbacks. Try to cover the increased elemental damage taken with resistance to that same element.
- Using the Astral Gravity effect of Astral Belt will knock enemies down if you're also enchanted by Relent Gale. Allies can do it too if you give it to them!
- Elemental Augment does wonder when paired with a high elemental attack and Twin Dance or Akimbo.
- Swap Position is valuable to your teammates. Consider packing it if you have no other spells you'd like.

### Promotions

Class LV20+ Mages can promote to:

- Evoker
- Hexer
- Rune Magician
- Ruler
