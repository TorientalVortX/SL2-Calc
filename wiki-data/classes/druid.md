## Wiki notes

Mysterious and elusive, Druids wield natural magics to preserve and protect nature from unnatural threats. The manner in which they perform this greatly varies; some caretakers peacefully heal forests harmed by magic, while others are rumored to punish those who carelessly strip the land of resources, even if it means burying civilizations in sand.

Much like nature, they are not always kind, nor entirely merciless - and never to be underestimated.

-In-Game Description.

### Strengths

- Very strong summons with very little cost.
- Great synergy with basic attackers and Duelist promotions.
- Defensive options in Devil Vines and Listblume.
- Support potential from Curate base class and skills like Fulgur of Flight.
- Large statistical boons in Wild Shape skills, certain Druid Plants, and Curate's Purity Edge.
- Can use any wooden weapon as a casting tool for Nature, Isespian, and Sylphid spells courtesy of Nature's Conduit.

### Drawbacks

- Spells cannot be cast while wild shaped.
- Certain Druid Plants and spells apply harmful debuffs to allies or outright damage them.
- Druid Plants often require significant time and momentum investment.

### Tips

- Mortefiore will still apply to a target if the infliction check fails, and will do so with a longer duration.
- Animal Companion call skills can have different ranges and effects than their regular counterparts. Wolf Howl (Call), for example, can be cast at range.
- Transplant can only target your plants. You can't steal someone's garden.
- Dragging Vines is a 1 momentum displacement tool if you can fulfill its requirements.
- Quickness tending your Nature's Bounty plants can prevent an enemy (or bird) from stealing them after they've grown.
- Bear Bash is a 150% SWA attack that can crit.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Curate Class.

- Win a fight with a wooden weapon equipped as a Curate.
