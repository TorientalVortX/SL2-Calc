## Wiki notes

"Originally conceptualized on Kysei, the heavy-armored Arbalest is a fearsome predator. Requiring great strength and training, they fire weapons such as swords and axes chained to their bodies from large metallic bows. While effective, they are impractical in normal warfare, and are usually only seen as specialists."

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Great synergy with classes that grant you survivability or also use heavy armor, such as Black Knight, Demon Hunter (Matador Stance), Ghost, or Solblader.
- Blowback Cannon applies heavy knockback, knockdown, and damage in a large AoE. Use it to stake claim to an area in a fight.
- Heavy Tackle ignores knockback immunity and can apply knockdown, making it a useful tool for disengaging or displacing an enemy.
- Strong enemy displacement.
- Easily reaches high hit values due to its weapon type and Archer skills.

### Drawbacks

- Despite heavy armor passives, the class lacks additional bonuses to damage reduction or parries, relying only on raw Defense and Resistance; the class isn't particularly tanky on its own.
- Firing Posture makes the arbalest immobile and is outperformed by other options such as Volley Formation from Tactician or Gust Arrows from Archer, though the latter stacks.
- Special Armaments are limited to swords, axes, and spears. The only way to use unconventional weapons, such as tomes, is by monoclassing Arbalest (Arbalest/Arbalest).
- Skills other than Blowback Cannon have low scaling, requiring the Arbalest to crit or make use of subclass skills for a reliable source of high damage.
- Arbalest doesn't play well with other classes; it's selfish and prefers to stick to its own skills.
- Struggles with zoning, unlike Ranger.

### Tips

- Buster Cannon greatly lowers the target's Physical Defense, Magical Defense, and Armor. Use it against well-defended enemies to increase the damage you and allies deal to them.
- Chained Boomerang and Straight Cannon will both apply the on-hit effects of your Special Armament. You may want to choose a weapon that has such an item effect.
- Oil Chain reduces the Momentum cost of most of the 'Chained' skills, but it also leaves behind powerful field tiles when triggered. Leading or pulling targets through these tiles can deal a lot of extra damage.
- Shore Up's high Momentum cost makes it difficult to use efficiently. Try getting bonus Momentum from a critical hit or hitting an enemy's weakness before using it so you don't spend a turn doing nothing but reloading.

### Unlocking

- To unlock Arbalest, equip a heavy armor torso and win a battle using a bow as a Level 20+ Archer.
- Alternatively, you can equip a bow in your main hand and a sword, axe, or spear in your offhand.
