## Wiki notes

Through intensive study in a certain library on Karaten, a Mage can learn more about an ancient arcane art that allows one person to link together the flow of Focus into specific sigils that consistently shift arcane energies into specific altercations to the environment, turning the area around it into primed, but volatile traps.

Those sigils are called runes, and Mages who study and utilize Runes are called Rune Magicians.

### Strengths

- A large stack of runes can deal very large amounts of damage or kill weaker targets outright.
- Access to Immobilize, Blind, Glowing, Magnetize, Slow, and Knockback inflictions.
- Reliable dispel in Null Rune.
- Terrain control and zoning potential in Warding Rune and stacked runes.
- Both high AoE and single target damage, depending on whether a rune is engraved or placed on the ground in stacks.
- Great Accuracy on rune stacks with Haste Rune.
- Fast offence and setup when mainclassed with Swift Rune Crafting.
- Low skill-slot consumption due to Arcanum: Rune and access to the entire class without Destiny: Mage.

### Drawbacks

- No innate damage reduction.
- Vulnerable to Silence should an enemy get close enough to apply it.

### Tips

- Smoking Rune will prevent an attack from dealing damage to your rune. When approaching an enemy, use multiple Smoking Rune's to keep your large rune stack safe from harm.
- The hidden Ninjutsu talent tree can make you and your allies immune to the smoke from Smoking Rune.
- Null Rune's engrave effect can apply 1 dispel to the target once per turn, giving it use even in melee.
- If an opponent does reach you, try engraving two runes on them for 4 momentum and using your remaining 3 to cast an offensive spell or flee with Blink.
- A Warding Rune on a large rune stack can make entire swathes of terrain impassable to both enemies and allies. Use it carefully so that your teammates might contribute.
- You have great synergy with any evade buff providing class due to Smoking Rune and your zoning abilities. Most attacks made past your zone of denial will be subject to farshot penalty and are very unlikely to hit you.
- Salvage Rune provides a 1:1 FP refund if the rune is still alive at the end of the fight. Great for PvE!

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Mage Class.

- Speak to the Librarian in Karaten's Library. (G6)
- Read a tablet written in ancient Korvaran with the appropriate Crystal Terminal upgrade. (Korvara)
