## Wiki notes

The elegant martial fighters which are known as 'Duelists' have graced battlefields for ages. Compared to sheer brute force used by some, they prefer to implement precision and speed to outmatch their opponents.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Strong synergy with other basic attackering classes.
- Fleur is a tool unique to Duelist that can enable a third 3m action in a turn.
- Low FP costs.
- Riposte, Poise, Flottement and Eviter make Duelist live up to its name against other basic attackers.

### Drawbacks

- Being damaged will nullify bonuses from Poise.
- Targets with high Critical Evade can reduce or remove your chance to activate Fleur.
- Reliable damage options require being in or entering melee range.

### Tips

- Sidecut and Sillcut are your premier repositioning tools and cost almost nothing to use.
- Equipping an Eresh can turn Eclair Lacroix into a terrifying tool for users of Dark ATK.
- Cyclone Spear has a variety of useful elemental impacts, has no cooldown, and boasts a large AoE.
- Attacks and Eviter can rapidly damage your weapon. Consider ways to mitigate this, such as the maintenance talent.

### Promotions

Class LV20+ Duelists can promote to:

- Ghost
- Kensei
- Firebird
