## Wiki notes

The power of faith is known by practically all people of Sigrogana, having witnessed the war of the gods firsthand. While their number has dwindled, the Priests keep the faith alive. These servants of gods utilize divine magic to aid the faithful and smite the damned.

Given the faith's very wide reach, most Priests are of the Mercalan Church, but those of other gods are not uncommon, either.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Powerful healing skills.
- Can cure multiple status ailments and provide temporary immunity to them.
- Has powerful damaging spells in Shine Ray and Needle despite being a supportive class.
- Sanctuary prevents most special tiles from being created in its area.
- Simple execution; great for new players.

### Drawbacks

- High FP costs with little means of recovery outside Pacifist Boon.
- Interference prevents Priest's healing skills from doing anything to targets inflicted with it.
- Minimal mobility, relying on Quickness and the basic movement command like other Curate promoted classes.

### Tips

- Detailed Care applies a very large heal boost on targets directly next to you. If you have the opportunity to make use of it through Rescue or communication with your teammates, it's always worthwhile to do so.
- Shine Knights can be difficult to summon as a C-Rank invocation, but are powerful allies once they're out - especially when spirited.
- Sanctuary also prevents tile creation by teammates, but is ignored by Solsphere, Cherry Blossom, and Rune Magician runes. Consider what your allies might like to do before casting it.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Curate Class.

- Win a fight with the devotion talent at Rank 5+.
