## Wiki notes

Void Assassins are somewhat reckless users of the Void, an extra dimension that is used more safely by the Mage's Guild to transport people long distances in an instant. By using this poorly understood phenomenon, they can teleport short distances quickly, and enhance their deadly skills even further.

However, this comes with significant risk. Over time, such careless exposure to the Void's energies can cause a fatal condition known as Void Poisoning. But for some, the power is worth the price...

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Naturally high chance to score critical hits when attacking from behind with Backstab, heavily increased critical damage with Deadly Arms, and means to push their critical even higher if needed.
- Void Assassins, if main class, gain a lot of tools to effectively fight against Mages and casters.
- Incredibly strong displacement tools in Black Bolt, Vanishing Strike, and Detogate, all of which can drastically shift a fight in a Void Assassin's favor.
- Voidgates with Void Refraction can multiply a Void Assassin's or allies projectile damage exponentially.
- Very high mobility.

### Drawbacks

- A Void Assassin's potential plummets if they're unable to get behind an enemy.
- Reliant on critical hits for the bulk of their damage.
- No damage reduction against physical damage.

### Tips

- Black Bolt's interference effect ignores Status Resistance, while the silence does not.
- Void pollution tiles created by Black Bolt increase the skill cost of units standing inside it by a massive amount. This includes you.
- Extend Void extends more than just Void Assassin status effects.
- Cutthroat's effect is guaranteed if you're affected by Veil Off and applies Knockdown on a critical hit with Break Down.
- Absorb Veil can help you get FP back in a pinch.
- Don't give up if an opponent has high Critical Evade; mount the wall.

### Unlocking

- Use the Mage's Guild teleportation as a level 20+ Rogue. (G6)
- Use Blink as a level 20+ Rogue. (Korvara)
