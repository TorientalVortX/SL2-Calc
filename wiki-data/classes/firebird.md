## Wiki notes

Hinted to draw some inspiration from the followers of the Phoenix God Helondis, the art of a Firebird revolves around an unique ground-aerial combat style that emphasizes the full use of polearms, keen accuracy and fleet acrobatics, aiming to overwhelm and catch enemies off-guard with attacks faster than the eye can see.

The use of Magical Feathers only elucidates this art's namesake, letting Firebirds absorb elemental power, take off to the skies with powerful leaps, stay afloat while fighting flying enemies on even grounds, or perform deadly dives on unsuspecting enemies. For a Firebird, the sky is not the limit.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Strong mobility and longer attack range than other melee classes.
- Spears are often high hit weapons, and Falcon Strike, Lift Off, and Soaring Spear ignore evasion, making Firebird adept at fighting evasive enemies.

### Drawbacks

- Becomes significantly weaker against a target with high Critical Evade, as with most other Duelist promotions.
- Magical Feathers can be difficult to manage, leaving a Firebird without their powerful bonus effects until restored.
- Despite their numerous boons for being airborne, gaining the status can be difficult without the use of Riser.

### Tips

- Proper use of Boon of Apus separates the average Firebird from the greatest.
- Dive Down can both inflict knockdown and reduce a target's defense.
- Roc's Rise doesn't grant knockdown immunity on activation; there's nothing stopping an opponent from knocking you down until you run out of feathers.
- Ignite Power synergizes well with skills that create a large number of field tiles, such as Cyclone Spear while enchanted with Nerhaven or the appropriate badge.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Duelist Class.

- Score the 'Soarer' EXP Bonus Condition during a battle. (Gain the airborne buff 8 times in one battle.)
