## Wiki notes

Demon Hunters are a... unique breed, for sure. Their name is earned from the way they fight, a chaotic and stylish dance; if they couldn't kill demons, who could? Many 'proper' warriors see them as madmen who violate every basic principle of combat. Demon Hunters just view it as a way to utilize every tool to their advantage.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Loads of skills and stances for every situation.
- Reaver's Martial Lawbreaker allows the Demon Hunter to use any kind of weapon for their skills/Soldier skills.
- Synergizes with every other class.
- Reliable crowd control and knockdown in Crashing Bull, Bellowing Stag, and Clay Pidgeon.
- Very high mobility.

### Drawbacks

- A Demon Hunter's damage is often divided into several smaller hits, making them less effective against targets with high armor.
- Middling skill damage outside of Matador Stance.
- Cobra Stance provides no additional evade support, requiring a subclass to make use of the passives.

### Tips

- Winged Serpent is your prime mobility skill. Use Leaping Lizard next to a target to become airborne for free!
- Using Crashing Bull into Bellowing Stag produces a large AoE knockdown effect. Enchant yourself with Talvyd or the applicable badge item to make it even bigger!
- Quick and Wild applies a weapon's on-hit effects for 1 momentum.
- Jump Cancel resets repeat action tracking when you become airborne for the first time in a turn. Place it strategically in your skill combos for optimal value.

### Unlocking

- Defeat Kinu (Fight 2) at the Chinatoa Mines as a level 20+ Soldier. (G6) (If already done, just win any battle).
- Read a tablet written in ancient Korvaran with the appropriate Crystal Terminal upgrade. (Korvara)
