## Wiki notes

Holders of magical lanterns which are enchanted with the power of pseudo-elementals, a poorly understood entity somewhere between an elemental and a youkai, the Lantern Bearer uses the power of its light to great effect.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Large AoE on most spells and a variety of elemental damage to deal with it.
- Wide variety of inflictions in interactions, especially with soaked.
- Gentle Embers lets any Lantern Bearer switch from wide-area damage dealing to providing team-wide immunities at will.
- Gentle Embers Quetal provides interference immunity, making the class a stellar choice for healers.

### Drawbacks

- Lantern Bearer has only Quickness and the basic Movement command to rely on when moving, rendering it somewhat immobile.
- Spells affected by Farlight do significantly reduced damage at the edge of their radius.

### Tips

- Spells affected by Farlight do extra damage at close range, but lose damage the further the target is from the caster. This also applies to their Status Infliction; a target directly next to you has a much higher chance of being inflicted than one far away.
- Gentle Embers is a toggle. If you need knockdown or interference immunity, even as an offensive caster, use it!
- Geist Schritt (Zwei) and Whynesfelt (item) activate on Lantern Bearer's AoE spells, save for Phoenix. Be careful who's in your radius!

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Curate Class.

- Win a fight with Mage at level 10+ as a level 20+ Curate.

or

- Win a fight while having Use Staff at Rank 3+.

### Additional Lore

Lantern Bearer can summon forth one of the eight Pseudo-Elementals to their own aid. These entities are formless and conceptual, therefore they cannot be summoned onto physical forms like a Mage's Elementals, thus the only possibility to summon them is into formless objects, like flames, in a Lantern Bearer's case.

The basis of their use in this context is much more similar to summoning Youkai, but only partially.
