## Wiki notes

Black Knights are an intimidating presence in any fight, towering over foes with their huge armor and terrifying reputation. Epitomizing the principles of physical defense and protecting allies first learned as Soldiers, these warriors use the power of the Black Wind to force enemies to fight on their terms.

The Black Knights of Kysei are infamous for being nigh unkillable, a reputation that has seen them hired as bodyguards for wealthy individuals.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Skills that cleanse debuffs, including Fear, Hesitation, Charm, Poison, Rampaging and a way to completely negate On-Hit/On-Critical effects from the enemy's equipment through Steel Aura.
- Wide AoE displacement and two gap-closers to keep up with mobile opponents.
- Stalemate is a frontal parry that uses Defense to scale, reducing damage taken by 50% and applying Flatfoot if your main weapon's weight is 15 or more.
- Incredible resistance to physical damage from Negation, Indomitable, and their natural inclination to Defense.
- Several statistical boons, many of which increase against enemies they've inflicted with fear.

### Drawbacks

- Black Knight's defenses don't extend to magical damage.
- Burn reduces a Black Knight's Defense significantly, and more than other classes.
- Locked to heavy armor for the majority of their bonuses.

### Tips

- Face your opponent. Bulwark and Stalemate won't do anything if you're struck in the back.
- A weapon with the Haunted Soul enchantment applies a Status Infliction-ignoring fear on attack, and occurs before the attack is made.
- While Forced Move is a powerful grouping tool, you might not want everything around you all at once. Consider dispersing a dangerous crowd by using it's alternative push effect; it reduces their movement!
- Board Shaker does not affect flying or airborne units.
- Checkmate will always hit a target inflicted with Flatfoot or any immobilize, including Grapple.

### Unlocking

- While in battle, move in an L shape (exactly like a Knight piece in chess) as a level 20+ Soldier. Note: Teleportation from equipment such as Mercury Boots will not qualify as movement for the unlock.
