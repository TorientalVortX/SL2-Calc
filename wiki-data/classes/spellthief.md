## Wiki notes

Those who are talented with magic are held in high regards among other mages. But those who aren't, instead, learn to steal from them. Such is what a Spellthief is. Rogues who have pushed the concept of 'theft' a step further, and eventually learned that even talent can be stolen if you are cunning enough. This "talent" being magic itself.

Tricky, cunning and always a step ahead of every authority who dare give chase, those well-dressed, spectre-like thieves will always make sure those "talented mages" will never see it coming.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Spellthieves are the bane of Mages or any other Magic-based opponents. Whenever they steal a spell from an enemy, that spell is removed from their spell list for the rest of battle, and the Spellthief gains it.
- Versatile tool kit provides increases to Hit and Evade, debuff removal, Confusion, and whatever spells they have stolen.
- High physical and magical damage output.
- Plays very well with all other classes.

### Drawbacks

- No physical damage reduction.
- Unlike the casters they stole them from, all stolen spells have a minimum cooldown of 3 rounds.

### Tips

- You can steal and use certain monster spells with Spell Snatch.
- Mystic Dagger changes your attack's damage type to magical, much like Power Gradation.
- Snatch Spell works as Geist Schritt (Zwei) does, but only for spells. Their interactions are identical; you can prevent a Hexer from casting Wretched Oil or a Lantern Bearer from casting Salamander, for example.
- Your stolen spells persist through multiple battles, and if unequipped, become spell card items in your inventory; these can be used to change your current copy spells at will. Keep a stash of different cards for every situation!
- Stolen spells use your skill rank when casting or a default of 1; for example, if you were to cast Phoenix with no skill points from Lantern Bearer invested, it would cast at skill rank 1. If you invested 5 points into Phoenix and had a +1 Phoenix rank spirit, you would cast it at rank 6. Destiny: Rogue ignores this limitation and always casts your stolen spells at their default max rank.

### Unlocking

- Steal a status effect as a level 20+ Rogue.
