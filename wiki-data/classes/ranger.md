## Wiki notes

Some are better in-tune with nature than others, sometimes choosing to live among it rather than in villages and cities. Enter the Ranger; a skilled hunter that draws upon the power of nature to deal with their foes.

Some say the origin of their unique magic arrows were taught to them by the rarely-seen Druids, so that they could aid in the defense of nature against the unnatural and those that would destroy it, such as undead or greater terrors such as Hyatt of old.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Flip Shot and Retreat Flight make excellent spacing and retreat tools for the aspiring zoner.
- A plethora of useful status effects from Annorum.
- Two healing abilities that don't require Faith in Cherry Blossom and Gentle Garden.
- Exceptional range from Gust Arrows, Longdraw, and Expert's Aim, as the name might suggest.
- High accuracy due to their weapon type and Archer passives.
- Fortune Wind from Archer makes Ranger a highly adept evade.
- Simple execution; great for new players.

### Drawbacks

- High FP costs. Be careful how often you use your Ranger spells.
- Close-shot penalty often forces a Ranger to move or use Flip Shot, lest they miss entirely.
- Ranger has no additional damage reduction options, instead relying on a sub class or other means to tank.
- Far-shot penalty can negate your absurd range entirely if the opponent is evasive.

### Tips

- Annorum gains 2 charges when you cast any spell, no matter the class.
- Twin Shot deals a lot of damage and can critically strike like a basic attack. Also goes through Voidgates!
- Rain Trap comes with a plethora of bonus effects when struck by certain abilities. Create dark water tiles with Ghost Arrow, apply the effects of Pressure Fletch to a crowd, or simply revel in the constantly refreshing soaked and Marked Target effects it provides.
- Pressure Fletch applies three dispels when enhanced with Annorum, one on each of its hits. Use your high hit to remove evade buffs from a target or just get rid of something you don't like.
- Thunder Bolt's Annorum effect places a sigil on the ground that ruptures at the end of the round, inflicting hefty sound damage and knockdown that ignores evasion.
- Frigid Arrow applies a very high level freeze when enhanced by Annorum.
- Ghost Arrow's range is infinite. Why would a ghost care about gravity?

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Archer Class.

- Win a fight with a bow equipped in your main hand and light armor equipped as your torso.
