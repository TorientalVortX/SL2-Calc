## Wiki notes

A Martial Artist will sometimes go into a long period meditative isolation and re-emerge capable of dramatic feats of combat skill, earning the title of Monk. Monks achieve greater insight to the workings of their inner energy (or 'Ki') than the average bare-knuckle brawler, using it to heal allies, boost their own abilities or just kick people so hard their liver flies out their ear.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Possesses a large variety of skills and damage types to utilize, together with their ability to use practically any stat efficiently. Monks could be considered one of the most versatile and most self-sufficient classes to play.
- Benefits greatly from it's Main Class abilities available, while not relying on them so much to be considered a poor subclass option either.
- Body of Isesip can be used to cancel an opponent's attack up to 3 times; this treats the attack as if it never hit you at all.
- Great at engaging AND disengaging fights! Many of your tools boast considerable range, mobility and disruption such as displacement or knockdown - or you can zone entire halves of a battle map with Setting Sun.
- Power Up can offer nearly unlimited FP recovery by exchanging your Momentum.
- Backflip is a low commitment mobility option that can allow you to close the gap quicker than standard movement.

### Drawbacks

- Monks are a very resource reliant class and this must be kept in mind; poor management of your Ki can lead to scenarios where you spend a lot of momentum to recover precious resources.
- Ki can only be generated by spending momentum on Power Up, waiting on Spirituality, Ki Awoken, or by hitting enemies with All Fight active. This leaves Monk unable to generate Ki while staying on the offensive without being main class or spending health to gain Ki with Ki Awoken
- While Monks are a versatile class, they only particularly excel at dealing good damage with their basic attacks or heavily ki-reliant attacks such as Setting Sun or Kadouha.

### Tips

- Fists possess low critical damage and rely on stacking critical damage and on-hit effects to really pump out damage with their basic attacks. Try utilizing effects such as Serpent Strikes or choosing weapons with good on-hit effects to increase your damage if you feel you are struggling.
- Setting Sun is one of your greatest tools to throw out in a fight when you can! When throwing it out, if enemies remain in the circle, don't be afraid to just drop it down manually, as catching 2+ enemies with it can be huge damage.
- All Fight is great for managing your ki, and synergizes with effects that hit many targets at once since you gain Ki per enemy hit. Try using AoE attacks such as Heaven Kick, or enchants that cause your weapon attacks to hit things in an AoE such as Volcanic Seed on your weapon.
- Ki Awoken can be used to generate a large amount of ki and massively inflate your statistics, allowing Monk to temporarily push it's limits and use every single one of it's tools very well; certain skills like Ki Blast benefit directly from being in your Ki Awoken state, just remember to de-activate it before it gets too out of hand.
- Try pairing Monk with classes that enhance your Basic Attacks, such as any Duelist or Rogue subclass, or classes that increase your Elemental ATKs such as any Mage class.
- Races with access to Sharp Claws traits (Such as Felidae or Lupine) can trade a bit of health for a large heap of unprotectable damage, also expanding their damage type from Blunt to either Blunt or Slash.

### Unlocking

- Use Meditate as your first action in combat while at full HP as a level 20+ Martial Artist.
