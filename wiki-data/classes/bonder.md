## Wiki notes

Bonders, like their name suggests, favor bonding with a small number of Youkai. Through that bond, the Youkai and the Bonder work in harmony, bringing out the potential in each. To a Bonder, Youkai are not only tools of power, but irreplaceable friends.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Incredible statistical boosts when Installed with Youkai.
- Allows Youkai to act immediately when summoned with Fast Offense.
- High mobility with Pinball Strike, Warp Strike, and certain Youkai skills.

### Drawbacks

- Defenses are dependent on Youkai.
- High FP costs.

### Tips

- Infernal Summon both summons a Youkai and does a very large amount of damage.
- Hell Above and Heaven Below can greatly increase both you and your Youkai's effectiveness when used correctly.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Summoner Class.

- Win a battle while having three Youkai or less in your Summoning slots.

or

- Win a battle with a Youkai that has 60% or more Friendship.
