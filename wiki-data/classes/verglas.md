## Wiki notes

Verglas are Martial Artists who trained rigorously under the harsh and cold landscapes of Hyoya, similar to Monks, but different due to their spiritual path inclined towards unforgiving cold as the only form of energy they need to channel through their bodies.

When necessary, they engage in combat with a variety of kick techniques, and can generate ice through a type of spirituality they learn during training. The most dangerous place to engage a Verglas is in the snow and ice, and a skilled Verglas can make that environment appear anywhere (albeit briefly).

-In-Game description.

### Disciplines

Disciplines, or "Stances", are a mechanic tied to acquisition of Bear's Might, Fox's Cunning and Hare's Agility. Verglas offensive skills are typically tied to a discipline of some kind and will shift you into that stance when used; while in that stance any ice damage you inflict will grant or power up a unique buff by 5 LVs, and grant you a new basic attack skill only while you are in that stance.

- Bear's Might grants you a buff which increases your critical damage by LV, as well as power with fist weapons by the same amount, these stack.
- Fox's Cunning grants you a buff which increases your Ice ATK by LV.
- Hare's Agility grants you a buff which increases your evade by LV.

Every stance's buff can be acquired all at once, however the LV of each buff will decrease by 5 unless you are in that stance at the end of the round.

### Strengths

- Utilizing Ice ATK means that SKI investment translates not only to increased Hit rate, but also increased damage.
- Able to nullify a target's Ice Resistance/Immunity/Absorption/Reflection with Winter's Bite for a few rounds.
- Through Ice Point Guard, they can gain access to the 'Guard' skill and the ability to parry most melee weapon types.
- The generation of Ice Sheets through Expanding Ice can greatly hinder enemy mobility; once you are sitting on a large number of Ice Sheets it becomes very difficult for enemies to approach you, allowing you to simply stay back if you want to.
- The combo-centric playstyle of Chimera Style gives the class easy to use free form combos, which makes excellent usage of the base class' passive, Combination Strike, to deliver the hurt with.
- Ice Puns.

### Drawbacks

- Relies mostly on Ice and Blunt damage, a prepared opponent (or specific monster species) can stack Ice Resistance and sharply reduce your effectiveness.
- The FP consumption of this class can be high at times, especially when using skills from the Fox discipline.
- The combo-based style may require you to stay close to melee range when wanting to using Hare or Bear disciplines, which can be dangerous sometimes.
- The class has no FP recovery, so once you are dry, you are dry. Consider a back-up plan such as a subclass that can provide FP recovery or filing an item slot with Blue Potions.
- Ice Puns.

### Tips

- Your first equipped Verglas discipline skill determines what stance you start battles in.
- Verglas pairs well with other classes that can generate a lot of ice sheets quickly, enabling them to gain control over the battlefield and accelerate their gameplan. This may include such examples like Mage subclasses as they have access to Kraken and Famiuga.
- Stacking up your stances is extremely important; you may wish to put up effects like Ice Point Greaves or Ice Point Guard immediately to inflict many instances of ice damage as once, as each stance provides significant stat contribution for the Verglas to use.
- Crane Hop can be used to move into Melee Range of an enemy and still combo skills together with the usage of Chimera Style
- Verglas can use Expanding Ice to claim an Ally's ice sheets as well, extending their duration and making them yours. Thus Verglas pairs extremely well with other ice users, or youkai such as Snow Crow.
- Icicle Spear, when fired from your Ice Point Guard will inflict Frozen on whatever enemy it collides with. Use this to get around enemies with high evade as being Frozen reduces their evade to 0 while active.
- Rapid Kick will apply kick skill effects multiple times, making it exceptionally effective with effects that trigger on kick skills, such as Bladed Soles or Ice Point Greaves.
- Once you have established control of the battlefield with many ice sheets, Ice Skate becomes an extremely potent method of moving around, allowing you to move beyond what is normally even possible provided you have a straight line of ice sheets ahead of you.

### Unlocking

- Win a battle after using Heaven Kick, Light Tomahawk and Peddling Wheel as a level 20+ Martial Artist.
- --OR--
- Win a battle with at least thirty ice sheets from you active on the battlefield at once as a level 20+ Martial Artist.
