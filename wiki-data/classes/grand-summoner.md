## Wiki notes

Once evolving past the title of a normal Summoner, and learning how to effectively summon multiple Youkai at once, one is to gain the prestigious worth of the Grand Summoner title. For it takes a truly talented Summoner to effectively command a horde of Youkai.

For Grand Summoners, Youkai are but tools of war and destruction, and so is their role to show how fearful their Youkai can truly be.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Great FP management through Absorb Ether and Cracked Mirror.
- Summon multiple Youkai at once with Summon Storm.
- Heal and Revive unsummoned Youkai with Reihou.
- Strong burst potential in Chain Unsummon.

### Drawbacks

- Youkai still have low Move, are rather fragile, and can't act immediately.
- Movement and defensive options are dependent on Youkai.

### Tips

- Against lonesome targets, rapidly summoning your Youkai and finishing with Chain Unsummon can often kill a target outright due to Dimension Resonance.
- Auto Summon summons the first Youkai in your Youkai list at the start of battle for no momentum cost.
- Stacking Ripple Dimension can help you get Youkai out significantly faster.
- Judgement Blade's effects apply to targets inflicted with Soul Chains.
- Installing certain Youkai such as Drowned Woman can act as a potent defensive layer on their own.

### Unlocking

- Summon and sustain three Youkai at once as a level 20+ Summoner.
