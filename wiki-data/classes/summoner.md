## Wiki notes

The title of Summoner is a rare honor. They possess a unique potential that many do not. They utilize their talents by gathering and contracting types of illusory creatures known as 'Youkai', beings that can only manifest if they have a sufficient source of Focus energy, which the Summoner supplies them with. In exchange, the Youkai acts to further the Summoner's will, aiding them in battle or lending them their strength in other ways. The destiny of a Summoner is often a twisted path...

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- The diversity of Youkai allows them to fill a wide range of roles.
- Works well with any other class due to the nature of Youkai.
- The Summoner can utilize some abilities of their Youkai through the Sync-Mind skill.
- Summoners can greatly increase their own stats with the Install skill.

### Drawbacks

- A Summoner is often reliant on their Youkai. It is important to ensure the well-being of your Youkai since they will not automatically recover after battle.
- Summoner has no way to damage the enemy by themselves.
- Youkai upkeep is FP intensive, even with Cracked Mirror.

### Tips

- Most of a Summoner's strength comes from its promotions, rather than the base class. Summoner serves as a baseline for their effects.

### Promotions

Class LV20+ Summoners can promote to:

- Grand Summoner
- Bonder
- Shapeshifter
