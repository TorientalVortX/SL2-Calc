## Wiki notes

Sometimes going in alone isn't the best solution, a proper Rogue knows this. You can't always trust your friends to do the job right, or go see a doctor in the middle of no where, so how does one best prepare for this? By building their own friends out of just about anything they can get their hands on.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Technological Marvel gives a half skill slot for every engineer skill equipped and Resourceful from Rogue adds additional skill slots. An engineer rarely runs into skill slot problems, no matter their subclass.
- Powerful summons for healing, dealing large burst damage, and protecting allies.
- High mobility in Jetpack.
- Numerous skills give a boost to belt items, increasing their range and the number of them available. This makes engineer a very useful support class who can use potions or magic stones from far away.
- Useful buffs to be provided to allies or used on themselves.

### Drawbacks

- High FP costs.
- Higher than average setup time.
- Vulnerable to ranged opponents and zoning.
- Reliant on Metalaegis to stay alive without a subclass.

### Tips

- All robot summons are 50 FP a pop, leaving an engineer dry if they don't collect their Salvage in longer fights.
- Most of the bots scale off of the engineer's class level, forcing any engineer who wants to use them to their full potential to be a Destiny Rogue.
- Item belt items can be expensive! Consider how much that magic stone costs before you use it.
- Hand Trigger can be used to detonate Grenade Launcher grenades without waiting for the randomized timer.
- Upgrade (Jetpack) can solve an ally's mobility problems.
- All upgrade skills provide YOUR SKILL LEVEL of that skill to an ally.

### Unlocking

- Win a battle with a wrench in your main hand as a level 20+ Rogue.
