## Wiki notes

The Evoker holds nothing back, turning even their bodies into dangerous weapons capable of reducing buildings to rubble. Unmatched in sheer magical power, an Evoker is an existence to be feared.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Strong FP management with Absorb Power and Arcane Tattoo.
- Very high damage output, even with simple 3 momentum spells.
- One of the strongest displacement options in Vydel.
- Several evade ignoring options with excel charges or Charge Mind.
- Simple execution; great for new players.

### Drawbacks

- No source of physical damage.
- Minimal defensive options.
- Silence disables all of Evoker's spells.

### Tips

- High-Speed Divine Words applies to all Mage promoted classes, so Hexer spells are also affected.
- Ryemei applies Interference when enchanted with Redgull and becomes a larger AoE with Charge Mind.
- Wind Slasher applies heavy damage over time when enchanted with Talvyd.
- Magaisendo breaks field tiles with hardiness 1 when enchanted with Galren.
- Most of Sear's value comes from the Cinder tiles it creates, not from the initial damage.
- Intensify Cold applies a hefty 50% ice weakness to your target. Pick up Verglas as a sub class to get the most out of it, or just set up for a friend!

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Mage Class.

- Win a battle with nothing equipped on your torso (or an Unarmored type armor).
