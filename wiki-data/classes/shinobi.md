## Wiki notes

Matured in the shadows and honed in secrecy, the shinobi hide in plain sight. As skilled at stealth as a spider, as easily infiltrating secure compounds as the wind blows in, rumored to possess countless secret arts. It is no wonder why even the legend of the Shinobi draws admiration - and fear.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Versatile Martial Artist caster class, and has veritably the largest toolkit available of any class.
- Elemental Arts further improve the class' versatility and playstyles available, nearly any stat spread can work.
- Hand Signs' silence coverage in combination with Kip Up make you very difficult to pin down effectively.
- False Target is a Sub-class parry skill that can help you escape many dangerous situations.

### Drawbacks

- Mobility centric playstyle means that immobilizing effects will severely limit what you may be able to do.
- Relies upon multiple elemental scalings to access the better parts of it's kit, leading to awkward and sometimes difficult stat spreads.
- Jack of all trades, lower damage scalings and some not quite-as-powerful effects make the class' versatility turn against it when a more specialized role is needed.

### Tips

- Studying up on which Elemental Arts to employ in any given situation will help shape your gameplan for any given fight.
- Rainbow Art: Rising Dragon can occasionally give casters a 3rd action when invoked by Furinkazan
- Suits hybrid defensive playstyles well, with the ability to evade cap itself and provide Swift Guard, Shinobi can be very difficult to take down.
- Using a Flower Wreathed Tunic or a piece of Equipment kitted with Seedbark will allow the Shinobi better access to Kiomite Moriomizu

### Unlocking

- Win a fight with a Shuriken in your main hand, and have a tome or spell equipped as a level 20+ Martial Artist.
