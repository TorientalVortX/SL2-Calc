## Wiki notes

Warriors who utilize the power of solmancy to stem the flow of evil, carrying with them a symbol of the sun itself. They are known for their potent power, but one must be careful to keep it in check. For the brighter the light, the greater the shadow it casts...

-In-Game description.

Solbladers are highly versatile and effective class that rely on utilizing the Solsphere; a sun-like field object that deals damage to enemies and enhances the Solblader with a powerful Holy Aura.

They also have Solcharges, a resource that can be spent on many of their skills and spells.

Finally, they are able to choose between three different styles: Speed of Light, Wall of Light and Flash of Light which modify how they approach combat and can be freely equipped and exchanged without any costs.

### Strengths

- Has a versatile toolkit that isn't restricted by weapon and armor choices, making it an appealing option to pair with.
- Has access to Radiant Solace, an incredibly powerful heal that expends your Solsphere to cast. Using it wisely can turn the tide of battle.
- Strong contender against any opponent who needs to be in melee range due to their naturally high Critical Evade, Solsphere, powerful short range skills, and Wall of Light.
- Incredibly powerful guard break in Chariot Star.
- Heavily defensive class that's difficult to interrupt. Wall of Light Solblader is great for new players.

### Drawbacks

- Class resource reliant, with little means of gaining it from a subclass.

### Tips

- Divine Spark swaps the majority of your elemental focus to Lightning scaling and damage. Great for those who don't wish to invest into Faith. (This doesn't apply to Radiant Solace, Holy Shield's parry chance, or Light On High's resistance reduction.)
- Holy Shield, Holy Sword, and Holy Wings cannot be dispelled. You'll always get your value in Sol Charges.
- Holy Shield combined with Blazing Sol and Wall of Light's Holy Aura effects makes a Solblader unbelievably tanky.
- Light On High lowers a target's resistance to most of your attacks.
- Your Solsphere does less damage the more it hits someone, up to 50%. This resets at the start of every round.

### Unlock Requirements

With Level 20+ Soldier, win a battle with a weapon that has the Faithful scaling tag (exclusively) equipped.
