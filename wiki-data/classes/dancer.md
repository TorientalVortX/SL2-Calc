## Wiki notes

The graceful steps of a Dancer can invoke powerful emotions. Attraction. Excitement. Pain. For one never really expects a curt stomp on the foot until it's too late.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Very high single-target damage.
- Sustains itself through the use of Rest Beat and Idol Step.
- Several crippling debuffs, such as interference on Thunder Steps, and Idol Step's effect.
- Simple execution; great for new players.

### Drawbacks

- Uses flat hit instead of weapon hit, greatly lowering the hit potential of the class.
- Extreme FP costs.
- Doesn't play well with other classes due to Dancer's Tempo.
- Struggles in large crowds.

### Tips

- Thunder Steps has a cooldown if you end its use too far away from an enemy. Consider your positioning carefully when using it.
- Unholy Diver from Dark Bard works with bonuses from your class, and has the highest damage potential of all dances when used in conjunction with Dancer.
- Even when used in a crowd, Idol Step's debuffs, high damage, and healing still provide a lot of value.
- Limbo is an independent effect that can negate projectiles thrown at you entirely.
- Idol Step can apply undesirable debuffs to allies. Be careful when using it near them.

### Unlocking:

Use every unique Bard dance in a single fight as a level 20+ Bard.
