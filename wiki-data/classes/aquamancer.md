## Wiki notes

Aquamancers are magic specialists in manipulating the element of water. Skillfully mixing the destructive power of the waves and the refreshing purity of the rain, those who are drawn to the element of water are often gentle, but dangerous, much like water itself.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Dancing Water can apply immobilize and ignore evasion, making it a very powerful tool.
- Crest Break helps an aquamancer deal with elemental resistances and deal more damage than they otherwise would; especially with an indignant idol.
- Purifying Aquae is a unique and powerful dispel, reducing debuff duration instead of removing them entirely.

### Drawbacks

- High FP costs.
- Minimal mobility, relying on Quickness and the basic movement command like other Curate promoted classes.
- Relatively slow build-up time.

### Tips

- Purifying Aquae, Refreshing Flow, Brine Blade, and Ensui Veil are applied to all allies during the effects of Call Storm.
- Ring of Pearls is both an evade buff and a parry, making Aquamancer uniquely suited to supporting another evade-based class.
- Servant of a Dark Authority turns Aquamancer into a dark water creating machine.
- Water to Wine can apply confusion in a large AoE.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Curate Class.

- Defeat any Fangs, the Whirlpool-type dungeon bosses. (Lost Beach on Korvara.)
