## Wiki notes

Tacticians are not your traditional soldier, but they perform an important role. After all, soldiers are only as good as their commander. Capable of utilizing spell and sword, Tacticians are usually intelligent individuals who can turn the tide of any battle.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- 10% stackable EXP gain increase for a Tactician's party.
- Can provide guard and small offensive boosts to their party globally.
- Enemy Evaluation and Analyze Weakness apply percent stat reductions to enemies; application of the former can remove a target from the fight on its own.
- White Prison can hold back targets without access to teleports.

### Drawbacks

- Limited damage output.
- Performance Rating can be difficult to upkeep in parties with squishy allies.
- The positioning requirement from formations often outweighs the effects they provide.

### Tips

- Youkai and friendly allied units (Engineer Bots, Mercenaries, etc) will count on formations for their benefits and for the Tactician Rank.
- Volley Formation can turn any ally with a ranged weapon into a serious zoning threat.
- Dark Eye's explosion effect makes your next Enemy Evaluation apply to that target as well. Consider keeping one dark eye by your side, inflicting yourself with Confusion via a Betrayer's Wit tome, and crippling multiple opponents from across the map!
- Splash applies soaked for a staggering 5+ rounds. Consider using your extra 1 momentum to help any ally use its effect.

### Unlocking

- Win a battle with a tome in your main hand as a level 20+ Soldier. (Main Class required.).
