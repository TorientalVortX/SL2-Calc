## Wiki notes

Boxing originated in a place called Bellaux, which was a territory of Chaturanga on Sigrogana before the Empire gained control of the entire continent. Bellaux still exists and is not very far from the Arena (although it isn't represented on the map at the moment). Bellaux was always interested in people fighting for sport; the Arena is just one example of this, but fists were always the original weapon of choice. They would fight in arena-like cages, hence the name Boxer.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- By building up Schwarz Sturm the Boxer can increase the damage of their skills by 5% per level, making them a class that ramps up to do a lot of damage with their skills over the course of a fight.
- Boxer can spend their Schwarz Sturm at maximum stacks on powerful finishers such as Grandupper, which can temporarily remove someone from the fight, letting gravity do the work on the way down.
- Geist Schritt allows Boxer to stay mobile and defensive at the same time, as it enters them into a stance which will deflect the next Basic Attack or Targeted Skill that is sent their way, and give them the momentum they spent on the skill back on a successful read.
- Boxer has many of the strengths you would expect of a class that can "Level Up", at lv8 Schwarz Sturm they can have a significant splash on any fight with incredible AoE tools such as Orkam Drehen and Nachbeben
- Boxer can get around Blunt Resistance with Gotthand after levelling up Schwarz Sturm to lv4.

### Drawbacks

- Boxer comes attached with some hefty FP costs and disincentive to recover it with active skills or potion usage, as using non-boxer skills will decrease your Schwarz Sturm level. Make sure you have a good pool of FP to draw from.
- Boxer can be a very selfish class; it will only build Schwarz Sturm levels from Boxer skills, and actively loses it from using any skills outside of Boxer and Martial Artist.
- Ironically, Boxers may struggle with extended fights with targets that can heal up your damage. In a battle of attrition they will likely become heavily disadvantaged when running low on FP, needing to spend most of their momentum on recovery instead.
- Skills that target the ground instead of an enemy will bypass Geist Schritt.

### Tips

- Geist Schritt (Eins) defines "Basic Attack" usage as skills that are or act as your "Basic attack" (And not basic attack skills such as Hanging), which are the default attacks that use durability located above the skills menu in battle. This can include anything that would normally go there, including Special Attack Maneuvers and Sub-Attack.
- Geist Schritt (Zwei) will typically affect attacks that would target you directly or target opponents around the user such as Wretched Oil or Lantern Spells. Monsters typically will always trigger Geist Schritt (Zwei) as they require targeting you with a skill to use it, even if it's an AoE that normally avoids it.
- Geist Schritt can be carried into the next round if you move later in the turn order than your opponent, allowing you to potentially smuggle an additional 3m into the next round if your opponent guesses incorrectly.
- A workaround for punishing enemies who may try to exploit the quirks of Geist Schritt is to use the Drei version of the skill, as an enemy targeting the ground technically hasn't targeted you at all, gaining you additional Schwarz Sturm levels.
- Regenschauer Can be used to bypass the damage resistances of enemies, which is great for dealing with temporary but effective damage reductions such as Guard or Painproof
- Asura Fist is the class' highest damaging ability, but cannot be used while Silenced, and is susceptible to being Spell Stolen.
- Sturm Shreik can be used to shut a mage up for a little while, inflicting silence on them, and much more effectively at high Schwarz Sturm levels.
- On The Ropes is a very powerful innate, You will count as a dense object for it to use as a damage boost, so boxers end up making pretty good assassins when punching people in the spine.

### Unlocking

- Win a battle while you are equipped with Boxing Gloves as a level 20+ Martial Artist. Boxing Gloves can be bought from the Badlands Arena (G6) or from the Casino in Meiaquar (Korvara) with special currencies.
