## Wiki notes

The Rogue can be many things, but if asked for a generalization anyone could answer 'thief'. However this does not limit it to just that, for that is just generalization. The kleptomaniac who has a keen interest in how to open things? A rogue. The headstrong ex-warrior whom favors stealth and somewhat-dirty-but-clever tactics in battle? A rogue. Interpretation is open to the mind.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Has the largest skill pool of any non-Will-based class due to the Resourceful skill. This makes them a good pairing with any other class.
- Flank allows the Rogue to deal extra damage with good positioning.
- Versatile combatant with bonuses Move, increased Evade, Pocket Sand to Blind, etc.
- Strong field control with traps.
- High mobility.

### Drawbacks

- No innate damage reduction.
- Few instantaneous damaging skills.

### Tips

- Hand Trigger can be used to instantly detonate any traps or bombs you've placed and grants them evade-ignoring properties at excel charge level 3.
- Quick Slide's trickery effect places a trap beneath your starting position.
- Both Rogue's weapon type and skills benefit from Guile investment. Certain skills, such as Pocket Sand or Quick Slide, have bonus effects that occur more reliably with higher investment in the stat.
- Steal has limits on what it can take. Learning what you can and can't steal from a target is valuable to any aspiring Rogue.

Class LV20+ Rogues can promote to:

- Void Assassin
- Spellthief
- Engineer
