## Wiki notes

Disciples of a religion, typically the Mercalan faith, these magically inclined healers are a staple in any group. While the Mercalan Tenets restrict how healing magic should be used, there is never a shortage of emergencies where it is needed.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- With the ability to heal and buff allies, Curates shine in groups.
- Rescue has a very long range, made even longer by Mass.
- Pacifist Boon remains the heart of a dedicated healer, both reducing cooldowns for healing spells and providing a large amount of FP regeneration.

### Drawbacks

- Limited damage output, encouraging another class to pick up the slack.
- Poor mobility.

### Tips

- Mass-enhanced spells are your bread and butter. If it would effect more than one target, it's almost always better to use it.
- Purity Edge can often outperform weapon enchantments outright.
- Silent Prayer both removes and grants brief immunity to silence. Keep it in your back pocket; you'll need it one day.

### Promotions

Class LV20+ Curates can promote to:

- Priest
- Lantern Bearer
- Aquamancer
