## Wiki notes

Soldiers are protectors of the common folk. Traditionally trained in physical combat for the purpose of defense rather than killing, their goal is to protect lives rather than take them. Athletic enough to rush into the center of combat when needed and sturdy enough to take on the mightiest of blows, these qualities are what all true Soldiers strive for.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- As a soldier, you can strategically place yourself in harm's way with Charge and Bash to push enemies away from vulnerable party members and to put yourself in melee range quickly.
- Fortitude, Bulwark, and Tenacity provide some innate heft to the class and its promotions.
- Weapon Discipline provides a large durability consumption reduction.

### Drawbacks

- Shinken is a soldier's only form of ranged attack, leaving them susceptible to ranged opponents and zoning.

### Tips

- Face your opponent. If using a shield, Bulwark's damage reduction can be maintained through Watchful Eye.
- Most of your base class skills come with excel and enchantment effects. Incise can apply interference when enchanted by Redgull, for example.
- Protect only affects basic attacks.

### Promotions

Class LV20+ Soldiers can promote to:

- Black Knight
- Tactician
- Demon Hunter
- Solblader
