## Wiki notes

Within the darkness of traditional performance thrives the Dark Bard. Their greatest allies are their piercing voices, soul-rocking tones, and... their fans, of course. Just be careful - you may not leave one of their shows without your nose getting bloodied.

### Strengths

- High damage output.
- Large AoE options.
- Restores the HP and FP of allies while increasing their elemental ATK.
- Strong displacement of enemies, allies, and the Dark Bard themselves.
- Hard Note breaks invocations. No exceptions.

### Drawbacks

- Lacklustre defensive options.
- Deals small amounts of damage to allies regularly, which can disable certain effects.
- Few options to deal with evasive targets.

### Tips

- Getting Into It makes reflecting, absorbing, or being immune to a Dark Bard's damage a difficult affair.
- Crazy Riff is one of the few ways to restore an ally's FP, and it restores a lot. Keep yourself and friends topped up in longer fights.
- On A Leash and Raid the Rafters let you manipulate enemy and ally positions with ease, but be careful when using the former, as it can prevent allies from moving.
- Second Set consumes all Darkness Hype on allies to heal them based on its LV, plus some bonus effects. This skill is global.

### Unlocking

- Wear the Sunglasses accessory and use a song skill in battle as a level 20+ Bard.
