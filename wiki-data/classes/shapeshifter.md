## Wiki notes

The exotic art of Shapeshifting is dangerous, yet powerful. By pushing the ability to 'Install' to another level, a Summoner can become a physical and mental vessel for their Youkai, and further increase its destructive capability.

Though such fearsome power comes at a heavy cost of one's "self", as such technique defies the laws of nature itself, the results of extended use are often grim, sad tales told on campfire stories.

---

Stories speak of Youkai who have taken over in these stages, leaving shapeshifters trapped within a prison of their own body, no longer who they once were. Some are are said to never return, at least...never the same.

As with most things, the general age of the art is not well known.There are certainly tales of shapeshifting creatures that have been told for hundreds of years, however. Despite such, practitioners of this art are far from common.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Strong evade support in Chaos Reflex and Night Shade.
- A wide variety of Installs and bonus effects make for an adaptable class.
- High physical and magical damage output.

### Drawbacks

- No innate damage reduction.
- Limited, but notable HP costs on most skills.
- Requires mainclass to function due to the nature of Install.
- High FP costs, especially on Youkai skills.

### General Tips

- If you have unspent Momentum you need to get rid of, try to use one of the Mimicry skills to get a useful effect. They all cost 1M, but can only be used by a specific race (you can use them while installed with a Youkai of that race).
- Self-Destructive Shifting lets you use the useful Mimicry more frequently, but makes their HP costs higher. Alternatively, you can utilize Preservation Instinct to reduce the HP cost of those skills instead. Note that you can't use them both at the same time!
- Like Mimicry skills, Grow Appendage has different effects depending on which Youkai you're currently installed with.
- Youkai Evoke skills use base hit, not weapon hit, and thus have drastically reduced accuracy.
- If playing for physical damage and critical, Bloody Shift can reduce the momentum cost of Phase Python's Phase Fang to 2 momentum, which is a basic attack with added poison infliction.
- If playing for magical damage, consider using Seiryuu's Hunter Wind to increase your damage against a target or installing yourself with Orbello.
- Jhun's Flame Jetwing is a powerful mobility option.
- Lilu can be Split Form next to an enemy, allowing them to immediately use Grapple.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Summoner Class.

- Speak to a mysterious person within Lispool's Youkai Realm. (G6) (Located right above the Church)
- Read a tablet written in ancient Korvaran with the appropriate Crystal Terminal upgrade. (Korvara)
