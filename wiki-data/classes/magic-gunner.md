## Wiki notes

Guns were originally created in Karaten - a simple to use weapon that fires 'bullets' of the user's focus, basically allowing anyone to use magic. However, they were not meant to be public - which is unfortunate, as the technology was stolen from under their noses. Nowadays, knock-offs can be found in seedy areas, gradually making the once-secret weapons more available, even to adventurers.

Arcane Shells, however, are less common, despite amplifying the power of guns significantly. In part due to being less understood, and partially because they significantly drain the user more than a regular gun, giving rise to the fear that they can make your lifespan shorter.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Has a variety of shells with different useful effects and debuffs, several of which ignore status resistance.
- Handguns, Shotguns, Rifles, and Launchers all have distinct synergies and play styles, all of which make use of the utility inherent to Arcane Shells.
- Recklessness removes and grants immunity several debuffs that would otherwise slow you down.
- Fortune Wind from Archer makes Magic Gunner a highly adept evade.

### Drawbacks

- Kensei or a target with a spirit mirror can reflect your own powerful shells back at you. Watch where you're shooting!
- A Magic Gunner needs to be able land critical hits consistently if they want to deal large amounts of damage or reliably activate Spark Drive.
- Overcharge locks Magic Gunner's strongest shell effects behind a cooldown.

### Tips

- Using the right Arcane Shell in the right situation is the key to victory.
- Ghost's Claret Call works with Akimbo and handguns very, very effectively.
- Overcharged Sonic Shell completely ignores your enemy's evasion.
- Overcharged Interference Shell breaks invocations, unsummons enemy units, and applies a long interference. This effect ignores status resistance and the invocation talent.
- Null Shell both reduces a target's resistances and applies FOUR dispels depending on whether it's charged or overcharged.
- Blaze Shell applies a burn from just a humble Charged Shot. Don't underestimate it!

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Archer Class and level 10+ Mage Class.

- Win a fight with a gun equipped in your main hand. (Does not work with mutated weapons).
