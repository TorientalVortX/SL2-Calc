## Wiki notes

Rather than live within bloody fray, some sit atop a throne and play. Of course, that's selling the Ruler short - they are just better suited as the decision makers, the ones supporting the heroes from afar - or perhaps more aptly said, optimally placing the pieces on the board.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- A Rook Automaton's Rookstar does a decent chunk of damage and can apply knockdown.
- Can cure and provide immunity to Confusion.
- Cruel Enchant allows base Mage and ruler enchantments to be applied to large crowds at a time.
- Crest of Bishop is a useful supportive tool that reduces the duration of debuffs on allies.

### Drawbacks

- Skill slots may be limited if subclassed.
- A lot of build-up is required to properly use the class, which may take one or two rounds of doing nothing but harvesting Geomaterials.
- Generating/managing Geomaterial can be troublesome in the heat of battle or if you are being targeted down by enemies who have ranged attacks.
- Damage output is very low for a Mage class, and in general.
- Few offensive spells and doesn't utilize Elemental Attack in any meaningful way.
- All of their offensive skills involving Whips have cooldowns, meaning that the class's offensive potential is very limited to small 'bursts'.
- Automatons require energy or enchantments to take actions. These are consumed quickly and can be dispelled right off.
- Vulnerable to 'rushdown' tactics.
- Limited defensive options.

### Tips

- Getting a Rook Automaton out quickly can both keep you protected and setup for its Rookstar ability.
- Ruler can quickly apply both Relent Gale and Astral Belt to their team, giving each target access to a 1 momentum knockdown.
- Be careful what elemental enchants you apply to your allies; you can disable a player's build or give them an unwanted elemental weakness.
- Crest of Bishop is your strongest supportive tool and something unique to you. Don't forget to use it.

### Unlocking

Win a fight with Whip equipped as a level 20+ Mage.
