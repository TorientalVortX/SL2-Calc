### Tips

- Some Mixtures deal damage to allies in the form of Kickback Damage. Certain skills that benefit from this would be Youkai Tamer from Grand Summoner, or Dark Revelry from Dark Bard.
- Mutagens sometimes offer powerful Enhancement Bonuses. However, strategies using several of these effects will require Status Resistance if they don't want to run certain risks. Obtain this with Warding enchantments, or by boosting your FAI and SAN stats.
- Damage dealt by Mixtures is generally boosted by your Chemist class level. Thus, you can make use of Archer Destiny to obtain a higher class level and therefore higher base damage; this is not necessary, however.
- Using Safety Gear will protect you from the effects of Mist field effects, such as Mixture Mists. This can be useful when creating long lasting Mist effects, such as with Evaporative, allowing you to move through or into them without danger.
- The sheer variety of effects available from Mixtures makes Chemist an extremely flexible class, capable of synergizing with many others, but it can be complicated as well. Take your time as you explore its options!
