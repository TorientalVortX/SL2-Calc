## Wiki notes

Bards are users of music and sound to great effect. As simple as a singer for drunks at a bar, or as fantastic as an enchanting singer who could rival a mage, one is likely to encounter a Bard anywhere people gather... to the pleasure or pain of those people. Bards have a reputation, one might say, and not always in the positive sense.

-In-Game Description.

### Strengths

- Empowers allies with a variety of statistical buffs.
- High mobility with dance skills.

### Drawbacks

- High FP costs.
- Locked to instrument-subtype weapons.

### Tips

- Most of a Bard's strength comes from its promoted classes rather than its base, as they provide strong enhancements to its skills.

### Promotions

Class LV20+ Bards can promote to:

- Performer
- Dancer
- Dark Bard
