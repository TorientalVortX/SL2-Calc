## Wiki notes

When desperate for power, there is always the option to turn to the forbidden Dark Arts. Such desperation becomes the breeding ground for what are known as Hexers. These Mages delve into curses and hexes, manipulating concepts like karma or inflicting harm, sometimes even tapping into the grudges of the deceased to amplify their anger and inflict harm upon the living.

Hexers are seen as vile practitioners, willing to embrace any means to gain power. Their actions inevitably draw the ire of the Geistritter, who tirelessly work to rectify the chaos wrought by these dark magicians.

-In-Game description.

### Strengths

- Strong in a team and can turn the tide of a fight easily.
- Innate tanky-ness from stat bonuses, Endurance, Walk In Darkness, and their spell effects.
- High Status Infliction and means to reduce their opponent's.
- Access to several of the most debilitating status effects and a few unique ones.
- Simple execution; great for new players.

### Drawbacks

- Most of Hexer's damage takes time to occur.
- Suffers to silence as well as any mage.
- No means to rectify its heavy FP costs on its own.

### Tips

- High-Speed Divine Words from Evoker works on Hexer's invocations as well.
- Eternal Darkness only extends Blind; it doesn't inflict it.
- Llunel's Decay only reverses regeneration, not healing.
- Fellel's Fumble makes the bearer's basic attacks always miss, but has no effect on spells or skills.
- Black Bubble is a massive AoE silence that also forms dark water the next round.
- Wretched Oil applies a high level poison that will do even larger amounts of damage with Menov's Fang.
- Earthbound Vengeance and Spirit Pain both have unique use cases. Remember; pain for the lone basic attacker, vengeance for the crowd!
- Death Knighting grants the bearer the Fallcall passive, increasing their damage against targets they've feared by a massive 50%.

### Unlocking

Requires level 20+ Mage Class.

- Win a fight with any weapon bearing Cursed or Doomed enchants equipped on your main hand.
