# Unarmored

**Slot 2 — Armor.** 33 items.

Unarmored is one of the three classes of Armor. Typically it is made of Cloth materials, has very little weight and provides large bonuses to Evade when upgraded. However it usually has low defensive properties; such as a low Armor value that cannot be upgraded very much and a mediocre Magic Armor value that can be somewhat upgraded. This type of Armor is most often used by characters who want to Evade or for classes where they require Cloth material (such as Void Assassin's Voidveil) or Unarmored type Armor to apply (such as Martial Artist's Unarmored Prowess or Evoker's Evocation).
