# Shields

**Slot 3 — Hands.** 20 items.

Shields are a special type of Hands equipment, they often provide added DEF or RES and sometimes added elemental resistances or specific protections. Additionally, all Shields will provide you with the Guard skill, which can greatly cut the damage you'll take. All shields are made of Metal.

### Lion Shield

- **Rarity:** 8★

- +2 DEF, +2 RES
- Grants Skill: Lion's Roar
- 3 Momentum, 15 FP
- Unleash a mighty roar. Increases the user's STR by 2 (+1 per 5 character levels) for 3 rounds. Enemies in range may also be Feared for 2 rounds.

_This lion-faced shield is very ornate, but sturdy. It can also roar, which will be sure to scare your enemies senseless._

**Found:** Random Drops, Argentyle Caverns, Beast Drop Table

### Magnetic Shield

- **Rarity:** 9★

- +3 DEF
- Reduces damage taken from Gun weapons by 3.
- Redirects Bow and Gun attacks on allies within 2 Range to yourself.

_Similar to a tortoise shell, this shield is made of magnetic metals that can be enhanced during brief moments. It will draw in certain attacks towards it._
