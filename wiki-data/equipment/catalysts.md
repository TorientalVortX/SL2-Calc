# Catalysts

44 catalysts. A catalyst is what applies an enchantment to a piece of
equipment, and each one names the slots it will accept — which is how an enchantment
ends up restricted to, say, Torso and Hands.

## Catalysts

### Black Hole

- **Rarity:** 10★
- **Can enchant:** Weapons

+10 Weapon Critical. +5% Critical Damage. When critically hitting monsters with this weapon (excluding bosses, and monsters whose level is 10 or more higher than yours): 5% chance for the attack to do a Vorpal Strike, dealing 9999 Akashic damage.

_A sharp and painful anomaly._

**Found:** Shrine Maiden

### Bleached Fang

- **Rarity:** 7★
- **Can enchant:** Torso

+25 Critical Evade

_A hard bone fang long bleached by sunlight._

**Found:** Gigas Rex

### Borneblood

- **Rarity:** 7★
- **Can enchant:** Weapons, Legs

Weapon: Gains 1 bonus Hit per 4% of missing HP of the attack target. Legs: After moving, if you are within 1 Range of an enemy, gain 1 Move per 10% HP they are missing for 2 rounds.

_This bloodmark, when borne and worn, will indicate a hunt is about to happen._

**Found:** Fangs

- **Rarity:** 1★
- **Can enchant:** —

**Found:** Feathered Serpent
