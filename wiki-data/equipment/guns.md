# Guns

**Slot 1 — Primary weapon (or slot 3 as a secondary).** 30 items.

Guns are versatile weapons. Rifles specialize in accuracy and range, shotguns in power, and handguns in reliability thanks to their multiple rounds. Launchers and throwers, while rare, have a wide area of effect, good for dealing with crowds of foes. Regardless, they all use similar stat scaling, letting you easily swap weapons to fit the situation. Guns in general also tend to be more suited towards basic attacks compared to Bows and Tomes. Guns typically deal Pierce damage. However, all can also use Hellhound rounds to change this to Fire damage. Guns are most often used by Magic Gunners and Demon Hunters.
