# Hands

**Slot 3 — Hands.** 48 items.

Hand equipment, much like Accessories, generally provide minor benefits such as increased stats or unique effects that can also be further improved by enchanting hand-slot catalysts that go in them. You may equip them in the third slot, but you can also opt to use a sub-Weapon instead for this slot. When upgraded, it grants a bonus to Hit to your Main Weapon and a bonus to Max FP.
