# Axes

**Slot 1 — Primary weapon (or slot 3 as a secondary).** 39 items.

Axes are weapons focused on damage. They have high power and critical damage at the cost of low accuracy and high weight, compared to Swords and Polearms. Axes typically deal Slash damage. Clubs deal Blunt damage. Soldiers, Ghosts, Black Knights, and Dark Bards are all suited for axes.
