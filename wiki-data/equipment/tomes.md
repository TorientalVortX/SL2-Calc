# Tomes

**Slot 1 — Primary weapon (or slot 3 as a secondary).** 53 items.

Tomes are short ranged weapons with average stats and tend to be fairly heavy but they scale primarily on Will instead of Strength like most weapons. In addition, they are casting tools, meaning that Damaging and Healing Spells will gain Scaled Weapon ATK from this weapon. Naturally, these weapons benefit spellcasters like Mages, Curates and their promoted classes the most. The majority of tomes are made of Paper and come in a variety of elements.
