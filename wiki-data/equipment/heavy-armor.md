# Heavy Armor

**Slot 2 — Armor.** 17 items.

Heavy Armor is one of three classes of Armor. It has an emphasis on great protection, offering high amounts of Armor and sometimes even decent amounts of Magic Armor but for the price of offering negligible or even negative Evade and typically having a large amount of Weight. Heavy Armor is so far exclusively made of Metal. For characters, as long as they have the means to carry it and aren't reliant on Evade, Heavy Armor is a great option. Even more so for Black Knight which gains many benefits for using it, such as increased physical protection, critical evade and even more damage for certain Skills like Board Shaker.
