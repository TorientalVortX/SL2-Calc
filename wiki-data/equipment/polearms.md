# Polearms

**Slot 1 — Primary weapon (or slot 3 as a secondary).** 57 items.

Polearms are weapons focused on accuracy. They have high hit and low weight at the cost of power, compared to Swords and Axes. Polearms typically deal Pierce damage. Mundane staves deal Blunt damage, while magical staves deal their respective element's damage. Duelists, Soldiers, Firebirds, and Black Knights commonly work well with polearms.
