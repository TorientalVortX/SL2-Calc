# Bows

**Slot 1 — Primary weapon (or slot 3 as a secondary).** 33 items.

Bows are an all around weapon with the potential to attack foes over long distances. They are best used by Archers and most of their promotions, which have access to many passive benefits, as well as Bow exclusive offensive skills. Bows are typically made of Wood and almost all deal Pierce Damage.

- 3 Momentum, 15 FP
- Daikyu's Potential Skill. Targets 1 unoccupied tile within 1 Range and fires a long-reaching arrow from the Daikyu, which travels up to 15 tiles away. Someone using a daikyu without the proper training will not have a functional one for long._
