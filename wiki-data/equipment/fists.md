# Fists

**Slot 1 — Primary weapon (or slot 3 as a secondary).** 33 items.

Fists are melee weapons that have lower Power but also generally have low Weight, higher Accuracy and perform two attacks (the damage for which is split evenly between them). Naturally, they are most useful for Martial Artists and their Promotion Classes. They are typically made of Metal and deal Blunt Damage, with the Claws subtype of Fists usually dealing Slash Damage instead.
