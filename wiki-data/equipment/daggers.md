# Daggers

**Slot 1 — Primary weapon (or slot 3 as a secondary).** 39 items.

Daggers are melee weapons with low Power but make up for it with high Critical Damage and Guile scaling. Daggers have the unique advantage of being equippable by any Class in the game. However, they are most suited for Rogue, Spellthief and Void Assassin as they have Dagger based skills and benefits. They are made of Metal and usually deal Pierce Damage.
