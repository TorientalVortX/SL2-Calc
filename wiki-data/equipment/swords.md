# Swords

**Slot 1 — Primary weapon (or slot 3 as a secondary).** 54 items.

Swords are balanced weapons. They have average accuracy, power, and weight compared to Polearms and Axes. However, most suffer from low critical damage. Swords typically deal Slash damage. Wooden swords deal Blunt damage. Duelists, Soldiers, Ghosts, and Black Knights commonly work well with swords. Kensei work best with katanas.
