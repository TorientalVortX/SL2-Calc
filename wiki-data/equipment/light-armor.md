# Light Armor

**Slot 2 — Armor.** 23 items.

Light Armor is one of the three classes of Armor. It tends to provide a broad amount of benefits across the spectrum, offering average amounts of Armor, Magic Armor, Evade and Weight. Light Armor has a roughly even mix of Cloth and Metal options. This class of Armor tends to be a solid option for characters who can't handle the weight of Heavy Armor options but want more sturdiness than most Unarmored options can provide. So far the only class that has a specific benefit for using Light Armor is Ranger.
