# Karakuri

Karakuri are artificial mortals created through a mixture of ritual and construction, originating from Yokoshura. They are somewhat similar to Mechanations or Homunculi, in the way that they are created by another with purpose. The creator of Karakuri are called Makers (or Tsukirimono).

### Blood Binding

Karakuri are created through blood ritual, one step of which involves binding a Youkai to its artificial body, creating a state similar to a permanent Install. As a result, Karakuri are always treated as youkai for effects that care about such a designation.


### Threaded Needle

A level of mechanical precision afforded to the Karakuri through its string-guided movements. They passively gain +3% Parry Activation Rate, +3% Critical, and -1 Farshot Penalty. Additionally, this method of internal control makes them slightly more resilient to being Slowed, Frozen, or Immobilized, gaining +5% Status Resist versus those effects.
