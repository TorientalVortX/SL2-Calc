# Human

A very prosperous race, they are much like what you'd expect. They are the most common race to find on Sigrogana and Gold, those being the most powerful human empires, but they pop up everywhere. Humans are mostly independent, following their own ambitions and sense of justice to wherever it leads them.

### Profession

Humans commonly spend long times studying and preparing for a specific type of job. Thus, when buying a 'History' trait, it does not cost a Trait Point.


### Quick to Master

Humans live in a world where they must constantly adapt to situations and circumstances they find themselves in, which makes them more versatile than other races. They gain +2 class skill points for every class. Additionally, their skill pool size is increased by 2, letting them equip more skills at once.
