# Umbral

Umbral are creatures that were affected by the Black Eclipse, an event where the sun passed behind The Hanging Star. Those at a certain location during this event, people and monsters included, were permanently altered as such. New generations seem to share and lose the curse at random. During night, Umbrals can become very difficult to see. Physical features of the umbral include pale skin, slightly pointed ears, and a black marking of differing design somewhere on the face. Umbral seem naturally drawn to caves and other dark areas.

### Fade Out

Allow yourself to be covered in the shade of night, granting +15 Evade for 3 attacks, 5 rounds. (Expires after being attacked 3 times or when the duration ends.) During night hours, this skill does not cost any FP to use.


### The Mark

A facial mark that all Umbrals have. Whatever appearance it has, it seems to protect the Umbral from the powers of darkness, while making them weaker to holy light. They gain 25% Dark resistance, but 25% Light weakness (both reduced by your Scaled SAN, however; min 0%), and if they take any form of magic damage, they recover 3 FP.


### Noctis Arcana

By tapping into the magic that marks them, Umbrals reduce the costs of all Spells they cast during night hours by 25%. However, this effect is reduced by your Scaled SAN (to a minimum of 0%).
