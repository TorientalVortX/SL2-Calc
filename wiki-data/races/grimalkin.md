# Grimalkin

The Grimalkin are a type of Felidae that typically possess gray or white hair and fur. They lack the physical strength and swiftness that their race is famous for, but they are one of the few types of Kaelensia which are actually suited to the arcane arts.

### Golden Eyes

The golden, beast-like eyes of the Kaelensia reduce the effectiveness of Blindness by SAN*2 (at 25 SAN, this means no Hit penalty is applied, even if you have the status).


### Fleetfoot

Being the fast creatures that they are, this unit gains +1 Move.


### Instinct (Felidae)

The Felidae are fast and when they feel like they're in danger, they get faster. In battle, while at 50% HP or less, they gain a bonus to SKI, CEL, LUC, and GUI equal to 1 + 10% of your Scaled SAN. At 25% HP or less, the bonus is doubled.
