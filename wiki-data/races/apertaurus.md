# Apertaurus

Apertaurus are a sub-race of Reapers, and are very similar to Reapers in nearly all respects but in some areas physically; instead of the normal, human-like feet that Reapers have, they instead have legs that end in furry, horse-like hooves. Since Lazarus is a large labyrinth, some speculate that Apertaurus are also excellent at navigating them, just like minotaurs. While this presents certain challenges when trying to use footwear, there are plenty of adaptive devices that make it possible, and some Apertaurus like to wear shoes in this manner to hide the sound of their footsteps, or just to appear normal.

### Apertaurus' Affinity

Apertaurus are naturally adept at using axes. Some theorize this is due to another labyrinth wandering entity, the minotaur, and their tendency to use them.


### Reaper Physique

Being used to the cold air inside of Lazarus, the Reaper race is more resistant to Ice damage, gaining 15% resistance to it. However, the still air inside of it has made them weaker to the power of Wind, gaining a 15% weakness to it.


### Soul Guide

Reapers are agents of Lazarus with a duty to help guide the souls of the lost to it. As such, they are particularly skilled at dealing with those of the Possessed race, dealing +10% damage to them. They are also excellent exorcists, gaining a variety of bonuses; it costs them 50% less stamina to exorcise spirits, and upon successfully doing so, they gain a status effect that increases post-battle HP/FP regen by 10% for 10 rounds, with the duration stacking up to 100 rounds.


### Macabre Culture

Reapers often wear bones over their clothes as decorations, and the rattling of them is something that serves as an easy indicator of their presence. When wearing equipment of Remains-type material (IE, Dragon Remains), the Reaper gains 2 Elemental ATK from that material, based on what left the remains behind.
