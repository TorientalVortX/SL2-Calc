# Phenex

The bird Kaelensia of the Phenex are a human-like race with features similar to Corbies; they possess very vibrant red or gold wings. Unlike most Kaelensia, they do not make Egwyn their home typically, instead coming from the high mountains of Kysei, where their patron, the great phoenix Helondis, originally roosted.

### Golden Eyes

The golden, beast-like eyes of the Kaelensia reduce the effectiveness of Blindness by SAN*2 (at 25 SAN, this means no Hit penalty is applied, even if you have the status).


### Flaming Feathers

The wings of the Phenex protect from certain elements, namely Fire and Light, granting a 15% Elemental Resistance to those elements. However, the same can not be said of Ice and Darkness damage, and the Phenex takes 15% increased damage from those elements. Furthermore, a limited ability to hover lets them use Movement while Airborne.


### From the Ashes

The power of the Phenex clan was shown once by Heldonis, and his people are not much different. When the Phenex is reduced to 0 HP, if they have at least 1 FP, half of that FP is consumed and the Phenex restores that much HP immediately (min. 1 HP; the FP consumption is reduced by your Scaled SAN%, but the HP restored is not). This effect can only trigger once per 10 Rounds, reduced by 10% of your Scaled SAN.


### Revive

The ressurection power of the Phenex clan is not limited to themselves. While not nearly as powerful, they can utilize the effect on others who are in a bad way. Using this skill also costs 10% of the Phenex's maximum HP. A target ally within 1 Range who is at 0 HP is revived with HP equal to your Scaled SAN*2. The target will not suffer any negative effects of being revived, such as Momentum loss or receiving the Badly Beaten effect.
