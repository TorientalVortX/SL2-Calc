# Muridae

Muridae, like all other Kaelensia, would appear human at first glance, if not for the pair of round and fuzzy mouse-like ears that they sport.

### Golden Eyes

The golden, beast-like eyes of the Kaelensia reduce the effectiveness of Blindness by SAN*2 (at 25 SAN, this means no Hit penalty is applied, even if you have the status).


### Muridae Instinct

Muridae are predisposed to kleptomania, but are also easily startled.


### Scurry

Muridae are quick critters who are used to having to run from Felidae. When not wearing Heavy Armor, they gain +1 Move, and a bonus to their flee chance equal to Scaled SAN/2.


### Ratsense

Muridae hear things better in some situations than others. When trying to pick a lock, they gain +10% to the success rate. However, they are naturally oblivious of traps, giving them a -5% chance to detect them.
