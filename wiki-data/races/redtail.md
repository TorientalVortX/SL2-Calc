# Redtail

A scarce race sporting a pair of fox ears as well as a bushy tail, the Redtails as they are called have a certain allure to them, drawing the curiosity of other races. While they are often mistaken for Kaelensia, due to their ears and tail, those in the know recognize that Redtails do not always have golden eyes, nor do they hail from Egwyn. Redtails, indeed, come from all over, although are more commonly found in Oniga.

### Fox God's Blessing

The power of the Great Fox Spirit's blessing, and a magical quality of the Redtail race, letting their luck become augmented in times of great peril.


### Green Dice

Changes the magical energies surrounding the Redtail into a green color. While green, your chance to apply and avoid luck-based status effects is improved by 1x (+1x 10 Scaled SAN, max. 5x) your Fortune Level%. However, if your Fortune Level is one, you instead suffer -5% (plus -5 per 10 Scaled SAN) to each.


### Fox God's Fortune Cookie

Redtails often carry around baskets of home-made fortune cookies in an effort to test out and train their luck. On use, the Redtail pulls out a fortune cookie and eats it, and reads the fortune. Depending on the fortune, the effect changes, which can be (really) bad or (really) good. After you eat a cookie, you can't eat another one for 3 rounds. Otherwise, you'd go through the day's basket in no time.


### Red Dice

Changes the magical energies surrounding the Redtail into a red color. While red, you gain Hit and Critical equal to 1x (+1x 10 Scaled SAN, max. 5x) per your Fortune Level. However, if your Fortune Level is one, you instead suffer -5 (plus -5 per 10 Scaled SAN) Hit and Critical.


### Yellow Dice

Changes the magical energies surrounding the Redtail into a yellow color. While yellow, you gain Evade and Critical Evade equal to 1x (+1x 10 Scaled SAN, max. 5x) your Fortune Level. However, if your Fortune Level is one, you instead suffer -5 (plus -5 per 10 Scaled SAN) Evade and Critical Evade.
