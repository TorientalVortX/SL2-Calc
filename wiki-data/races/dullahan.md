# Dullahan

Ghost stories of haunted armors and headless horsemen are common tales shared around campfires, but these urban legends also have their truths to them. Enter the Dullahan, which is little more than living armor, created by unknown circumstances.

### Burden Soul

The strength of the Dullahan is spiritual in nature, resulting in a body that is less affected by the weight of its collective. Increases your Battle Weight limit by 100, and your maximum carrying capacity by 30. Furthermore, the Interference status, which normally prevents HP and FP recovery, is only half as effective on Dullahans, meaning they still recover 50% of the original total. This resistance is increased by your Scaled SAN.

### Soul Engine

Rather than utilizing the energy of life, the Dullahan moves by the force of its soul, and in battle, this strains it to the point of exhaustion. It is very easy for a Dullahan to over-exert themself by not realizing how much power they are using, and so the skilled among these cursed souls often fight at a slow pace; something that inspires fear in their enemies.
