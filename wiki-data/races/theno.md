# Theno

Native to the seas south of even Egwyn, the Theno are an aquatic race of humanoids with smooth skin and fin-like protrusions near their ears which aid in swimming. They spend most of their lives in the sea and on small islands, often spending the night resting on rocks and singing songs. Those sailing the southern shores of Egwyn can attest to hearing them as they pass through, and some have been foolish enough to crash their ships into the rocks looking for the source... though some believe the songs themselves are magical and drive men to do so unwillingly.

### Theno Physique

The unique physique offered by the Theno from spending all their time swimming and singing grants them +15% Water and Sound Resistance, but for the same reasons, gives them a 15% Acid and Lightning Weakness.


### Voice of the Depths

The voice of the Theno is powerful, magically so. You gain +25% Status Resistance versus Silence effects. In addition, your base Sound ATK is equal to your character level, but it does not increase from stat points.


### Lost Sea

When you live in the sea, there's not much to fear in the shallows. While your base SAN is 15 or less, you ignore the effects of Dark Water. In addition, Soaked status grants you +15 Evade.
