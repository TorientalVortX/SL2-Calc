# Shaitan

The Shaitan are a race surrounded by rumors and fear. One says that those who enter The Dark Zones (places on the world which have yet to be explored, often due to terrifying conditions surrounding them) have two fates; one is to die. The other, those who return, return as Shaitans. They are so exceedingly uncommon in every day life that the Imperial Nation often states that they do not exist, and take reports of them as pranks or something other than what they are.

### Black Rend

Smash your claw into an enemy within 1 range. Deals Slash damage that ignores protection to the target equal to your character level + 50% of Scaled STR, minus your Scaled SAN.

- **power:** Level + 50% Scaled STR - Scaled SAN

### Dark Claws

Powerful black carapace protects and increases the force of the Shaitan's attacks. They gain +6 (-1 per 10 Scaled SAN) damage to all attacks made with non-ranged weaponry.


### Mind Scorch

Their psyche assaulted by things far more terrifying than most, the Shaitan is immune to Fear (if below 30 SAN), Hesitation (if below 20 SAN), and Charm (if below 10 SAN).
