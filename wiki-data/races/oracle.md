# Oracle

Oracles are types of corrupted that possess blank, grey eyes. They have difficulty seeing across long distances, but for that price, they are given a great gift; visions of future events. Unlike other types of Corrupted, Oracles are highly valued as consultants, and many kingdoms have a designated Oracle that serve their interests.

### Future Vision

An Oracle's vision of far-off places is always clouded with visions of the future, decreasing their Hit and Evade by 10% against any enemy beyond 1 Range. However, the Oracle also sees the future movements of enemies within 1 Range, increasing their Hit and Evade versus them by 10%. All bonuses and penalties are reduced by 1 per 10 Scaled SAN.


### Preordinance

Capable of seeing into the future in a limited fashion, the Oracle prevents enemies from getting back attacks.
