# Mechanation

Mechanations are, simply put, artificial humans. They walk and talk like humans, and some even possess human emotions, but they lack the natural blood, bones, and such that humans have. The miracle of Karaten's long history of magical science, Mechanations were perfected near the end of the Onigan Rebellion, in the year 290.

### Artifical Body

As a Mechanation's form is very different from a natural creature's, they are less vulnerable to Fire and Ice damage, reducing each by 15%. However, they also suffer a 25% weakness to Electricity as a result.


### Artificial Body

### Mechanical Strength

Unlike many mortal races, the Mechanation is not used to strain and exhaustion in the same way. They gain +20 to their maximum encumbrance. They also gain +1 maximum Physical and Mental Stamina every 2 character levels.
