# Heron

A type of avian Kaelensia, and perhaps the most famous, despite their rarity. Known as songbirds, they are well known for the angelic appearance, with lovely white wings and soft features, and more importantly, the charming voices they have. Hearons have a natural affinity with magic; more specifically, with Galdr; magical attunement with sound and song.

### Golden Eyes

The golden, beast-like eyes of the Kaelensia reduce the effectiveness of Blindness by SAN*2 (at 25 SAN, this means no Hit penalty is applied, even if you have the status).


### Heavenly Voice

The Heron's voice is truly magnificient - in the skies and high mountains where they live, those who hear their divine choir may consider themselves truly blessed; provided the same powerful voices don't cause an avalanche. ### Heron Physique

Herons are greatly in-tune with aural forces, and are more than resilient to them, granting +15% Sound Resistance. Their wings are unused to harsh gales, however, giving them a 15% Wind Weakness.


### Ancient Song

A song of old times in the Heronic language, carrying with it particularly strong galdric magic with it. When used, consumes all of your Momentum and gain Ancient Song LV X (X = Momentum used). At the start of the next round, you finish singing, restoring the HP of all allies excluding yourself by Y (Y = 10 * Ancient Song LV, min. of 10, max. of 15 or character level, whichever is higher).

- **target:** Ally Only
