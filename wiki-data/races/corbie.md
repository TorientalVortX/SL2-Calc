# Corbie

The winged Corbie are an elegant human-like race of bird Kaelensia that live high in the tall trees of Egwyn's forests. While their light bodies make them poor in close combat, they also possess a grace and speed that many races cannot match, that makes them capable of avoiding danger.

### Golden Eyes

The golden, beast-like eyes of the Kaelensia reduce the effectiveness of Blindness by SAN*2 (at 25 SAN, this means no Hit penalty is applied, even if you have the status).


### Flight

This unit, having wings, is capable of flight. While Airborne, this unit will hover when using Movement, allowing them to avoid field hazards and traps on the ground.


### Gain Air

Leap into the air and hover briefly with your wings, giving you Airborne status. (Airborne status interacts with certain specific skills and has no effect on its own.)


### Aerial Attacker

Best suited to attacking from the air. In battle, while you are Airborne, you gain +10 Hit, and a bonus to Power equal to 1 + 30% of your Scaled SAN.


### Stay Afloat

Toggle skill. While toggled on, all skills you use will be treated as if they were Airborne Compatible, meaning they won't end your Airborne status.
