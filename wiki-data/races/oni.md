# Oni

Oni are a horned race that have been known to dwell in places high within the mountains, typically the Oskard-Sothis mountain region in Korvara. The skin of an Oni is sometimes an unusual shade of red or blue, but this is not always the case - some have more common skin tones. Oni are a feared existence, known for having short tempers, an enjoyment of violence, and a hunger for flesh and blood.

### Banquet (Oni)

Oni are known to enjoy drinking blood, even (or especially) during battle. The Oni performs a basic attack with a special Oni Bite weapon (90% STR) on a target within 1 Range. If the attack hits, the Oni gains Sanguine Crest LV 2 (5 rounds). If the Oni already had Sanguine Crest, they refresh its duration and power it up to LV 4, but also become Clumsy for 2 rounds.


### Hazing Blood

Oni are said to be a hotblooded race, but the truth goes beyond metaphor. While Sanguine Crest is active, at the start of a new round, enemies within 1 Range take magic bonus damage equal to 5 + Sanguine Crest LV + half of the Oni's Scaled SAN. (Excluding enemies who absorb, reflect, or are immune to the damage type, Fire by default. Enemies cannot be defeated by this damage.)


### Graceless Destruction

The only thing scarier than an Oni is a drunk Oni. While they are Clumsy, the Oni gains +5% Phys and Mag Defense, and deal +10% Critical Damage with melee weapons.
