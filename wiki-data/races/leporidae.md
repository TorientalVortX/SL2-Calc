# Leporidae

Leporidae are a family of the human-like Kaelensia with hare-like features, including long, rabbit-like ears and strong legs. Hailing from Egwyn, they utilize their animal features to avoid danger, capable of hearing it coming from a mile away, and using their powerful legwork to either kick the threat into submission or bounce away faster than it can catch up.

### Golden Eyes

The golden, beast-like eyes of the Kaelensia reduce the effectiveness of Blindness by SAN*2 (at 25 SAN, this means no Hit penalty is applied, even if you have the status).


### Leporidae Physique

More adept at using the ground to their advantage, or simply more capable of avoiding its dangers, the Leporidae is harder to hurt with Earth damage, giving them a 15% resistance to it. Their ears are incredibly powerful, but also somewhat sensitive, and so they take Sound damage harder than others, giving them a 15% weakness to it. Mumbling is also less effective against them.


### Instinct (Leporidae)

Leporidae do not like being put into a corner, and when pushed, their keen senses come out in full force. When at 50% HP or less, the effect of Rabbit Foot increases by 50%. At 25% HP or less, when the Leporidae is targeted by a basic attack (or skill that uses a basic attack), before the attack happens, they turn to face the attacker.


### Rabbit Foot

Known for their strong legs, the Leporidae are certainly capable of delivering a painful kick. Lesser known is their natural affinity with weather magic, such as lightning, which is displayed better by wild monster hares. After using Rabbit Leap, the first Kick skill or Nature domain spell used this round gains bonus damage equal to (distance moved) + 50% of your Scaled SAN.


### Rabbit Leap

Uses the strong legs of the Leporidae to leap to a nearby location.
