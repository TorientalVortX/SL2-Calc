# Lupine

The Lupine Kaelensia are a human-like race that possess wolf-like ears and a tail. They are famously strong, like most beast Kaelensia, and just as resilient, absolutely refusing to go down without a lot of effort. They generally distrust the forces of magic, which is just fine, as they are ill-suited to it. Like all Kaelensia, they are from Egwyn most of the time.

### Golden Eyes

The golden, beast-like eyes of the Kaelensia reduce the effectiveness of Blindness by SAN*2 (at 25 SAN, this means no Hit penalty is applied, even if you have the status).


### Instinct (Lupine)

Lupines do not go down without a fight. In battle, while at 50% HP or less, they gain a bonus to STR, WIL, DEF, and RES equal to 1 + 10% of your Scaled SAN. At 25% HP or less, the bonus is doubled.


### War Cry

The Lupine roars, affecting all enemies within a 2 Range circle. Affected enemies suffer Sound magic damage equal to 25 + your Sound ATK, and have a chance to be inflicted with Hunted (LV: 10 + 10% of Scaled SAN), as well as Fear (3 rounds for both).

- **target:** Circle (2)
