# Hyattr

The Hyattr are a dragon-winged race known for their potent fire breathing capabilities, as well as their patron's destructive legacy. Hyatt was a red dragon, with bright crimson scales and a massive wingspan, which he abused by flying over the land and setting it ablaze with his fire breath, destroying many human settlements. A pointless show of power, but one that allowed him to subjugate many followers who would become Hyattr, lest their villages and towns be next in his path.

### Fire Wing

The scaled dragon wings of the Hyattr protect them from Fire, which is their signature element anyway, increasing their Fire Resistance by 15%. However, those same wings are vulnerable to the winds they fly on, increasing their Wind weakness by 15%.


### Dragon Hover

Leap into the air and hover briefly with your wings, giving you Airborne status. (Airborne status interacts with certain specific skills and has no effect on its own.)


### Channel Magic

Inhale deeply, channeling your focus into a potent magical energy, as well as drawing it in from the air. You recover FP equal to 5 + half of your Scaled SAN, as well as a 'Magic Energy (Dragon)' status until your next turn. If you use Flame Breath while you have that status, you can resize the length of the line, up to double its original size.


### Channel Destruction

Inhale deeply, drawing up a force of destruction from inside of you and the air you breathe in. You gain a 'Destruction Energy (Dragon)' status until your next turn, which increases your STR and WIL by 2 + 1 per 10 Scaled SAN. If you use Flame Breath while you have this status, the width of the line is increased significantly, and will leave behind Cinders for 2 rounds that deal damage equal to 5 + half of your Scaled SAN. It will also increase the damage of the skill by 100% of your Fire ATK.


### Fire Breath

By utilizing massive amounts of focus, the Hyattr race is capable of launching a line of fire as if they were exhaling. Deals Fire magic damage to all enemies in a line equal to half your Character Level + Scaled SAN. Its effects can change if you have Magic Energy or Destruction Energy stored. If you have Airborne status, you will also move the length of the line, but this requires the last square in the line to be unoccupied.

- **target:** Line (5)
